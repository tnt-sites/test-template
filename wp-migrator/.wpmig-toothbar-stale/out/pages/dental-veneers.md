    heading: What Are Veneers?
    text: Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-made shells that cover the front surface of your teeth to improve their appearance. Made from durable porcelain or resin, veneers are designed to change the shape, color, length, or size of your teeth. These shells are bonded permanently to your natural teeth, providing a long-lasting, beautiful smile. Veneers offer a natural look, and porcelain veneers are especially resistant to staining, making them a preferred choice for many patients.

    text16: At the next visit, we’ll prepare your teeth by removing a thin layer of enamel to ensure the veneers fit naturally.
