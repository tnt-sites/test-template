Teeth Whitening

Our one-hour teeth whitening service is designed to give you the brightest smile possible. Using professional-grade whitening products, we target stains and discoloration, leaving your teeth several shades whiter. Results typically last for a year and can be maintained with our take-home whitening kits. These kits come with custom bleach trays and professional-strength hydrogen peroxide gel, making it easy to touch up your smile at home.

The Chaser:
Take-Home Whitening Kit

Want to maintain that glow after your whitening session? The Chaser take-home kit includes custom-fitted bleach trays and professional-strength whitening gel. We use 3D scanning to create a precise mold of your teeth, eliminating the need for messy impressions. This convenient option ensures your smile stays vibrant on your schedule.

Happy Hour:
Private Whitening Parties

Looking for a fun and unique way to celebrate? Toothbar’s Happy Hour offers after-hours teeth whitening parties—perfect for bachelorette parties, birthdays, or a special night out. Gather your friends, pop some bubbly, blast your favorite music, and enjoy a private whitening experience that leaves everyone glowing. It’s the perfect way to kick off a memorable weekend in Austin with a smile that shines just as bright as you do.

The Detox:
Express Cleanings

Need a quick refresh between your regular dental cleanings? The Detox is a streamlined version of your traditional 6-month cleaning, designed to polish away surface stains, floss thoroughly, and leave you with a bright smile. It’s an excellent pre-whitening treatment or a quick pick-me-up to keep your smile looking its best.
