    subheading: Once Your Tooth Is Cracked
    text2: When the exterior of a tooth is damaged, a domino effect of issues can follow. Once the enamel is cracked, the softer, more vulnerable tooth material is exposed. Bacteria and decay can attack the interior of your tooth, leading to severe tooth infections or deep decay. Repairing a cracked or damaged tooth right away can prevent further complications that may require root canal therapy or even tooth extraction.
