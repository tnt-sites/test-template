    subheading: When is Root Canal Therapy Needed?
    text2: Root canal therapy is used when the interior, or pulp, of the tooth is compromised. This can be due to decay that has reached the inner canal of the tooth or when trauma or other problems have damaged the core. The pulp and nerve roots of the tooth are cleared from the inner canal and the interior is disinfected to stop any infection. Once cleaned, the canal is packed with a sterile material, resealed and capped to protect it from further damage.
    heading2: Root Canals Offer Pain Relief for Infected Teeth!
    text3: |-
      Since the nerves of a tooth reside in the interior pulp, if this area is infected or damaged, it can cause pain. It can be a dull ache or throbbing pain that can be almost unbearable. Root canal therapy removes the source of this pain, while protecting the tooth from possible extraction or loss. Once the procedure is completed, the pain from the infection is gone.
