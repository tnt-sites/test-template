    subheading: What is a Dental Bridges
    text2: One of the quickest permanent solutions to missing teeth is a dental bridge. Unlike partial dentures, these dental appliances are fixed in place and will not slip or move when you eat, laugh or talk. The traditional dental bridge uses the adjoining teeth to support pontics, or artificial teeth, that replace those that are missing. Using metal, dental crowns and pontics, the appliance is secured to your existing teeth to restore your smile.
    heading2: Preparing a Dental Bridge
    text3: If you have one or more teeth missing in a row, a dental bridge could be the right option to restore your smile. The traditional bridge consists of dental crowns that are placed on the existing teeth on either side of the missing teeth. The crowns are attached to the framework of the bridge that holds the artificial teeth in place.&nbsp;To prepare a dental bridge, the adjacent teeth are prepared to have these crowns fitted. Impressions and imagery are used to create the bridge to ensure a perfect fit in your bite. Once the laboratory creates the elements, usually within a few weeks, we can install your dental bridge and permanently restore your smile.
    heading3: Benefits of a Dental Bridge
    text4: Once a dental bridge is in place, it will give you back function as well as improve the look of your smile. You can chew the foods you love again and feel confident when you smile. It can also help minimize shifting of your existing teeth; when teeth are lost, it can allow other teeth to shift into the empty space. You can enjoy a complete smile and protect your remaining teeth from alignment issues.
