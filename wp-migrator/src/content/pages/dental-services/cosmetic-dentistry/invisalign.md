Using advanced 3D imagery, each set of clear aligners is customized to change your smile. Every few weeks a new set of aligners is used to get you closer to your perfect smile. Invisalign Dentist Littleton, fit the trays comfortably over your teeth without any metal or hardware. They are almost invisible and can be removed when you eat or to perform oral hygiene.</p>
