Dentistry Tailored to Every Smile

We understand that every person has a unique smile. That’s why Dr. Ty Reusser and Dr. Brooke Coltrane proudly provide a wide range of services, from preventive care and cosmetic dentistry to life-changing restorative procedures like dental implants and root canals, as well as same-day emergency dentistry. No matter what your dental needs are, our whole team is ready to step in and help your smile look and feel its absolute best.
