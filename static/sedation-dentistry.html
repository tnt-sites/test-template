Sedation Dentistry Wichita

Feel Relaxed During Any Dental Treatment

These days, it’s not uncommon to feel a little (or extremely) anxious about visiting the dentist. However, this should never be the reason for putting off dental care—not when you can relax through your appointment with the help of sedation dentistry. Continue reading to learn a little more about how we can use sedation dentistry to make your next dental visit a remarkably positive and stress-free experience, and if you’re ready to schedule an appointment, don’t wait to reach out today!

Oral Conscious Sedation

Oral conscious sedation is a convenient and effective way to help patients feel calm and relaxed during all kinds of dental procedures. Administered in pill form before your appointment, this method of sedation gently eases anxiety and instills feelings of warmth while keeping you awake and able to respond to our team. The level of sedation is carefully tailored to your needs, ensuring a comfortable experience, even for longer or more complex treatments.

Nitrous Oxide Sedation

Nitrous oxide, also known as “laughing gas,” is a safe and gentle form of sedation that we offer to help our patients feel more relaxed and comfortable during dental treatment. Delivered through a small mask placed over the nose and mixed with oxygen, nitrous oxide works quickly to ease anxiety and create a sense of calm while keeping you fully awake and responsive. Once the mask is removed, the effects wear off almost immediately, allowing you to resume your day without any interruptions.
