In-House Dental Membership Plan Wichita

Easy to Sign Up & Simple to Use

If you’re uninsured or underinsured, but you still want to receive top-tier dentistry from our team at R2 Center for Dentistry, consider signing up for our in-house dental membership plan. These annual plans offer an easier way to keep your oral health on the right track without having to worry about dental insurance claims, maximums, deductibles, or copayments. To sign up or learn more, call our office today!

*This program is a discount plan, not a dental insurance plan. There is no cancelation penalty. Patient’s portion is due the day of service.
