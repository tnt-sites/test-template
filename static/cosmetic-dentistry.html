Cosmetic Dentist Houston

You’ll Love Your New Smile

You don’t have to choose between health and aesthetics when it comes to your teeth. Working with our team, you can have a smile that feels great, functions seamlessly, and inspires confidence in every situation. From small improvements to dramatic makeovers, we’re ready to give you the look you’ve always wanted with cosmetic dentistry in Houston. Reach out today to get started.

Why Choose New Teeth Dental Solutions - Houston for Cosmetic Dentistry?

  • Serving Houston for 45+ Years
  • Top-Rated Cosmetic Dentist in Houston
  • Dr. Jetton is a Diamond+ Invisalign Provider

Veneers

Veneers sound too good to be true on the surface, but they live up to the hype. With just one procedure, we can improve the shape, size, color, spacing, and even alignment of your teeth, often in just two appointments. Thin shells bonded to the front of your teeth can create a perfectly balanced and symmetrical result you can trust to last for 15+ years.

Metal-Free Restorations

While fillings and crowns of the past were all about function and strength, we can now use these restorations to uplift cosmetically imperfect teeth. Tooth-colored restorations can fix cavities while blending seamlessly into your smile. And all-ceramic crowns can help an undersized, damaged, or slightly crooked tooth look like nothing was ever wrong with it.

Invisalign®

As a Diamond+ Invisalign provider, Dr. Jetton helps over 280 patients achieve perfectly straight and attractive smiles without cumbersome metal braces every year. Your treatment will be meticulously planned and use custom-made trays that disappear when you put them on. The only thing people will notice are your beautiful results!

Learn More About Invisalign

Teeth Whitening

  • In-Office Whitening: Get in and out in under an hour while we make your smile 6-10 shades brighter. Perfect if you don’t want to wait or have a big event just around the corner.
  • Take-Home Whitening: Unlike the kits you’ll find at the store, ours feature custom trays and a bleaching gel that’s about five times stronger than anything you can buy. You can relax on your couch and binge your favorite show while making stains disappear over the course of a week or so.

Dental Bonding

We can make a small chip, crack, or gap disappear in the time it takes to go to lunch. We simply apply a color-matched resin to the problem area and sculpt it to cover the flaw. Once it’s polished and smooth, you’re done! The procedure is fast, and results can easily last for over a decade.

Gum Recontouring

Your gums influence the overall look of your smile just as much as your teeth! With gentle and precise laser gum recontouring, we can fix a “gummy” smile by removing excess tissue. This makes the teeth look longer, fuller, and more eye-catching. The procedure only takes one appointment, and the results last for a lifetime.

Smile Makeovers

If you have multiple things you would like to change about your smile, we can help you do so efficiently. Combining multiple procedures lets us address aesthetic and health-related issues all at once, cutting down on your overall treatment time so you can enjoy your results as soon as possible.
