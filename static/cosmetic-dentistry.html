Cosmetic Dentistry Wichita

Achieving Your Dream Smile Has Never Been Easier

Most people agree that when they meet someone new or greet an acquaintance, the very first feature they notice is the person’s smile—meaning that your teeth are quite an important part of you and your personality! That said, if you’re unhappy with your teeth due to aesthetic flaws, know that cosmetic dentistry offers a wider range of solutions capable of helping. If you’re ready to achieve your smile goals, don’t wait to contact our office to set up a consultation. 

Why Choose R2 Center for Dentistry for Cosmetic Dentistry?

  • CEREC Same-Day Restorations Available
  • In-Office & Take-Home Whitening Options
  • Flexible Financing Options Available

Veneers

Veneers are a highly versatile cosmetic solution. These thin, custom-made porcelain shells can be bonded to the front of your teeth to correct a variety of concerns, including chips, cracks, stains, gaps, and unevenly shaped teeth. Better yet, veneers are durable, stain-resistant, and crafted to match the color and contour of your natural teeth, giving you a flawless, long-lasting smile. Whether you want to enhance just a few teeth or achieve a complete transformation of your smile, veneers can help!

Metal-Free Restorations

In the past, many types of restorations were created using metal amalgam, which was durable and reliable, but unfortunately, not natural-looking at all due to its silvery/gray color. Thankfully, we can offer a much more aesthetically pleasing solution! Using durable, tooth-colored materials like porcelain, zirconia, and EMAX, we can create restorations that blend in seamlessly with your smile. Metal-free restorations are also biocompatible, safe for patients with metal sensitivities or allergies, and designed to preserve as much of your natural teeth as possible. 

Cosmetic Bonding

Cosmetic bonding is a quick, effective way to enhance your smile by correcting minor imperfections in your teeth. Using a small amount of tooth-colored resin, we can easily repair chips, close small gaps, reshape uneven edges, and even cover up stubborn staining in just one quick visit. The bonding material is carefully sculpted and polished to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, creating a beautiful, even look. Patients often love this affordable, minimally invasive treatment since it provides immediate improvements without the need for extensive procedures!

SureSmile Clear Aligners

SureSmile clear aligners offer a discreet, comfortable way to straighten your teeth without the need for traditional braces. These custom-made aligners are nearly invisible while being worn and are designed to gradually shift your teeth into proper alignment. Because they’re removable, you can easily maintain your normal brushing, flossing, and eating habits throughout treatment. Plus, SureSmile doesn’t just tackle crooked teeth—it can also assist with bite issues, crowding, and gaps, making it just as capable as metal braces. 

Teeth Whitening

We offer both in-office whitening treatments for fast, dramatic results, as well as take-home whitening kits that come with the added convenience of whitening on your own schedule. Both options use professional-grade whitening solutions that deliver better and longer-lasting results than anything you can purchase yourself at the store or online. Whether you’re preparing for a special occasion or simply want to refresh your smile, our customized whitening treatments can help remove years of stains and discoloration, leaving you with a confident, glowing smile!
