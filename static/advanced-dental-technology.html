Advanced Dental Technology Wichita

Delivering More Comfortable & Precise Care

Advancements in dental technology continue to transform the way we deliver care, making treatments more comfortable, precise, and efficient for our patients. From instruments like our digital impression system that eliminates the need for messy molds, to our soft tissue laser that facilitates gentler procedures and faster healing, modern dental technology enhances every aspect of your appointment, from start to finish. Our commitment to using only the most up-to-date equipment and techniques ensures that you receive the highest quality care possible in the most convenient and comfortable manner imaginable.

CT/Cone Beam Scanner

Our CT/cone beam scanner is an advanced imaging instrument that gives Dr. Reusser and Dr. Coltrane a complete, 3D view of your mouth and jaw for more precise and personalized care. This technology goes beyond standard X-rays by capturing detailed images of your bone, teeth, and soft tissues, making it especially useful for planning dental implants, evaluating jaw health, or diagnosing complex dental issues. Better yet, the scan is quick, comfortable, and delivers an incredibly high-quality image. 

Digital Impressions

Digital impressions offer a faster, more comfortable, and highly accurate alternative to traditional impression methods. Instead of messy, uncomfortable trays filled with putty, we use advanced scanning technology to capture precise 3D images of your teeth and gums. These digital models allow us to design crowns, bridges, and other restorations with exceptional accuracy. Plus, the process is quick and completely noninvasive for patients!

Soft Tissue Laser

Our soft tissue laser allows us to perform a variety of dental procedures with greater precision, comfort, and faster healing times. This advanced tool uses a focused beam of light to gently treat the gums, making procedures like gum disease treatment more efficient and far less invasive. Patients also often experience little to no discomfort, minimal bleeding, and quicker recovery compared to traditional methods.

Intraoral Camera

Our intraoral camera allows you to see what we see during your dental exam and during certain treatments, giving you a clearer understanding of your oral health. This small, handheld device captures high-resolution images of your teeth and gums, displaying them in real time on a nearby screen. With this detailed view, we can detect issues like cavities, cracks, or even gum disease early on and then explain treatment options more effectively. This camera not only improves accuracy in diagnosis but also enhances communication, helping you feel more in the loop and confident about your dental care.
