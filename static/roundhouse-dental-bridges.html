Experience Superior Stability with Roundhouse Dental Bridge

A roundhouse dental bridge is an innovative solution for individuals missing multiple teeth. Unlike traditional dental bridges, which rely on crowns placed on adjacent teeth for support, a roundhouse dental bridge is anchored using dental implants. This method offers enhanced stability and functionality, closely mimicking natural teeth.

In a roundhouse dental bridge procedure, dental implants are surgically placed in the jawbone to serve as sturdy foundations. Once the implants are securely in place and have fused with the bone, a custom-made denture is attached to the implants. This bridge consists of prosthetic teeth designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth and is precisely designed to ensure a perfect fit.

Is a Roundhouse Dental Bridge Right for You?

Our dentists may recommend a roundhouse dental bridge if you have lost multiple teeth or if there is a concern that individual implants might not provide sufficient support on their own. By distributing the biting and chewing forces across several implants, this type of bridge reduces the risk of implant failure and ensures a more stable and durable solution.

Benefits of Roundhouse Dental Bridges

The benefits of roundhouse dental bridges include:

  • Long-lasting solution: With proper care, implant restorations can last many years, often a lifetime.
  • Natural appearance and function: The prosthetic teeth are designed to look, feel, and function like your natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak and smile with confidence.
  • Bone preservation: Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss that often occurs after tooth loss.
  • Stability for surrounding teeth: By filling the gap left by missing teeth, the bridge helps maintain the proper alignment of your remaining teeth.

The Roundhouse Dental Bridge Process

Receiving a roundhouse dental bridge involves a multi-step process. Initially, our dentists will perform a thorough examination and develop a treatment plan tailored to your needs. The first surgical procedure involves placing the dental implant posts into the jawbone. After a healing period, during which the implants integrate with the bone, the final bridge is fabricated and securely attached to the implants.
