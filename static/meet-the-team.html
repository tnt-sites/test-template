Meet Our Houston Dental Team

Great People, Great Service

Our team in Houston is always ready to offer a kind ear and quick answer. You’ll enjoy efficient service from friendly, dedicated professionals who will feel like old friends after your first visit.

Christina Office Manager

Christina has been a part of the dental world for over 15 years, and she loves to see the renewed confidence on a patient’s face after having work done. Originally from Denver, she is a proud mother and grandmother, so she loves to cook, enjoy the outdoors, and most of all, make time for friends and family.

Jeanette Patient Coordinator

Jeanette joined us over a decade ago, and every day since, she has dedicated herself to providing our patients with the best care and service possible. She comes to us from McAllen, TX, and attended Everest College. Her life outside of the office is filled with love and activity thanks to her four wonderful children.

Sunita Dental Hygienist

Sunita loves building relationships and helping people achieve their best dental and overall health. She is a graduate of Howard University and has been in the dental field for over 20 years. A Maryland native, she is married with three sons. You’ll often find her cheering on her boys while playing sports, cooking healthy meals, and learning everything she can about living a healthy lifestyle.

Cindy Dental Hygienist

Cindy believes that dentistry can be tremendously empowering, making a big difference in a patient’s health and self-esteem. She has over 15 years of dental experience both as an assistant and hygienist, and she’s married with three children. Outside of work, she is a full-time soccer mom who loves to try new restaurants when she can.

Carolina Lead Assistant

Carolina has had an amazing 30+ year dental career. In that time, she helped create countless smiles and built even more connections within the community among local families. She and her husband have four sons and a dog, and she likes to relax by shopping, collecting perfume, and dancing!
