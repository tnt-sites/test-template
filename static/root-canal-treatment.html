Root Canal Treatment Hays

Tooth Pain Stops with Us

Our dentists in Hays understand how much toothaches can impact your daily life, and we can help. We offer same-day emergency examinations to help determine if root canal treatment is needed. If you’re experiencing dental pain, please don’t hesitate to call our office. We’ll be happy to help get your oral health on the right track and preserve your natural tooth.

Why Choose Lifetime Dental Care for Root Canal Treatment?

  • Same-Day Emergency Dental Appointments Available
  • Sedation Available for Your Comfort & Relaxation
  • Same-Day CEREC Dental Crowns

Do I Need Root Canal Treatment?

The best way to know whether or not root canal treatment is right for you is to schedule an examination with our emergency dentists. We will examine your affected tooth or teeth, as well as capture necessary X-rays. We typically recommend this procedure when the following symptoms are present:

  • A tooth is visibly decayed, allowing bacteria to reach the inner chamber of a tooth.
  • A tooth is cracked or noticeably damaged enough to allow bacteria to reach the inner chamber.
  • A severe toothache has developed, which often results in lingering sensitivity to hot and cold items.
  • You notice a pimple-like bump (abscess) on your gums near the painful tooth.
  • Your face or neck has swollen on the side of your mouth that’s causing you pain.
