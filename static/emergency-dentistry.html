Emergency Dentist Hays

We Can Provide Fast Relief from Dental Pain

At Lifetime Dental Care, we are happy to offer our patients same-day emergency dental appointments in Hays. That means that when life throws you a curveball when you least expect it, you can call our Hays dental team for help. We see all ages and offer comprehensive dentistry in one place, allowing us to handle most dental emergency situations right here in-house.

Why Choose Lifetime Dental Care for Emergency Dentistry?

  • Sedation Dentistry Available for Your Comfort
  • Same-Day Examinations for Patients in Pain
  • We Accept & Maximize Dental Insurance

How We Treat Dental Emergencies

  1. Call Our Team: When you contact our office during a dental emergency, we always do our best to see you for an exam that same day. We’ll also provide you with first aid guidance over-the-phone to minimize your risk of any additional dental damage.
  2. Get Examined: Our office is equipped with advanced technology like our cone beam/CT scanner, intraoral camera, and digital X-ray system to provide faster, clearer, and more comfortable diagnoses. After any necessary imaging has been completed, you’ll be examined by Dr. Lowe, Dr. Grove, or Dr. Nelson.
  3. Receive Treatment: Based on the findings of your oral examination and review of your diagnostic images, your emergency dentist in Hays will put together a treatment plan to address any discomfort you’re experiencing as well as improve your oral health.
