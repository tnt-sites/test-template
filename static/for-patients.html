Getting Started is Easy

We’ve been welcoming patients through our doors since 1981, so we know how to make a good first impression! To ensure your initial appointment at our office in Houston goes smoothly, just look over the page below to find answers to some basic questions. Did we not cover something here? No worries, just give us a call!

Your First Visit

Once you get scheduled with us, you’ll be sent our forms to fill out digitally. That way, when you arrive, we can check you in nice and fast. You’ll be greeted with a smile and by name, and before you know it, you’ll be escorted to the treatment area. We’ll capture some images, Dr. Jetton will perform an exam, and he’ll discuss his observations with you using visuals so you fully understand your oral health. From there, he’ll go over treatment options. If there is enough time, he can complete many procedures at the same visit!

Dental Insurance

We make using your dental insurance refreshingly straightforward. Once you let us know who your provider is and give us your membership information, we’ll take care of the rest. We’ll verify your benefits, give you an estimate of your coverage, and handle the entire claim for you. Want to know more about how we can use your plan? Sure thing—reach out today.

Flexible Financing Options

You can get the care you need today and pay gradually on a flexible schedule. Just click the buttons below to choose the one that suits you best and apply online. Most patients enjoy little or no interest, and there are no prepayment penalties or hidden fees.

In-House Savings Plan

Don’t have insurance? Live on a fixed income? Primarily freelance? If so, our savings plan was designed just for you.

It offers the savings of dental insurance with fewer restrictions and hassles—all for a lower price. You can cover your routine care for the year with a flat fee and gain access to sizeable discounts on most other treatments. Want to learn more about how it works? Great! Just give our friendly team a call.

Special Offers

Spending less and getting more when it comes to dentistry is easy, especially at New Teeth Dental Solutions - Houston. We offer a low-cost first visit for new patients, and the fee can be rolled into paying for our savings plan, meaning saving money helps you save even more money (without compromising on quality).
