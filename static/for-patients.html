New Dental Patient Information Hays

Where You Get to Enjoy Visiting the Dentist

We are excited to join you on your journey to optimal oral health. Whether you are seeking cosmetic enhancements, family dentistry or specialized treatments, we are here to help. Your radiant smile is our mission! We provide personalized care, offering you comfortable, high-quality treatments that make a difference.

Our primary goal at Lifetime Dental Care is to provide you with an exceptional experience from the very beginning. Part of achieving this is ensuring that you and your family feel relaxed and at ease while visiting us. Our thoughtfully designed dental office in Hays is a testament to this commitment, and we are excited to offer you a warm and welcoming environment.

Your First Visit

After you schedule your first appointment, our team will send you our patient forms via text message as well as a VIP intake form, where we will ask you questions to make sure we’re able to provide you with a first-class experience.

When you arrive at our office, you will meet your dental assistant and dental hygienist who will speak with you about your medical and oral health history, take X-rays, and conduct your dedicated cleaning. Then, one of our dentists will complete an examination and present you with any treatment recommendations. Before you leave, our front desk staff will schedule follow-up visits as needed.

Dental Insurance Welcome

Our office is dental insurance-friendly, so we’re happy to accept all PPO dental insurance plans! We will file claims on your behalf to help maximize your benefits as well as answer any questions you may have. Our top priority is to take all the stress out of handling dental insurance so you can sit back, relax, and get the most out of your visit.

CareCredit Financing Available

Our office is proud to be partnered with CareCredit to offer our patients little-to-no interest financing. Once you have applied and been approved, we can help you schedule treatment in an affordable, low-stress fashion. With additional flexible financing, our uninsured or underinsured patients are able to more easily fit the cost of care into their budgets.

Pay Online

For added convenience, our patients can now take care of their payments online from the comfort of home instead of having to call or stop by the office in person. Paying online is safe and secure, and your personal information will be protected every step of the way. If you have any questions or experience any issues, don’t hesitate to let us know.
