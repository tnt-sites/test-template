Tooth-Colored Fillings

Our approach to fillings addresses all of the issues with traditional metal ones. These are color-matched to your enamel to create a natural appearance. They also seal off the tooth better to prevent future cavities and cracks. And, because they are metal-free, they don’t cause temperature sensitivity.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are small, cap-like structures that are designed to fit over the visible surface of a broken, treated, or otherwise damaged tooth. They offer an added layer of protection to reduce dental sensitivity and extend the life of your natural tooth, preventing the need for more extensive interventions, such as tooth extractions. We craft our crowns from high-quality materials like porcelain and gold so you can make the most out of your restored tooth.
