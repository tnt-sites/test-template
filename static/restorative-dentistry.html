Restorative Dentistry Hays

Strengthening & Protecting Your Natural Teeth

Decay, infection, and dental damage can all impact the health and function of your natural teeth. The good news is that our team can help rebuild and preserve them with high-quality dental restorations, such as same-day dental crowns and tooth-colored fillings. Our goal is to get your smile back in working order and protect it with long-lasting restorations so you don’t have to worry about replacing missing teeth. To learn more about our restorative dentistry treatment options, call our Hays dental office today.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

A dental filling is a restoration used to repair minor to moderate tooth damage. We may recommend a filling if a tooth has been damaged by decay (for example, if it has a cavity) or suffers from minor fracturing or chipping. The dental filling is used to restore the tooth to its original structure and function, and return your smile to good health. We are proud to offer biocompatible tooth-colored fillings that can blend in seamlessly with your smile while strengthening your teeth.

Learn More About Tooth-Colored Fillings

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are small, cap-like structures that are designed to be secured over the top visible surface of teeth that have either received treatment or are damaged. Our team crafts our crowns from a variety of high-quality materials depending on your aesthetic goals, including gold, ceramic, and zirconia. Once cemented onto the affected tooth, the crown will help protect it from future harm and allow you to use it like you normally would!

Learn More About Dental Crowns

CEREC Same-Day Crowns

Typically, dental crowns take about two weeks to craft when they’re sent to a dental laboratory. With our CEREC in-house milling machine, CAD/CAM software, and digital impression system, our dentists are able to design, create, and place your crown in one appointment. Furthermore, your restoration will never leave our team’s site, allowing us to craft it with care, precision, and intention, as we’re the ones who know you and your unique dental needs!
