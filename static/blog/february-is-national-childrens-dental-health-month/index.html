February is National Children’s Dental Health Month

The American Dental Association has designated February as National Children’s Dental Health Month. (http://www.ada.org/en/public-programs/national-childrens-dental-health-month). This is a month to raise awareness of the importance of oral health and developing good oral hygiene habits. Children in the US miss 51 million School hours each year due to dental problems according to the NEA (National Education Association). Teaching all children to brush two minutes, two times a day can greatly decrease that number. Not to mention visiting the Dentist two times a year. The ADA has free, fun activities for kids to help ‘Defeat Monster Mouth’ on their website. Contact Artisan Dental today to schedule your child for an appointment! https://artisandentalmadison.com/
