Tooth Extractions Houston

Making Problem Teeth Disappear

Dr. Jetton always prioritizes saving teeth rather than removing them, but sometimes, a tooth can be damaged beyond repair. In these situations, we prioritize comfort and communication so you understand why we recommend a tooth extraction in Houston and how it will happen. That way, you enjoy peace of mind and a calm experience.

Why Choose New Teeth Dental Solutions - Houston for Tooth Extractions?

  • Same-Day Emergency Dental Appointments
  • Sedation Dentistry Available for Nervous Patients
  • Serving Houston Smiles Since 1981

When are Tooth Extractions Necessary?

  • When a tooth has severe decay or physical trauma to the point that it can’t be fixed with a filling or crown.
  • A tooth has been cracked beneath the gum line.
  • Advanced gum disease has made the tooth unstable.
  • More room is needed in the mouth for a denture or orthodontics.
  • The tooth is impacted, or stuck (often happens with wisdom teeth).
