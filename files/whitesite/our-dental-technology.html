Smarter Technology That Better Serves Your Smile

Modern technology allows us to deliver dentistry that is more precise, more comfortable, and more efficient than ever before. That’s why our practice continually invests in the latest tools to improve diagnosis, streamline treatment, and reduce your overall time in the chair. From our soft tissue laser that lets us perform a variety of treatments with ease, to digital impressions that ensure accurate, perfectly-fitting restorations, if it’s designed to improve your smile’s wellness, you can find it here.

Soft Tissue Laser

Our Picasso soft tissue laser offers a much gentler alternative to traditional treatment methods, which involve the use of a scalpel. Instead, this tool uses a beam of focused light, which is a more comfortable experience and encourages less swelling, less bleeding, and generally better healing. It’s a great option for gum recontouring, treating gum disease, and other gum-related procedures.

CEREC Same-Day Technology

With CEREC technology, you can receive a custom-made crown in just a single appointment. The process is simple: we first take a digital scan of your tooth, and then the restoration is designed virtually before our in-house milling machine fabricates it from a durable block of ceramic. There are no messy impressions, no temporary crowns, and typically no second appointments!

Digital Impressions

Thanks to our iTero digital scanner, gone are the days of messy, physical impression trays—instead, we can quickly and comfortably scan your teeth in just seconds using this small, handheld device. These precise digital impressions lead to better-fitting crowns, aligners, and other solutions, and they can all be previewed beforehand to ensure you feel confident.

CT/Cone Beam Scanner

Our CT/cone beam scanner provides detailed 3D images of your teeth, jawbone, and nerves in a single, quick scan. This level of detail is crucial when planning procedures like dental implants, since it ensures the posts go exactly where they need to. It’s also useful for detecting hidden problems, evaluating things like wisdom teeth, and assessing other concerns.
