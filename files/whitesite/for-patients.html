Your Smile’s Exciting New Journey Starts Here

Choosing a new dentist is an important decision, and we’re honored that you’re considering our practice! Our whole team is dedicated to making every patient feel comfortable, respected, and cared for from the moment they walk in. This page should cover what you can expect as a new patient so your first visit goes as smoothly as possible and you can arrive feeling prepared. Of course, if you don’t see something listed or you still have questions, don’t hesitate to give our office a call!

 

Your First Visit

During your first visit, we’ll get to know you and your smile through a comprehensive exam. We want this to be a warm and unhurried experience, so we’ll be sure to take the time to listen to your concerns, answer your questions, and learn about your dental goals. Our aim is for you to leave feeling confident and informed about your care! To help things go smoothly, please bring a few things along with you:

  • Photo ID
  • Insurance card (for those with dental insurance)
  • Questions, concerns, and hopes for your smile!

Dental Insurance & Payment Options

We believe cost should never stand in the way of great dental care. Our team accepts many insurance plans and is happy to help you understand your benefits, file claims on your behalf, and maximize your coverage. For added convenience, we also offer a variety of payment options. If you ever have questions about your plan or what you need to pay out-of-pocket, don’t hesitate to give our knowledgeable team a call!
