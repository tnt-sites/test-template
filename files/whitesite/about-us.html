About Our Medford Dental Office

Making a Difference in Medford Since 1979

Welcome to our practice! We believe great dental care starts with who we are, not just what we do. Our five core values guide every decision we make, every treatment we recommend, and every patient we serve. Here’s a closer look at what you can expect when you let us tend to your smile:

Honesty

You can handle the truth.?

Whether it’s good news or bad news, we’ll always give you the real news. We believe transparency and truth help you make the best decisions about your care.

Integrity

We embody the genuine core of who we are.?

It’s always the right thing to do the right thing, even when it’s hard. Here, you can fully expect us to do the right thing for you. We put people over profits, every time.

Grace

Our hearts lead our hands.?

We’re people serving people. Great dentistry begins with empathy, understanding, and compassion. Then it delivers real-life solutions that meet you right where you are.

Hard Work

You deserve our best.?

However you want to say it – second mile service, above and beyond care – we do it. We work hard to bring our best to the table for every visit.

Curiosity

Advancements keep coming.?

From cutting-edge technology to innovative treatments, we continue to pursue the best and latest options available for you, like Botox®, Invisalign®, sleep apnea solutions, pain-free digital X-rays, CEREC same-day crowns and bridges, cone beam imaging, laser dentistry, scrape-free air abrasion, gunk-free digital scanning, and much more.
