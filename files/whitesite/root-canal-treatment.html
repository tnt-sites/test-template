What Happens During a Root Canal?

The root canal process begins with us numbing your mouth so that you stay comfortable throughout the procedure. From there, a small opening is made in the tooth, and the infected and inflamed tissue is carefully removed. The root canals are also cleaned, and then the tooth is disinfected before it’s shaped with a biocompatible material and sealed. Most teeth are also protected with a dental crown to help restore strength and function to the tooth.
