Get to Know Wenyu Qu, DMD

Dr. Wenyu Qu is dedicated to providing thoughtful, high-quality dentistry, and his patients truly appreciate his gentle touch as well as his attention to detail, no matter how big or small the case may seem. He’s always sure to listen to his patients and walk them through every option, so that when it comes to their results, they can smile happily and healthily for years to come.

Background & Education

Originally from Lincoln, Nebraska, Dr. Qu completed his undergraduate years at Vanderbilt University in Nashville, Tennessee, with a degree in molecular and cellular biology. Following graduation, he attended Boston University, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree and fell in love with Boston. At BU, he received foundational training in digital dentistry, learning to incorporate CAD/CAM technology into his practice.

Advanced Dental Training

Dr. Qu achieved his Certificate of Advanced Graduate Studies in a General Practice Residency at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where he managed complicated treatment plans and engaged in the unique challenges of special needs dentistry. He has also completed training in implant dentistry through Smile Systems and is experienced in both implant placement and implant restorations.

Home Life

Dr. Qu loves spending time with his wife, traveling the world, and watching good movies.
