Veneers

Veneers are small, thin coverings that are applied to the front surfaces of teeth to mask flaws like small chips, stubborn stains, and even slight gaps. They’re normally made from porcelain since it mimics the sheen of your natural enamel, and they can be custom-designed so that they’re the correct size and shape of your teeth.

Metal-Free Restorations

Metal-free restorations give you a strong, lasting solution that blends in seamlessly with the rest of your smile, since they don’t contain any silvery, gray metal materials. These are ideal for patients who want a healthier, more natural-looking alternative that’s also biocompatible and gentler on the mouth.

Cosmetic Bonding

Cosmetic bonding is a quick, affordable way to refresh your smile, and it only takes a single appointment. We’ll first apply a tooth-colored composite resin over any teeth with flaws like small chips or stains, and once it’s been sculpted into an ideal shape, it’s hardened with a special light and polished to perfection.

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening at our office delivers brighter, longer-lasting results than any over-the-counter products. We use Zoom! in-office whitening and take-home kits to ensure consistent, natural-looking results for even the toughest stains. We offer Glo in-office as well, which uses LED-activated technology for a comfortable experience..

Gum Recontouring

If you have an uneven gum line or excessive gum tissue covering up large portions of your teeth, you’ve probably noticed the impact it has on your smile. Gum recontouring gently reshapes excess or uneven gum tissue to create a more balanced and proportional smile. Plus, the procedure is quick and comfortable with minimal downtime, thanks to our office’s soft tissue laser.

BOTOX

After we’ve rejuvenated your smile, BOTOX can be the ideal complement that helps your overall facial appearance feel smoother and more confident as well. With a few precise injections, we can minimize the look of facial lines, wrinkles, crow’s feet, and other imperfections in the skin.

Smile Makeovers

A smile makeover combines several cosmetic treatments into one personalized plan built around your specific goals. We’ll start by using virtual smile design to show you a preview of what’s possible before any work begins, and then once we’ve settled on what treatments are needed, it’s carried out in phases and may include any combination of services from teeth whitening to veneers and beyond.
