Dental Checkups & Cleanings

Regular checkups and cleanings are the cornerstone of good oral health, even if you stay on top of your brushing and flossing habits. During each visit, our team will carefully remove plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing can’t reach. We’ll also screen for cavities, check for signs of gum disease, and take X-rays when needed to ensure we have the full picture. Most patients benefit from these visits every six months, but we can tailor your schedule to your individual needs.

Gum Disease Treatment

Gum disease is a leading cause of tooth loss in adults, but it’s actually highly treatable when caught early. For many patients, scaling and root planing is the first step in combating this condition. This deep cleaning removes the disease-causing bacteria from below the gumline and smooths the roots so that the nearby gum tissue can begin healing and reattaching.

Arestin, which is a localized antibiotic, can also be used to treat gum disease, often in tandem with scaling and root planing. For more advanced cases, we offer laser periodontal therapy (LANAP), which is a minimally invasive alternative to traditional gum surgery.

Nightguards for Bruxism

Many people grind or clench their teeth during sleep without realizing it, which can lead to worn-down or cracked teeth, jaw pain, and morning headaches. A custom-fitted nightguard creates a protective barrier between your upper and lower teeth, absorbing pressure and preventing damage. Made from an impression of your teeth, these devices are comfortable, durable, and much more effective than store-bought options.

Icon Resin Infiltration

Icon resin infiltration is designed to treat early cavities before they progress to the point of needing a filling. It’s great for addressing cavities as they form between the teeth, since these can be harder to notice as they develop. Unlike traditional fillings, there is no drilling needed; instead, a special resin is applied that penetrates the weakened enamel, stopping decay in its tracks and reinforcing the tooth. It can also help improve the appearance of mild white spot lesions on teeth.

Curodont Treatment

Curodont is a revolutionary, no-drilling treatment that literally helps your teeth to repair themselves! Applied to early-stage cavities and white spot lesions, it uses peptide-based technology to form a scaffold that attracts the calcium and phosphate from your saliva, which in turn fortifies your enamel. The best part is, this process is quick, painless, and preserves your natural tooth structure.
