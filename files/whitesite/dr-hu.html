Get to Know Anqi Hu, DDS

Guided by her core values of honesty, integrity, hard work, and curiosity, Dr. Hu believes in treating every patient like family and living by the motto, “When we know better, we do better.”

Background & Education

Dr. Anqi Hu is dedicated to helping patients achieve healthier smiles, improved function, and lasting confidence through thoughtful, personalized dental care. She completed her undergraduate studies at Emory University and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry, one of the nation’s leading dental programs. Following dental school, Dr. Hu pursued advanced clinical training through an Advanced Dental Education program at Case Western Reserve University and Cleveland Medical Center, where she refined her skills in comprehensive, patient-centered dentistry and complex treatment planning.

Advanced Dental Training

Dr. Hu is passionate about lifelong learning and staying at the forefront of modern dentistry, so her patients always have access to the most advanced and effective care. She is an active member of the Academy of GP Orthodontics and the American Academy of Sleep Medicine, reflecting her strong commitment to whole-health dentistry. Her clinical focus includes Invisalign® clear aligner therapy, oral appliance therapy for sleep health, and facial injectables, allowing her to enhance not only smiles but also comfort, function, and overall well-being.

Dr. Hu’s philosophy is simple yet meaningful: treat every patient the way she would care for her own loved ones—with honesty, compassion, and attention to each individual’s unique needs.

Home Life

Outside the office, Dr. Hu enjoys maintaining an active lifestyle through fitness and spending quality time with her husband, family, and her beloved golden doodle. She believes that balance, wellness, and strong relationships are essential to living well—values she brings into her care for every patient she serves.
