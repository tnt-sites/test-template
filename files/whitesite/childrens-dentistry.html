Kids’ Dental Checkups & Cleanings

Regular checkups and cleanings are the foundation of lifelong oral health. During these visits, we can gently remove plaque and tartar, check for cavities, and monitor how your child’s teeth and jaws are developing. These visits also help kids build good habits and get comfortable with the dentist.

Silver Diamine Fluoride

Silver diamine fluoride (SDF) is a painless, needle-free method used to stop cavities from progressing. We can apply it directly to your child’s affected teeth, where it kills decay-causing bacteria and strengthens enamel. It’s an excellent option for young children, as well as those for whom traditional fluoride solutions aren’t preferred.

Dental Sealants

Sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth, where deep grooves trap food and bacteria. The best part about these is that they don’t require any drilling, making them very kid-friendly! They’re also able to withstand the daily forces of biting and chewing, serving as an effective barrier for many years.  

Athletic Mouthguards/Sportsguards

Whether your child plays football, basketball, soccer, hockey, or another contact sport where a dental injury is likely, a mouthguard is essential protective gear. Unlike store-bought options, the mouthguards we can provide for your child are made from an impression of their teeth, meaning they’ll fit perfectly and comfortably, in addition to providing superior protection.
