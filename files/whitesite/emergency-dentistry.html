Emergency Dental Treatments We Provide

Our doctors provide comprehensive emergency dental care focused on bringing you relief and restoring your oral health. Our available treatments include:

  • Tooth extractions: When we cannot save a severely damaged or infected tooth, gentle extraction provides immediate pain relief and prevents complications from spreading.
  • Crown placement and repair: We restore damaged teeth with temporary or permanent crowns, protecting your tooth structure and helping you feel comfortable again.
  • Root canal treatment: This treatment removes infected tissue while saving your natural tooth, often providing significant pain relief within hours.
  • Restoration re-cementation: Lost crowns, bridges, or fillings can be quickly re-secured or replaced, restoring both function and your peace of mind.
  • Fractured tooth repair: We offer several approaches for broken teeth—from simple bonding to protective crowns—tailored to your specific situation.
  • Soft tissue injury treatment: Injuries to your gums, cheeks, or lips receive gentle, thorough care to promote proper healing and prevent infection.

If you're experiencing something not listed here, please don't hesitate to call. Dr. Qu, Dr. Hu, or Dr. Hobaica will carefully evaluate your situation and ensure you receive the right care, whether in our office or through a trusted specialist referral.

Emergency Dentistry Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if my tooth gets knocked out?

We understand this can be frightening, but acting quickly gives us the best chance to save your tooth. Handle the tooth by the crown only, avoiding contact with the root. Rinse gently with water if debris is present, but don't scrub. Keep the tooth moist by placing it back in the socket if possible, or store it in milk. Time is critical—the success of reimplantation decreases significantly with each passing hour.

Can I come in today for dental emergency care even if I'm not a current patient?

Absolutely. We welcome new patients for emergency dental care and understand that urgent situations don't wait for established relationships. Simply describe your situation when you call, and we'll arrange the earliest available appointment to help you.

Will you just pull the tooth or try to save it?

We're committed to preserving your natural teeth whenever possible. Our team will always carefully evaluate each tooth's condition and discuss all viable treatment options with you. Extraction is recommended only when conservative treatments aren't clinically appropriate, and we'll always explain our reasoning so you understand your options.

How do I know if a toothache is a real emergency?

Trust your instincts—if you're concerned, we're here to help. Seek immediate care for severe or worsening pain, facial or jaw swelling, fever, or pain that disrupts sleep. These symptoms may indicate infection or other serious conditions requiring prompt treatment. When you're uncertain about urgency, don't hesitate to call and describe your symptoms—we'll help assess the situation.

What if my dental emergency happens after hours?

Don't worry—we're still here to help. Call (781) 395-5545 and follow the recorded instructions. We'll provide guidance on managing your situation and help determine whether immediate care is necessary or if treatment can safely wait until the next business day.
