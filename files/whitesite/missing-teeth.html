Dental Bridges

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring a custom-made replacement tooth to the healthy teeth on either side of the gap. Made from durable and natural-looking materials, these replacement teeth can restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence. They typically rely on the neighboring teeth for support but can sometimes be bolstered with dental implants.

Dentures

Dentures offer a dependable way to replace multiple missing teeth, or even entire arches, since they restore both function and appearance. In addition to offering traditional full and partial dentures, we can also provide implant-retained dentures and All-on-X here at our Medford office, which offer more stability as well as other benefits.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are widely regarded as the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. A small titanium post can be placed directly into the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone to act as an artificial tooth root. It supports a custom crown that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth. Implants can help to prevent bone loss, restore most of your biting power, and can last for decades with the right care.

Full Mouth Reconstruction

Full mouth reconstruction is a comprehensive, personalized approach that rebuilds and/or restores all of the compromised teeth in your mouth, normally carried out in phases. It may include things like crowns, bridges, implants, gum disease treatment, and other procedures to correct extensive damage, wear, bite function, or tooth loss.
