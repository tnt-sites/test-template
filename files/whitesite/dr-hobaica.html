Get to Know Benjamin Hobaica, DMD

Background & Education

A native of Clinton, New York, Dr. Benjamin Hobaica graduated Magna Cum Laude from Utica College with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. He went on to graduate Cum Laude while earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, Boston University. Following graduation, he completed a one-year post-doctoral professional education program at the James M. Rozanski Dental Residency program in Utica, New York.

Advanced Dental Training

Dr. Hobaica is a member of the New York State Dental Association, the Massachusetts Dental Society, and is involved in the American Academy of Implant Dentistry. He is also an attending faculty member for dental students from both Boston University and Tufts University and is involved with community health in suburban Boston. His interest in both implant placement and implant restorations has led to completion of the Boston MaxiCourse, an AAID-accredited program involving 300 course hours of surgical education and training in implant dentistry. In addition to implant dentistry, Dr. Hobaica is committed to all aspects of general practice, including restorative (fillings), endodontics, extractions, and cosmetic dentistry.

Home Life

He loves spending time with family and friends. As a former college baseball player and coach, he also enjoys watching a local baseball or hockey game.
