Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are perhaps the most versatile of all restorations. They are small caps that can be custom-made to look and feel just like your natural teeth. When a tooth is severely damaged or decayed, a dental crown is often able to cover and protect it from future harm. They’re also the go-to restoration following a root canal. With the right care, a dental crown can last for several years.

CEREC Same-Day Dental Restorations

In the past, the process of receiving a dental crown usually took multiple appointments, not to mention it was generally uncomfortable since physical impressions had to be taken. We’re excited to offer a more modern and effective approach using CEREC technology. We can now plan out, create, and place a high-quality dental crown in just one visit. There’s no need for physical impressions, no need for a temporary crown, and no waiting around for your permanent solution.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings are one of the go-to, time-tested methods for repairing cavities while keeping your smile looking natural. Made from a durable, biocompatible resin, they’re carefully matched to the shade of your surrounding teeth, while also bonding directly to your enamel and allowing more of your tooth’s structure to be preserved.
