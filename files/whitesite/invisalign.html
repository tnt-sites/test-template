How Invisalign Works

Your Invisalign journey begins with a consultation and a digital scan of your teeth, which is then used to create a precise treatment plan. You’ll advance through different sets of aligners, and as they’re worn, they will apply gradual pressure to your teeth that facilitates micromovements over the course of your plan. The teeth are slowly but surely brought into their ideal positions, and you’ll continue advancing through your subsequent sets until your teeth are perfectly aligned and your bite is harmonious.

Is Invisalign Right for Me?

Most people who wonder about clear aligners turn out to be excellent candidates. Invisalign addresses many common orthodontic issues effectively, and both adults and teens achieve great results with this treatment at Medford Dental Wellness.

The conditions it corrects most successfully include:

  • Crowded teeth that overlap or feel difficult to clean properly
  • Gaps between teeth that affect the look or feel of your smile
  • Mild to moderate bite issues, including overbite, underbite, and crossbite
  • Minor relapse after previous orthodontic treatment

That said, every smile is unique. More complex bite or jaw concerns sometimes require alternative treatments, and the only way to know for certain is through a thorough examination. If you've been putting off straightening your teeth because you weren't sure it was possible without traditional braces, it's worth finding out. Many patients are surprised by what clear aligners can actually accomplish for their specific situation.

What Happens at Your Invisalign Appointment

Getting a detailed understanding of the process makes it much easier to commit, so here's exactly what your care looks like at Medford Dental Wellness from start to finish.

Your first appointment combines consultation and records gathering. Our team will review your bite, discuss your goals, and take digital impressions of your teeth. There are no goopy molds involved. Those scans feed directly into the Invisalign system, which uses them to map out your tooth movement and fabricate your custom aligners.

Once your trays arrive at the office, you'll return to receive them. During this session, we'll walk you through proper wear and care, and may place small tooth-colored attachments on certain teeth to help the trays grip and move them precisely. You'll leave with your first sets of aligners and complete instructions.

From there, your progress check-ins are typically brief. Each time, we'll confirm your teeth are tracking as planned and provide your next sets of aligners. These appointments are usually spaced several weeks apart, so Invisalign fits into a busy schedule without constant disruption.

At treatment completion, you'll have a final assessment to review your results and discuss retainer options to maintain your new smile long-term.

How Long Does Invisalign Treatment Take?

For most patients, clear aligner treatment runs somewhere between 6 and 18 months. Where you fall in that range depends on what your smile needs: minor crowding or spacing issues tend to resolve on the shorter end, while more involved bite corrections take longer.

The single biggest factor in staying on schedule is consistent wear. Aligners need to be in for 20 to 22 hours each day to do their job. That leaves time for meals, brushing, and flossing, but not much else. Patients who treat their aligners as optional tend to see their timelines stretch.

A few other factors that influence treatment duration:

  • The severity of crowding, spacing, or bite misalignment
  • How reliably you move through each set of aligners on schedule
  • Whether any mid-course refinements are needed along the way

During your consultation at Medford Dental Wellness, our team will assess your specific situation and provide a personalized timeline estimate so you know exactly what to expect.

Frequently Asked Questions About Invisalign

Does Invisalign hurt?

Most people feel some pressure for a day or two when they switch to a new set of aligners. It's not sharp pain, more like the feeling that something is working. It typically fades quickly as your teeth adjust.

Can I eat and drink normally when using Invisalign?

Yes, because you remove the aligners before meals. You can eat whatever you want without worrying about broken brackets or food restrictions. Just brush before putting them back in.

What if I lose an Invisalign aligner?

Let us know as soon as possible. Depending on where you are in treatment, we'll either move you to the next set early or order a replacement. It happens, and it's not a crisis.

How do I clean Invisalign aligners?

Rinse them with lukewarm water and brush them gently with a soft toothbrush. Avoid hot water, which can warp the plastic. Keeping them clean also keeps your breath fresh throughout the day.

Will people be able to tell I'm wearing Invisalign aligners?

Most people won't notice at all. The aligners are clear and fit closely against your teeth. Some patients wear them through work presentations and social events without anyone saying a word.

Is Invisalign faster than braces?

It depends on the case, but treatment timelines are often comparable. The bigger factor is consistency: the more hours a day you wear your aligners, the more predictably your treatment stays on track.
