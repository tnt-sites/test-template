When are Tooth Extractions Necessary?

Extractions are recommended when a tooth can’t be repaired or when leaving it in place could prove harmful to your oral health. That said, we’ll always be sure to explain why it may be needed, and we can walk you through the process to ensure you’re confident before moving forward. Some common reasons for tooth extraction include:

  • Severe decay or damage that is too extensive to restore with a crown or filling
  • Advanced gum disease that has loosened the tooth
  • Infection or an abscess that can’t be treated
  • Impacted teeth that haven’t properly erupted (often the case with wisdom teeth)
  • Overcrowding that requires space for orthodontic treatment
