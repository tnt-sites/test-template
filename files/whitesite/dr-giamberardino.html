Get to Know Anthony Giamberardino, DMD

You’ll find few people who are as passionate about dentistry as Dr. Giamberardino; it’s safe to say it’s his biggest passion, in addition to being what he does for a living! That said, he’s spent the better part of his career honing his skills, continuing his education, and ensuring he has the skills and knowledge to help smiles thrive.

Background & Education

Dr. Anthony Giamberardino graduated with a Bachelor of Arts in Biology from Harvard College and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine.

Dental Experience

He began his career in 1985 as a private practice associate in Everett, Massachusetts. He eventually moved to Medford, Massachusetts, where he worked with Harvard Community Health Plan, treating and coordinating multi-specialty adult dental care. In 1993, he opened his own private practice. To this day, he continues to deliver excellence in adult restorative, preventive, and cosmetic dentistry.
