Individuals undergoing orthopedic treatment will always desire a brighter smile at the end of the day. That's where the choice of braces color comes in. Bearing in mind that the prime goal of braces is to improve your teeth' appearance, a perfect color brace choice will be most fulfilling. Please note that color braces don't whiten your teeth, for that you will need [professional teeth whitening](/austin-teeth-whitening/). However, choosing the right color can make your teeth look whiter and more appealing.

## Dark Braces:

[These braces make a difference based on the concept of contrast](https://yorkortho.ca/what-braces-color-are-used-for/). When you choose dark-colored braces, which can include deep purple or blue, you create some form of contrast to your ever-shining teeth. This gives an illusion of whiter teeth. This could be the case if you applied dark lipstick on your lips to contrast your bright-colored skin. Your skin will be more appealing.

However, make sure you find a balance between dark braces and white teeth. Extremely dark braces can create a negative impression, drawing more attention to braces and not your lovely teeth.

## Light Subtle Braces:

Subtle colors like baby pink can be extremely effective choices for your brace colors. The associated soft tone of the braces reduces their visibility, making your white more visible to the outside world. On the other hand, sublime silver braces draw a stylish vibe of teeth, with less attention being drawn to the braces.

## Bright Braces:

In this case, choose neon yellow or electric orange for your braces as they make your teeth brighter and easily noticeable. Are you a fashionista who wants your braces to stand out? Neon yellow or Electric orange should be your ultimate choice!

## Classic Clear:

If you are a patient who wants to achieve a more natural look in the presence of braces, then classic clear-color braces should be your first choice. These braces have less contrast, which means maintenance of your natural teeth look. Choose this color to maintain a decent look after your orthodontic treatment.

## Glitzy Gold:

This choice will not only add appeal to your teeth but also will make them look whiter.

The choice of brace color depends on your needs and preferences. You may want to create a natural look or make your teeth more appealing after the orthodoncy.
