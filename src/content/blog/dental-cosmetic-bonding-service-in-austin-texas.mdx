## **Preventative Dentistry**

General oral hygiene exams include cleanings, x-rays, and gum health checkups. These are important[routine and preventative procedures](https://www.cdc.gov/oralhealth/index.html) to ensure the teeth and gums are generally healthy. They are preventative in nature because they are designed to be scheduled twice per year in order to proactively address dental concerns before they become problematic.
