## Cracked Tooth Repair in Littleton

Despite their alarming appearance, they are typically nothing more than what we refer to as "craze lines." The craze lines on your teeth are tiny fractures in your tooth enamel that form from regular wear and tear and are only visible on the tooth's outermost layer. As long as the craze lines do not pierce the dentin, they are not dangerous.

It isn't uncommon for me to see patients afraid that they may [have a cracked tooth](https://www.healthline.com/health/cracked-tooth) that needs to be repaired. Do not be alarmed if you discover small, vertical lines on your teeth when looking in the mirror. Another significant distinction: a broken tooth is not the same as a chipped one. A dental instrument called an "explorer" might be used by dentists and hygienists to discover a chip in a tooth. It's also possible to discover a chip by touching the tooth's surface with your tongue. If the explorer can't discover a chip, the damage is very superficial and does not need further treatment.
