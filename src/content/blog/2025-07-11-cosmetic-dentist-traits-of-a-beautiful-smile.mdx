Do you want to know what makes a smile “attractive?” Researchers have spent countless hours studying this subject, trying to identify what people look for when hiring a potential candidate or meeting someone new. Many of the traits that are prioritized include whiter, straighter teeth and appropriate gum display. If these are traits you desire but currently lack, keep reading to discover how a visit with a [cosmetic dentist](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/cosmetic-dentistry.html) can help you achieve a truly beautiful smile.

## What Traits Define a “Beautiful Smile?”

It’s no secret that the first thing people notice when meeting someone new is their smile. This feature can make a significant difference when interviewing for a job, meeting someone new, or preparing to deliver a presentation to the board at your workplace.

But what constitutes a “beautiful smile?” Are there certain traits your pearly whites and gums must exhibit? According to researchers, yes, there are, and they include:

*   Tooth Color
*   Tooth Size
*   Gum Display
*   Gum Color
*   Straightness
*   Spacing
*   Bite

## Are There Ways to Address These Smile Issues?

Yes, there are many ways you and your cosmetic dentist can improve the look of your smile. Whether it’s an overbite, excess gum tissue, gaps between teeth, yellowed stains, chips, cracks, or oversized teeth that need to be addressed, customized solutions are readily available.

Some of the most common cosmetic dental treatments you might consider include:

*   **Custom Veneers:** These thin sheaths of porcelain or ceramic fit over the front surfaces of teeth to hide all imperfections. They are custom-made to change the size, shape, and color of teeth for a fully transformed appearance.
*   **Gum Recontouring:** Removing excess gum tissue around your teeth can create a more balanced aesthetic, allowing you to expose more of your tooth structure if you struggle with “short” or “stubby” teeth.
*   **In-Office Teeth Whitening:** Intrinsic (below the surface), extrinsic (surface), and age-related tooth stains can be removed with in-office teeth whitening that takes only one hour to complete.
*   **Metal-Free Dental Restorations:** Repairing broken or decayed teeth doesn’t require traditional amalgam. Instead, we use metal-free dental restorations for a more blended look that provides optimal protection.
*   **Smile Makeover:** This comprehensive approach to cosmetic treatment addresses multiple problem areas instead of treating each one individually, which can take longer and cost more.
*   **SureSmile Clear Aligners:** This subtle orthodontic solution can address overcrowding, crooked, or gapped teeth, as well as a misaligned bite. This allows for a more radiant and functional smile.

Give your smile the chance to shine. With cosmetic dentistry, you can make the kind of impression that leads to the kind of positive and transformational results you deserve in all areas of your life.
