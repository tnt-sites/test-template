**Artisan Dental featured in WDA Journal** Artisan Dental is excited to share that we are featured in 2 articles in the current issue of [Wisconsin Dental Association Journal](https://www.wda.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/21_WDAJ_SeptOct_FINAL.pdf) (WDAJ).

1\. GREEN DENTISTRY: Madison practices find multifaceted value in sustainability.
A Q&A discussing sustainability in dentistry.*(beginning on page 10)*

2. [Carbon neutral certification](/carbon-neutral/) helps Artisan Dental serve all stakeholders.
*(page 14)*

“We see carbon offsetting as an important element of a comprehensive plan to reduce our negative impact and enhance our positive impact, realizing our mission to serve all of the stakeholders connected to our business,” said [Scott Andersen](/doctors-team/scott-andersen/), Artisan Dental’s Director of Stakeholder Stewardship and co-owner of the practice with his wife, [Dr. Nicole Andersen](/doctors-team/dr-nicole-andersen/). “We feel we have both a responsibility and opportunity to be good stewards of the environment we all enjoy and depend on.”
