Even with regular dental visits, your teeth can become stained and discolored from various factors, including red wine, coffee, tea, and tobacco use as well as the natural process of aging.

[Texas Health and Human Services reports show](https://www.dshs.state.tx.us/dental/pdf/Adults-and-Oral-Health-Brief.pdf) that, in addition to many other dental issues that can add up when left unaddressed, these effects can be even more pronounced in those who do not visit the dentist as recommended, which can take more time and costs to fix the problem once they do.

Stained teeth can take a toll on your confidence, which, according to [mental health experts](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/basics/confidence), can lead to anxiety and more. This, in turn, can cause you to smile less and shy away from social situations where you normally shine, resulting in missed opportunities in work and life.

However, [professional teeth whitening](/), such as the services offered by [Austin Express Teeth Whitening](/express-services/), can help boost your self-esteem in the following ways:

## Teeth Whitening Helps Restore a Youthful Appearance

Yellow, brown, and grayish stained teeth make your smile look worn and dull, which can show your age or cause you to look older than you are. It can also give the impression that you neglect your oral health, even if you don't.

Whiter, brighter teeth, on the other hand, are linked to a healthy, more youthful appearance.

Even whitening your teeth just a few shades lighter can help take years off your smile for a more vibrant look.

## Teeth Whitening Helps Stave off Plaque for Stronger, Healthier Teeth

Plaque buildup, also known as tartar, is the main cause of tooth decay and gum disease, which can eventually lead to tooth loss and more.

It also creates a sticky film on the teeth that allows foods to adhere to it, which can also cause tooth stains.

However, [teeth whitening is shown](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8352057/) to help prevent the formation of plaque following the procedure for long-term protection against the breakdown of the tooth enamel.

This way, you can enjoy whiter, brighter teeth knowing you have additional plaque-fighting power to complement your daily oral health regimen.
