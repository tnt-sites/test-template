## What to Know about Veneers

It only takes one or two imperfections in your smile to ruin its entire appearance. Minor issues with chipped, discolored, uneven or misshapen teeth can become major distractions to a beautiful smile. Thankfully, veneers can hide these flaws, leaving your smile flawless and beautiful. Just think, you can have the smile you’ve always wanted without invasive treatment or removing and replacing natural teeth.

Before opting for teeth veneers, it's crucial to understand a few things about this treatment.

1. **Veneers aren’t for everyone**– Problems with decay, oral infection, gum disease and bruxism can keep you from getting veneers.
2. **Veneers are permanent** – Drs. Glenn & Michelle Taylor will remove a small layer of your tooth enamel to accommodate your veneers. Once the veneers are bonded to your teeth, they become a permanent part of your smile, removable only by a dentist when it’s time to update your treatment.
3. **There are different types of veneers** – Porcelains are the strongest and longest-lasting veneers, enduring up to 15 years. Composite veneers are less durable and don’t last as long; however, they’re just as attractive and come at a lower cost.
4. **Veneer treatment is customizable** – You don’t have to treat all your teeth to get optimal results from veneers. You can place veneers only on the teeth you want to “fix.” We’ll ensure your veneers match the color, size and shape of nearby teeth to give your smile a seamless appearance.
5. **Veneers can “fix” multiple problems** - Veneers can address various cosmetic issues in one treatment, such as stained teeth, gaps between teeth, crooked teeth, chipped teeth, and misshapen teeth.
6. **Your choice of dentist makes all the difference** - Choosing an experienced dentist for your veneers can significantly impact the success of your treatment. Drs. Glenn & Michelle Taylor are highly skilled in their field and can help you get the most from your procedure. We’ll work with you in selecting the type of veneers you need and outlining a treatment plan you’re comfortable with to produce the beautiful results you desire.
