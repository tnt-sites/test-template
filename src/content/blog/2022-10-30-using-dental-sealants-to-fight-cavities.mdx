## How dental sealants can protect the smile

Dental sealants are an integral part of preventive dental care. Sealants are actually thin, plastic coatings that are applied to the back teeth — the molars and premolars. These teeth have grooves on their surfaces that are deep enough to trap food and bacteria. Over time, these trapped substances can lead to tooth decay.

## How does a dental sealant work?

Sealants act as a special barrier, protecting the teeth from decay-causing bacteria. They are most commonly used in children and adolescents, but they can be used in adults as well. This extra line of defense against cavities and periodontal disease can save the smile from significant damage caused by these conditions.

## When are dental sealants used?

Dental sealants are usually applied soon after the permanent teeth come in, around the ages of six to eight years old. However, they can be applied to older children and adults who have deep grooves in their teeth that are susceptible to decay.

## How long will a dental sealant last?

Sealants will last for several years, but they will eventually need to be replaced. Your dentist at Taylor Street Dental will check the sealants at your regular dental visits and reapply them if necessary.
