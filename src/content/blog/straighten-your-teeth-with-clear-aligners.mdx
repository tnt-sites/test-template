Clear aligners have grown hugely popular over the past few years as a better alternative to traditional braces. They are made of clear plastic and are made from molds of the mouth to properly fit so crooked or crowded teeth can realign into proper position. If you are considering getting clear aligners, you should know more about them.

## How do Clear Aligners Work?

Just like traditional braces, clear aligners are worn in the mouth to reposition the teeth. The orthodontist takes X-rays and molds of the teeth so the patient can have the proper fit. Thanks to wonderful advancements in technology, special software is used to create a treatment process for each patient. Once the clear aligners are made, the patient can begin to fix their alignment issue as their teeth begin straightening. While you use the aligners, you are normally required to come in for a checkup every six or so weeks. Austin Invisalign dentistry will ensure that you have the appropriate number of follow-up appointments.

On average, clear aligners are meant to be worn for around 22 hours per day. You are able to remove them to brush your teeth, floss, and eat meals. Usually, a new set of aligners is required to be used one to two weeks. As your teeth shift into a proper position, you need a new set. The number of aligners needed depends on the individual patient and their needs. Austin cosmetic dentistry can ensure that you get what you need out of your aligners.

## How Long Does Treatment with Clear Aligners Last?

In general, the total amount of time it takes for treatment with clear aligners depends on the individual patient and the condition of their teeth. When you receive Austin Invisalign dentistry, treatment time is based on how much the teeth need to shift in the mouth. This means that the more crooked the teeth or the further off the bite, the longer the treatment. However, on average, treatment with clear aligners can take anywhere from 10 to 24 months. If you have previously had braces and your teeth shifted a little again over time, you might only require 10 weeks of treatment. No matter what your circumstances, the Austin dental spa will inform you of your expected amount of treatment time.

## What are the Advantages of Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners carry many advantages that make them popular alternatives to traditional braces. One of the most obvious is that they are made of clear plastic, which makes them virtually unnoticeable to most people. This can eliminate a lot of the self-consciousness you might otherwise have if you had metallic braces.When you get clear aligners from Austin cosmetic dentistry, you can breathe easier knowing that the aligners are comfortable to wear. Unlike braces, they are not made of wire and brackets, so you don’t have to worry about potential cuts and painful nicks in your mouth.  
Clear aligners are also easier to care for and clean. You can remove them to eat and drink, so you don’t have to worry about getting food particles stuck in your teeth and not being able to completely remove them. This also helps you to practice better oral hygiene, in general.
