## What is Laser Deep Cleaning?

[Laser deep cleaning](/vista-ca/laser-deep-cleaning/), also known as laser-assisted periodontal therapy, involves the use of specialized dental lasers to effectively clean and treat oral tissues. Unlike traditional cleaning methods, laser deep cleaning is minimally invasive and virtually pain-free, making it a preferred choice for many patients. The dental laser emits a focused beam of light that selectively targets and eliminates bacteria, plaque, and tartar buildup, while preserving healthy tissues.

## Benefits for Teeth

One of the primary benefits of laser deep cleaning is the thorough removal of plaque and tartar. The precision of the laser enables it to target and destroy bacteria, eliminating hard-to-reach deposits that grow in hard to reach places like under your gum line. The laser's ability to specifically target bacteria helps in achieving a more comprehensive cleaning compared to traditional methods. By removing these bacterial accumulations, laser deep cleaning significantly reduces the risk of tooth decay, helping your mouth stay cleaner and healthier.

## Benefits for Gums

Laser deep cleaning [offers remarkable benefits for gum health](/vista-ca/laser-deep-cleaning/). If you’ve been diagnosed with gum disease, this cleaning is especially effective because of its ability to specifically target and eliminate the bacteria that causes gum disease. Without treatments like this, gum disease could lead to gum recession, tooth loss, and other serious oral health problems.

An added benefit is that the laser can also encourage gum tissue to regrow, helping your gums to heal faster. It even reduces gum inflammation and bleeding, as the laser can seal blood vessels during the procedure, providing immediate relief and comfort. With targeted cleaning, this technique minimizes the risk of gum recession, keeps your gum tissue intact, and prevents tooth sensitivity and pain.

## Benefits for Overall Oral Health

Laser deep cleaning plays a crucial role in maintaining overall oral health. By removing the bacteria that causes gum disease, it prevents the progression of periodontal disease, which, if left untreated, can lead to severe oral health complications.

Gum disease has been linked to various systemic health conditions such as cardiovascular disease, diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis, and more. By addressing gum disease at its root, laser deep cleaning will help maintain a healthy mouth and reduce the risk of these potential complications.
As an added benefit, oral bacteria are responsible for bad breath, so deep clearing will also help you have better, fresher breath and even whiter teeth! resulting in fresher and more pleasant breath.
