## What Is Teeth Grinding?

[Teeth grinding](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/guide/teeth-grinding-bruxism) is the habit of forcefully pressing two teeth together by tightly clamping them. This gives the patient a sense of relief from stress. Teeth grinding doesn't always occur at night during sleep, as many people think. Some patients clench their teeth during the day, and in extreme situations, it is a constant habit.

**Teeth Grinding Causes and Symptoms**

Personality traits are usually the main cause of teeth grinding. Highly stress-prone people and those who easily get angered or irritated are likely to [clench their teeth](/restorative-dentistry/). In some cases, parents can pass down the habit of teeth grinding to their children.

**Are All People Who Grind Their Teeth at Fault?**

Teeth grinding is often associated with [anxiety](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/323454) and stress, but it also has physical causes such as a misaligned bite or an abnormally positioned jaw. The problem also occurs when the teeth are decayed, worn out or misaligned.

If the jaw is not in proper condition, then the muscles around it become irritated, and this causes teeth grinding. At times, stress worsens the problem, causing the patient to grind teeth more often. Reaching out to our Austin Cosmetic Dentistry in such a case can help.
