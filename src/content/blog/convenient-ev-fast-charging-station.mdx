Artisan Dental is fortunate to have one of the most powerful (EV) charging stations conveniently located right across the street from us! [MGE's EV fast charging hub](https://www.mge.com/newsroom/news-releases/articles/mge%e2%80%99s-ev-fast-charging-hub-is-open) is located at the corner of E Washington Ave and S Livingston St and features 8 fast-chargers and through a partnership with Tesla, the hub also has 8 Superchargers.

MGE's ChargePoint public charging stations provide electricity that is 100% renewable – generated by [wind farms in Wisconsin and Iowa](https://www.mge.com/our-environment/green-power/wind-power/mge-wind-farms). As a sustainable business, we are excited to have this option available for the convenience of our team and our patients.
