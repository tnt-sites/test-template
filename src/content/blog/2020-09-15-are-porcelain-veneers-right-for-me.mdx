## What are porcelain veneers?

Dental veneers are ceramic restorations that are made to cover imperfections on the teeth near the front of the smile, known as the anterior teeth. These teeth are the most noticeable when patients smile, laugh, and speak with others. When problems occur on these teeth, many patients opt to cover them with porcelain veneers to disguise the issues and improve the appearance of the smile. This can completely transform a patient’s smile, especially if they have been self-conscious about the appearance of their teeth for many years!

## Are porcelain veneers right for me?

When it comes to cosmetic dentistry, patients of Springfield, MA have a wide selection of services to choose from. Porcelain veneers are just one of many options that patients can consider when looking to enhance their smiles. The best candidates for porcelain veneers are patients who want a long-lasting restoration that covers specific problems near the front of the mouth and are free from cavities and periodontal disease. It is also important that patients considering porcelain veneers visit their dentist regularly, as the natural tooth structure underneath the veneer is still susceptible to cavities and periodontal disease. These conditions can not only impact the health of these teeth, but the smile as a whole.
