According to an article in the [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2010/10/21/fashion/21SKIN.html), more than half a million Americans chose to enhance their smiles with veneers in 2006 alone. It is believed that the trend has steadily progressed each year since, making it one of the most popular dental procedures available.

## What Are Veneers, and How Are They Maintained?

Our natural teeth can become damaged and chipped over time. We may be born with crooked teeth or imperfections that detract from our beauty. Veneers allow dental patients to restore their smiles to a perfect exterior without the need for invasive implants or root canals.

Additionally, up to [50 percent of the alveolar bone's width](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6926561/) can be lost in the first year after a tooth extraction, making veneers a favored procedure.

Veneers are the ideal procedure for many patients who like the feel of their natural teeth and do not want to remove the root of the tooth. The veneers are thin shells of composite or porcelain material that are carefully molded and sculpted to fit seamlessly over natural teeth.

Veneers often require dentists to drill and remove the external material from the tooth without breaching the pulp chamber of the tooth. This makes patients eternally dependent on maintaining their veneers in pristine condition to retain a perfect smile.

Once the enamel is filed off the tooth, however, it is more prone to decay and must be cleaned more often and more thoroughly. This higher level of maintenance is critical in the period when the veneer fails and breaks off the tooth. Any exposed tooth material that is not properly sealed can start to decay much faster than a regular cavity develops.

## How Long Do Veneers Last?

Veneers can be expensive, and feel like they will last forever once they are initially fitted. The quality of dental care in fitting a patient with veneers can make all the difference in the world. Low-quality work can fail after just a few years. This is why many dentists offer a warranty to ease the risk of unexpected financial burdens if they do fail prematurely.

What many patients may not realize is that composite veneers typically require higher maintenance and have a shorter lifespan of seven years or less. Porcelain veneers are now becoming the favored treatment because they feel more like natural tooth material and last up to fifteen years with lower maintenance. *See* https://www.forbes.com/health/dental/how-long-do-veneers-last/
