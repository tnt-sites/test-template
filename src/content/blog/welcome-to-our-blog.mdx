## Meet Dr. Jetton

Dr. Brad Jetton was born and raised in Wichita Falls, where he knew early-on he wanted to pursue a career in dentistry. In addition to being fascinated by treating and caring for teeth, he also liked the idea of getting to know new and interesting people in the process!

After earning his bachelor's degree in biology at Midwestern State University and his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Texas Dental Branch (now called the UT Health Houston School of Dentistry). He also proudly completed the American Academy of Implant Dentistry’s Maxicourse to provide top quality dental implant treatment right here in the office!

## About Our Practice

In addition to offering a variety of tooth replacement options, Dr. Jetton and the team are pleased to be a full-service general dentistry practice, including treatments like preventive exams and cleanings, dental crowns and fillings, cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign, and emergency dentistry. We even offer sedation options to help keep you feeling calm and comfortable during any treatment!

We’re also proudly dental insurance friendly, welcome flexible financing, run frequent special offers, and have our [own in-house membership plan](/for-patients/#membership) for those who don’t have private dental insurance and want to save money on routine care.
