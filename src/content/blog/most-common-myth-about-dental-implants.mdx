Dental implants are a popular dental restorative treatment, but there are still many misconceptions and false information circulating online. From myths about dental implant failure rates to unnecessary aftercare practices, it is important to get the facts before undergoing any dental treatments. In this blog, we will discuss five of the most prevalent dental implant myths so that you can make an informed decision with confidence.

## Myth One - Dental Implants Are Not Durable

Many people worry that dental implants may not be durable enough for everyday wear and tear. However, when dental implants are placed properly and cared for correctly, they can last a lifetime. Dental implants fuse directly with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration, which provides superior stability compared to dentures or dental bridges.

### Quality Materials Used

Dental implants are made from titanium, which is biocompatible and highly durable. Titanium is also resistant to corrosion and bacteria buildup, making dental implants an ideal long-term dental restorative solution. The dental crowns that cover dental implants are made of porcelain or ceramic materials matched to your natural teeth shade. This ensures that your dental implant looks and functions just like your natural teeth.

### Proper Aftercare Practices

Caring for dental implants is much like caring for your natural teeth. You should brush and floss daily and visit the dentist every six months for routine dental checkups. It’s also important to avoid hard foods that could damage your dental implants, such as candy or ice cubes. Additionally, if you grind your teeth at night, make sure to wear a dental guard to reduce pressure on the dental implants over time.

## Myth Two - Dental Implants Are Painful

Many people think that dental implant surgery is painful, but this couldn’t be further from the truth! In fact, dental implant surgery is one of the most comfortable dental treatments available because anesthesia is used during the procedure. During dental implant surgery, your dentist will place the dental implant directly into your jawbone, and then attach a dental crown over the dental implant. These steps are completed quickly and with minimal discomfort.

### Local Anesthesia

To ensure that patients experience little to no pain during dental implant surgery, local anesthesia is used to numb the area around the dental implants. This allows your dentist to complete the procedure without causing any unnecessary pain or discomfort. The effects of local anesthesia usually wear off within several hours after dental implant surgery, so you can expect to be back in your regular routine soon after treatment.

### Post-Op Care

After [dental implant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_implant) surgery, some level of soreness or tenderness is normal. You may experience mild pain or discomfort while biting or chewing, but this should not last longer than a few days. Your dentist will provide instructions on how to care for your dental implants after surgery, such as taking medications and avoiding certain foods. It’s important to follow these instructions carefully in order to support dental implant healing and reduce the potential for dental implant complications.

### FAQs

Q: How much do dental implants cost?

A: The cost of dental implants varies based on the complexity of the case and your individual dental needs. Speak with your dentist to get more accurate pricing information for dental implant treatment.

Q: Are dental implants covered by insurance?

A: Some dental insurance plans may provide coverage for dental implant surgery, although this is not always the case. Be sure to check with your insurer before beginning dental implant treatment.

Q: Can I replace a single tooth with dental implants?

A: Yes! Dental implants can be used to replace one, several, or even all of your teeth. Your dentist can help you determine the best dental restoration option for your needs.

Knowing the truth behind common [dental implant](/dental-implants-littleton-co/) myths can help you make an informed decision about dental treatments with confidence. Dental implants are a safe and effective dental restorative solution that can last a lifetime when placed properly and cared for correctly! If you would like more information about dental implants, don’t hesitate to speak with your dentist.
