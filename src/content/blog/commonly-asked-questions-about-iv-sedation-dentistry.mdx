Many people have intravenous or [IV sedation](/vista-ca/dental-anesthesia/) for their dental procedures. The common reason for being under this type of sedation is dental anxiety. Requesting a form of sedation dentistry will prevent fear and pain from taking over. If you want to know more about **IV sedation**, here are some FAQs and the answers to them.

## What happens during this form of sedation dentistry?

_IV sedation_ is a type of anesthesia. An anesthesiologist will administer it through the patient’s vein. This form of sedation will help the patient relax during any dental procedure. The patient must go through a consultation with the dentist. This will help determine if this is the right form of sedation for the procedure.

The anesthesiologist will place an IV needle to connect the IV line to the patient’s vein. The top of the hand is the usual site. The sedative will run through the tube and into the patient’s bloodstream. The IV line will remain in place during the procedure. The anesthesiologist will watch the patient’s vital signs while the dentist focuses on the patient’s mouth.

### What are the different levels of IV sedation?

A dentist can offer two levels of sedation. Choosing between moderate sedation and deep sedation depends on what the patient needs or prefers. Moderate sedation is conscious sedation. This type of IV sedation gives the patient a high level of relaxation while remaining responsive to verbal instructions. Dentists recommend this for patients who need long dental treatments. They also suggest it for patients with moderate dental anxiety.

Deep sedation is for patients suffering from severe dental anxiety. It is for patients who will be having more invasive treatments like oral surgery. Many dentists refer to this sedation as a controlled state of depressed consciousness. In this state, the patient wakes up and falls asleep several times during the surgery. The patient will not remember what happened during the procedure. Any verbal command will not receive a response from the patient as well.

The last memory of the patient will be counting backward. Other clear memories after that will be the end of the procedure and the drive home. Grogginess after the procedure will be easy to sleep off. Giving the patient time to recover in a quiet room at home would be good for the patient’s well-being.

### How should the patient prepare for IV sedation?

The effects of IV sedation can last for hours after the procedure. Arranging for someone to drive the patient home after the procedure is in order. Refraining from eating or drinking for at least six hours before the procedure is also a standard. The patient cannot even drink juices, water, or coffee. This will prevent aspirating these liquids while unconscious.

Wearing tight clothes will impair breathing. It will also make it difficult to administer IV sedation. Refraining from wearing makeup will reveal the patient’s real skin color during the procedure. Removing contact lenses will prevent any damage to the patient’s cornea.

## IV sedation can help make your dental procedure more pleasant

Dental anxiety can be debilitating to some people. It can even prevent you from getting proper dental care. Your dentist can recommend IV sedation to help you or your loved one from having an anxiety attack at the clinic. The relaxation the patient receives will allow the dentist to perform the procedures with ease.
