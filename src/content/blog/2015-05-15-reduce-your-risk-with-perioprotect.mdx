Taylor Street Dental Associates is proud to offer a variety of treatments to protect your mouth from damage, disease and decay. Gum disease is one of the most debilitating oral health conditions possible, one that can negatively affect your overall health not just the gums. In order to offer the maximum protection possible from gum disease, we now offer PerioProtect. This treatment is customized for each patient and allows your dentist to manage the bacteria in the hardest to reach corners of your mouth. This includes the areas in between the teeth and along the gum line. The goal of PerioProtect is the manage oral bacteria with minimally invasive techniques. [](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/man-324103_1280.jpg)If you suffer from advanced gum disease and are considering scaling or root planing procedures it may be time to also consider [PerioProtect](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/gum-disease-treatment.html#perio). Unfortunately even after removed with scaling or planing, bacteria can regenerate and continue to infect your mouth. PerioProtect uses custom dental trays to apply a prescription solution to your teeth and gums to help manage the presence of bacteria along the gum line and in between the teeth. It only takes a few minutes a day to dramatically improve your oral health. Call our office today to schedule a consultation to see if PerioProtect is right for you.
