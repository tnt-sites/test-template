## What Are Same-Day Crowns?

Same-day crowns are custom-made dental restorations designed, milled, and placed in your mouth in one appointment. Thanks to advanced CEREC (Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics) technology, your dentist can take digital impressions, create the crown on-site, and secure it within a few hours.

## Candidates for Same-Day Crowns

Most people who require a dental crown can be great candidates for same-day crowns. Here are some specific situations where you might benefit from this convenient option:

**Cracked or Broken Teeth**

For a cracked or broken tooth that needs immediate restoration, a same-day crown can quickly repair the damage and restore function, protecting your tooth from further harm.

**Tooth Decay**

When you have severe tooth decay and a filling isn't enough to regain strength, a same-day crown can cover and protect it, preventing further decay.

**Old or Damaged Crowns**

If you already have a damaged or worn-out crown, a same-day crown can be an excellent replacement option, allowing you to avoid multiple visits.

**Cosmetic Improvements**

Same-day crowns are also great for improving the look of teeth that are discolored, misshapen, or uneven, helping you achieve a natural-looking smile in just one visit.

## When Same-Day Crowns May Not Be Ideal

While same-day crowns are suitable for most, there are instances where there might be better options. Your dentist may recommend another restorative treatment for complex cases involving severe alignment issues or if you require extensive dental work.
