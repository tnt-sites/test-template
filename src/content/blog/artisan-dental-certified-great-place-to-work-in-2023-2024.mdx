[Great Place To Work](https://4ddl9.r.bh.d.sendibt3.com/mk/cl/f/sh/1f8JIKXx3IkdaCaCij0tUkIbgY/Wuh6XeaEXUoz) Certification recognizes employers who create an outstanding employee experience.

Great Place To Work Certification criteria are the global standard for quantifying and benchmarking the employee experience. Using the Great Place To Work Trust Model and the Trust Index Survey, evaluates workplace culture from the perspective from one of our most impactful stakeholders. The two-step process includes surveying employees and completing a short questionnaire about your workforce.

We're excited to join companies such as Salesforce, Hewlett Packard, Hyatt and many others as a Certified Great Place To Work!

We appreciate and thank all of our team members that work together to make Artisan Dental an office that delivers exceptional quality care, and does so in a respectful, empathetic, fun way.
