A [special needs dentist](/vista-ca/special-needs-dentistry/) serves a crucial role in dentistry because they can treat patients who may otherwise feel uncomfortable or not be able to visit a dentist. They are trained and licensed and focus on accommodating the needs of individuals who may need more emotional and physical support than the average dental patient.

## Reviewing the unique benefits a special needs dentist can offer

This review offers an overview of the benefits that a **special needs dentist** can provide and how they are different from a traditional dentist, who does not focus on treating patients that require special care. This should help patients make a more informed decision as to whether a _special needs dentist_ is right for them or a loved one.

### What makes a special needs dentist unique?

A special needs dentist treats patients who need additional care due to physical, mental, emotional, behavioral, and psychological conditions or limitations. These may include but are not limited to individuals with autism spectrum disorder, Down syndrome, traumatic brain injury, and dementia.

### When is a special needs dentist recommended?

Special needs dentists treat the dental needs of individuals with learning and development disabilities. They can also treat patients that may have physical limitations, such as those who are physically disabled or have mobility limitations. They can also treat the dental needs of individuals who may have a condition that increases the risk of dental anxiety or phobia, such as ADHD or an anxiety disorder. In essence, anyone who needs additional care and support (whether it is physical, mental, emotional, etc.) should consider a visit to a special needs dentist.

### How can a special needs dentist accommodate the special needs of patients?

A special needs dentist understands the uniqueness of treating individuals with specific conditions that may affect their oral health. For example, a child or adult with Down syndrome is at a higher risk of developing dry mouth, which subsequently can increase the risk of oral infections, tooth decay, and gum disease. A special needs dentist can address the specific risks with a long-term prevention plan.

### Additional benefits of visiting a special needs dentist

Special needs dentists understand how to make patients with special needs feel more comfortable and confident during their visits. Their staff is generally friendly and knows how to work with individuals who have physical or mental disabilities. They also know how to work with the parents and loved ones of someone with special needs, to ensure that they have the information that they need to properly care for the child or loved one's oral health in between dental visits with the special needs dentist.
