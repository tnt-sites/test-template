There are many different reasons why you would need to wear a nightguard provided by your dentist to assist with the issue of Bruxism. This is the scientific name for grinding your teeth while sleeping that has become a popular reason for visiting our [Austin Invisalign](/) Dentistry office. The Sleep Foundation explains each time you grind your teeth at night you are placing up to 250 pounds of force on your teeth.

## The Symptoms of Bruxism

Our Austin Preventative Dentistry office is staffed by professionals who are experts in understanding when Bruxism is taking place. The exact number of people who are affected by Bruxism is difficult to know because many of us do not know we are affected by this condition when it occurs during ur sleeping hours. [The Sleep Association](https://www.sleepassociation.org/sleep-treatments/night-guard/) has produced a comprehensive list of the symptoms associated with the medical condition of Bruxism including:

- Teeth grinding
- Dull headaches in the morning
- Pain and soreness around the face
- Fatigue caused by interrupted sleep

## Do you Need a Night Guard?

The [night guard](https://www.sleepassociation.org/sleep-treatments/night-guard/#:~:text=Night%20guards%20are%20also%20known,bite%20plates%2C%20or%20bite%20splints.&text=When%20you%20clench%20your%20jaw,the%20enamel%20of%20your%20teeth.) is one of the most impressive options for you when you are diagnosed by our Austin Invisalign Dentistry experts as suffering from Bruxism. There are many different names used for a nightguard, including the names mouth guard, bite plate, bite splint, and dental guards. Our dental professionals at The Toothbar will recommend a night guard when you visit our Austin preventative dentistry experts and look to understand the oral health issues you are facing. When you need a nightguard, our experts will look for the best options, including the use of an over-the-counter option that can be effective.

You will also find yourself faced with several choices when it comes to making the correct decision about which nightguard to chose. There are various options open to you that our experts at The Toothbar will help you to decide between. Different materials and strengths of material will be available to you depending on the severity of your teeth grinding.
