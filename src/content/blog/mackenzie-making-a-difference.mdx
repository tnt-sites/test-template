At Artisan Dental we appreciate that we are an interwoven part of a larger local and global community. We are heartened by the opportunity to support  organizations with owner and staff volunteer hours, free dental services, and/or ongoing financial contributions.

A recent example: Mackenzie, one of our amazing dental hygienists, and friends participated in the [2017 25th Scenic Shore 150 Bike Ride](http://events.lls.org/pages/wi/2017ScenicShore150/). This is a two day, 150-mile cycling event to benefit the Leukemia and Lymphoma Society along Lake Michigan. Mackenzie and friends were part of the 1500 cyclists that helped raise almost $1,400,000! Congratulations on completing such a worthwhile event and thank you for helping blood cancer patients live longer, better lives.
