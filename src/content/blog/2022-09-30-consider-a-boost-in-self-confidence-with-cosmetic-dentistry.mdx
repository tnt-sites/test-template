## What is cosmetic dentistry?

[Cosmetic dentistry](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/our-services.html) is a branch of the dental field that focuses on improving the appearance of teeth and gums. This can be done through various procedures, all of which are designed to give patients a smile they can feel confident about. _Some of the most common procedures performed by cosmetic dentists include:_

*   Teeth whitening: Teeth whitening is one of the most popular cosmetic dental procedures. It can be done in-office or at home, and it can make a big difference in the overall appearance of your smile.
*   Dental veneers: Veneers are thin, custom-made shells designed to cover up imperfections on teeth. They are usually made of porcelain or ceramic and can be used to fix various issues, including cracked, chipped, or discolored teeth.
*   Dental bonding: Dental bonding is a fast and easy procedure in which a tooth-colored composite resin is applied to the surface of a tooth to effectively improve its appearance. It can be used to fix a variety of different issues, including cracked, chipped, or discolored teeth.
*   Invisalign®: Invisalign® is a transparent aligner system used to straighten teeth. It is an alternative to traditional braces, and it can be used to correct a variety of different dental issues.
