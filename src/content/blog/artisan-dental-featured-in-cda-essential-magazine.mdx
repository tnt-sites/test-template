Artisan Dental is honored to be featured in The Canadian Dental Association Magazine.

The article "[Making a Dental Office Carbon Neutral](http://www.cda-adc.ca/en/services/essentials/2023/issue1/20/)" describes the multi-step process that Artisan Dental used to become environmentally sustainable.

*"We consider the environment to be a stakeholder in our practice because it provides our business with necessary inputs; clean air, clean water, and nutrient rich soil."*

You can find the article beginning on page 20 at this [link](http://www.cda-adc.ca/en/services/essentials/2023/issue1/20/).
