## What is Invisalign?

[Invisalign](https://www.invisalign.eu/en/what-is-invisalign/Pages/What-Is.aspx) reports the latest technology for straightening most teeth is clear plastic trays. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign moves the teeth using removable plastic trays that allow you to eat the foods you love and avoid the pain of metal wires installed in your mouth.

### The Popularity of Invisalign

[At Toothbar](/), we have seen a growing number of people using Invisalign to improve their smiles. There are ten million people estimated to use clear plastic trays in the U.S. alone. The speedy nature of using clear plastic liners coupled with the low cost has made this a popular option.

Teens and adults around the U.S. have put off the use of traditional braces because of the difficulties they have in finding food to eat and attending multiple orthodontist appointments. Using clear plastic liners with the help of the experts at Toothbar makes the process of straightening teeth easier to complete.

### How to Enjoy Using Invisalign?

The Invisalign brand is the most effective clear plastic liner on the market. By following the instructions of the Austin Invisalign dentistry experts at Toothbar, your Invisalign experience will be improved. The more time you spend wearing your clear plastic trays, the more effective the process will be.

[Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/health/does-invisalign-work#success-factors) reports your experience of Invisalign will be shortened when you wear your liners for between 20 and 22 hours. Only removing your liners to clean them and to eat will mean your teeth will be moved into shape in a short period of time.

### Eliminate the Pain of Traditional Braces

Traditional braces can be damaging to your mouth because they are manufactured from metal wires. Clear plastic liners have the ability to limit the self-confidence issues the wearers of braces suffer with as they move through the process during their awkward teenage years.

Traditional braces can mean you will have to avoid your favorite foods because they can cause problems for your metal braces. Because you can remove your clear plastic liner to eat and clean it, you will find a better way of completing the teeth straightening process.
