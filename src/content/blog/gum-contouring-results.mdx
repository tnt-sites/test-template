Having a beautiful and inviting smile is one of the most notable attractive qualities. However, you are worried that your gum line may be uneven or that the public may describe your smile as 'gummy.' Whereas many factors come into play in creating a [perfect smile](/), your teeth' size and shape have a significant role to play. Even of more importance is the ratio of the mouth to gums. If you describe your smile as 'gummy' or are unhappy with your smile, gum contouring surgery may be right for you.

## What is Gum Contouring?

When done on its own, [dental contouring](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/guide/gum-contouring-procedure) is considered a cosmetic procedure. Even though cosmetic procedures are viewed as unnecessary, the Toothbar understands that your self-esteem and subsequent mental health may be affected by a gummy smile. For cosmetic purposes, you may choose to have your gum reshaped to improve your smile. At Toothbar Austin cosmetic dentist, you will improve your confidence by correcting your smile.

For some people, gum contouring is necessary as part of periodontal procedures like crown lengthening, regenerative procedures, and pocket reduction.

Gum contouring is also not considered a cosmetic procedure when gum tissue is added after gum recession has occurred or trimming overgrown tissue covering part of the tooth crown.

General dentists and periodontists perform gum contouring. The procedure, when done for cosmetic reasons, may not be covered by your medical insurance. The cost for gum contouring varies depending on the complexity of each patient's needs. However, Austin cosmetic dentistry can guide you through the entire process, and the dentists do their best to bring out your confidence in your smile.

Gum contouring is done in the dentist's clinic using scalpels, radiosurgery, and lasers. The Toothbar ensures that the procedure is as pain-free as possible for your comfort. A local anesthetic may be applied to numb the area where the procedure is performed.

After surgery, healing may take a few days or weeks, and the dentist may prescribe pain medication and offer advice on foods to eat and how to brush. The procedure is not without risks, and rarely, the gum tissue is likely to relapse.

[Gum contouring results](https://www.colgate.com/en-us/oral-health/adult-oral-care/gum-reshaping) are permanent, and when done by a proper dentist, your smile is guaranteed to improve significantly. The risk of relapse has only been reported in sporadic and isolated cases.
