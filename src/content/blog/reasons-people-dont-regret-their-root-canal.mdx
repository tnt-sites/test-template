[Root canals](/root-canal-therapy/) are a notorious dental treatment with a bad reputation. Many people fear them because of the myths that float around them. However, they aren’t as bad as they might seem. In fact, getting one can actually benefit your oral health in many ways. Here are three reasons why patients often don’t regret this procedure.

## Your Toothache Will Go Away

When a tooth is infected, it causes a severe toothache. The inner structures are being attacked by bacteria, but this problem won’t go away on its own. A root canal helps remove the affected tissues and thus relieves any pain that you were feeling.

Not only that, but you can also prevent the issue from worsening. When left untreated, you risk developing complications like a dental abscess or tooth loss in severe cases. You can avoid a bigger headache by getting this procedure sooner rather than later.

## Advanced Dental Technology Makes the Process Easier

A few people might be worried that getting a root canal is a very painful process. The good news is that your dentist will work to make it as comfortable as possible. Using specialized instruments, the process is done effectively and efficiently.

Powerful local anesthesia will numb the area, so it might feel like you are getting a simple filling. Not only that, but you can also ask for ways to ease any anxieties you have. [Dental sedation](/dental-anxiety-and-fear/) may be what you need as it’s designed to help you relax.

## You May Save Your Tooth

In the end, you will be able to preserve more of your natural smile. Since tooth loss is possible from an untreated tooth infection, stopping it in its tracks can help you save the structure itself. You won’t need to worry about dealing with a gap in your grin and restoring it. A root canal is also often more cost-effective compared to replacements.

This procedure isn’t anything you should be scared of. It’s routinely done, and you will be able to reap the benefits it can give you. Remember the three things above, and you can feel confident knowing you won’t regret salvaging your smile.
