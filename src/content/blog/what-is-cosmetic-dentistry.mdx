Cosmetic dentistry has become increasingly popular as more people understand the importance of a beautiful smile to their quality of life. Research shows that the global market for these dental services reached [33.6 billion dollars](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/cosmetic-dentistry-market) in 2022 and is expected to grow at a rate of 13.5% between 2023 and 2030.

## General Dentistry vs Cosmetic Dentistry

General dentistry aims to maintain and restore tooth health by performing cleanings, checkups, cavity fillings, restorative crowns, etc. In contrast, cosmetic procedures focus on the patient’s appearance. A cosmetic dentist may whiten teeth, apply veneers, place dental implants, and insert crowns. While some of these procedures may improve tooth health, the main goal is to improve the patient’s appearance.

The trend toward this dentistry perhaps started with actors and other celebrities who were greatly concerned with their public image. Now anyone who is concerned with their smile may seek treatment, especially those who deal with the public. Cosmetic procedures are widely available, quite safe, and reasonably priced.

## Benefits of Cosmetic Procedures

People with tooth issues such as gaps, discoloration, chips, etc., often are quite self-conscious about their appearance. Sadly, many have endured bullying and other unwanted attention due to their imperfect smile. [Studies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9754742/) have shown that patients with dental issues may suffer from depression and anxiety, conditions that improve after they have cosmetic dental procedures.  
This type of dentistry is not a luxury meant only for the rich and famous. It is meant to help anyone with tooth issues that detract from their appearance.
