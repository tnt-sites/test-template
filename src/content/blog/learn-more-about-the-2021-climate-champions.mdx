A HUGE thank you to the [Dane County Office of Energy and Climate Change](https://daneclimateaction.org/) for producing these 2 videos. The [Overview video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f9Y6sQmmw74) is a nice overview at what our local Climate Champions are doing and the [Artisan Dental video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Wccc7P8etX96xWzMEWoHeDS3arr8imWQ/view) is an expanded insight into what we're doing here at Artisan Dental. We are honored to be included in this amazing group of change makers and congratulate them on their accomplishments.

The Climate Champions program recognizes local entities that are leading on climate action. Last year the Office of Energy and Climate Change recognized thirty organizations as Climate Champions, with 48 awards issued across nine categories. Read more about the 2021 Climate Champions from the [press release](https://www.countyofdane.com/PressDetail/10955) and take a look at the [Climate Champion map](https://arcg.is/5qPzu), which features all of the past and present Climate Champions with details about their accomplishments.

The 2022 Climate Champions [application](https://daneclimateaction.org/Initiatives/2022-Climate-Champions-Program) window is now open. Apply now until August 1. Awards will be announced in September.
