## Who Should Get a Deep Teeth Cleaning?

A deep cleaning is a dental procedure used to treat gum disease and gingivitis. Your gums support your teeth, keeping them in place. However, when your gums become weakened or inflamed, it can impact your teeth.

A typical [professional tooth cleaning](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK554695/) removes plaque from your teeth. However, a deep cleaning cleans the gums as opposed to the teeth.

[Signs that you need a deep cleaning](https://www.cdc.gov/oralhealth/conditions/periodontal-disease.html) include:

- - Swollen gums

- - Bleeding gums

- - Loose teeth

- - Bad breath

Gum disease occurs when plaque turns into tartar and reaches the gums. The bacteria in the tartar causes your gums to inflame. The bacteria can also lead to bad breath. ## Benefits of a Deep Teeth Cleaning Near Me

After you get a deep dental cleaning, your gums will become less inflamed in the next few days after your appointment. You will experience relief from tooth pain. You will also notice that your gums won't bleed as much after you brush your teeth. Finally, your breath will smell better.

A deep cleaning can prevent the bacteria on your gums from reaching your teeth, which would require additional (and more expensive) dental work.

## What Happened During a Deep Cleaning?

Your first appointment with your dentist will involve a dental exam. If you are a good candidate for a deep cleaning, you may be able to have the procedure performed during the same appointment. Alternatively, you can reschedule to get the deep cleaning done.

Your dentist will apply a topical numbing cream on the affected area or give you laughing gas before the deep cleaning. During the deep cleaning, they will use a powered metal tip to scrape away the tartar on your teeth. They will then spray water into the open pores in your gums to rinse away the bacteria. Finally, they will use a manual scraper to remove any remaining plaque and tartar.
