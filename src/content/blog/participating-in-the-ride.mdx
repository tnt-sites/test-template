[**The Ride**](https://theridewi.org/)

The Ride is a Wisconsin biking event that benefits UW cancer research and celebrates life. Starting in Sun Prairie, WI, The Ride travels through beautiful Wisconsin countryside. With five routes to choose from, there’s a ride for everyone from beginners to seasoned cyclists. Artisan Dental team members are participating in this event and would love to have you join our team on September 17th!

In preparation for The Ride, our next Wellness Series Event - [Let's Ride!](/artisan-dental-wellness-series/) is a leisurely bike ride leaving from right in front of Artisan Dental on September 13th. Please join us - All abilities welcome!
