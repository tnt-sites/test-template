Once you have finished your orthodontic treatment and your smile has never looked better, there’s one more step in the treatment to long-lasting results: retention.

!

**Why Are Retainers Essential?**

Orthodontic treatment realigns your teeth by gradually moving them into better positions. But even after the braces come off, your teeth naturally want to shift back to their old ways. This is where retainers step in.

There are many forms of retention, but the most common is Trutain retainers. Trutain retainers are made from clear, durable plastic, are custom-molded or scanned to your smile. They’re comfortable, virtually invisible, and critical to preventing relapse. Without consistent retention, your teeth may start to move—sometimes quickly. Months of progress can be undone in a matter of weeks without proper post-treatment care.

**Benefits of Trutain Retainers!**

-   Clear and Discreet – Unlike traditional retainers with wires, Trutain retainers are barely noticeable.
-   Custom Fit – Designed to fit your unique bite, ensuring optimal comfort and effectiveness.
-   Durable and Long-Lasting – When cared for properly, Trutain can last for 2-3 years.
-   Easy to Wear – Lightweight and simple to use, making daily retention easy to stick to.

**Retainer Care Tips!**

To protect your investment and keep your smile in place:

-   Wear as directed- Most patients wear retainers full-time immediately after treatment, then transition to nighttime use. Follow your orthodontist’s guidelines closely.
-   Clean Daily- Rinse your Trutain retainer with cold to lukewarm water after each use. Brush it gently with a soft toothbrush (no toothpaste!) to avoid scratches or damage.
-   Avoid Heat and Harsh Chemicals- Keep your retainer away from hot water, dishwashers, or direct sunlight, which can warp the plastic. Never soak it in mouthwash or alcohol-based cleaners.
-   Store Safely- Always use your retainer case. Pets love to chew on them, and they’re easy to lose if wrapped in a napkin or left on a countertop.
-   Check for Fit and Damage- If your retainer feels loose, tight, or uncomfortable—or if it cracks—contact your dentist right away. A poor fit means it’s not doing its job.

**A Lifelong Smile Investment**

Think of your Trutain retainer as a small daily habit that protects a major investment—your smile. Retention isn’t optional; it’s the final and ongoing phase of orthodontic care.

Whether you’ve had traditional braces or clear aligners, keeping your teeth aligned requires commitment. With the help of Trutain retainers and proper care, you can ensure that your confident, beautiful smile lasts a lifetime.
