## How Veneers Work

Perfect smiles don't always happen naturally. Most people have one or more tooth flaws they'd love to get rid of. Porcelain veneers can do just that. These custom-crafted porcelain shells can be cemented to the surface of your teeth to hide tooth flaws and improve their appearance.

By placing veneers on your two front teeth, for example, we can close a front tooth gap, enlarge a small tooth, or make a misshapen tooth appear more uniform and even. Once bonded to your teeth, porcelain veneers become a permanent part of your smile. Any imperfections in your teeth will magically disappear, and all people will see are smooth, shiny, flawless teeth. In just two dental visits, porcelain veneers can make your smile appear whiter, more symmetrical, and more aesthetically pleasing.

## Problems Veneers Can Fix

If you have minor imperfections in your smile that you'd like to fix, you could be a candidate for veneers. Veneers can be used to:

- Close gaps between teeth
- Mask stains and discolorations that tooth whitening can't fix
- Improve the shape or size of malformed or irregular teeth
- Camouflage slightly misaligned teeth
- Hide chips and cracks in your teeth
