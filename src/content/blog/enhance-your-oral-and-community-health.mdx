## **Enhance your Oral and Community Health**

June, 2014

*by Dr. Nicole Andersen, D.D.S.*

When we began dreaming about what we wanted to accomplish with Artisan Dental we knew that we wanted to be a good neighbor and an active member of the community both locally and globally. We feel so fortunate to be a part of such a wonderful community.

With that in mind, now through Sept. 3oth, 2014, as a new Artisan Dental patient you will receive $50.00 off any preventive, restorative or cosmetic dental service and Artisan Dental will donate $50.00 to one of our
non profit partners of your choice by simply mentioning that you read this article.
