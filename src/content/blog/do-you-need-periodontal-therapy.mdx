## What is Periodontal Therapy?

[Periodontal therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Periodontal_disease) is a dental procedure that helps to prevent and treat gum disease. The main goal of periodontal therapy is to reduce inflammation, remove plaque buildup around the teeth, and restore gums that have already been damaged by gum disease. Depending on the severity of the case, periodontal therapy may involve deep cleaning, antibiotics, laser treatment, or surgery.

## Signs and Symptoms of Gum Disease

Gum disease can cause a variety of symptoms including redness and swelling around the gums, bleeding when brushing and flossing, receding gums, bad breath that won't go away, and lose of teeth. If you experience any of these symptoms, it is important to see your dentist right away in order to get the proper treatment.

## Do I Need Periodontal Therapy?

If your dentist has diagnosed you with gum disease or if you have any of the signs and symptoms mentioned above, then it's likely that you will need periodontal therapy. The type of treatment you receive will depend on the severity of your condition, but it is important to seek treatment as soon as possible in order to prevent further damage to your teeth and gums.
