## What is iPus laser dentistry?

[Laser dentistry](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/dental-technology.html) in general is a state-of-the-art solution to common dental problems. With lasers, dentists can use light for vaporizing or cutting tissues. This alternative is a great no-drill, no-needle solution for many dental procedures. This treatment also greatly reduces the pain associated with various oral procedures and can reduce bleeding. Faster healing is also seen with laser treatments performed at our practice. _The iPlus laser device is often used in a variety of procedures. These include treatments such as:_

*   Teeth whitening
*   Gum recontouring
*   Crown lengthening
*   Dental fillings
*   Cold sores
*   Oral lesion removal
*   Root canal therapy
*   Treatment of periodontal disease

In addition, our doctors will often use the iPlus laser device for pediatric dentistry as it is less intimidating than traditional dental instruments for young children who may be fearful of the dental chair.

## What are the advantages of laser treatment?

Laser treatment is often seen as a less invasive way to address common dental concerns. These treatments are completed fast and with less pain, ensuring that patients are comfortable throughout their treatment. Reduce swelling and bleeding is often seen with laser dental treatments. In some cases, patients may not even need anesthetics or sedation for procedures treated using laser light.

## Who is a candidate for laser dentistry?

All patients are appropriate candidates for treatment with the iPlus laser dentistry device. We also encourage patients to ask questions about their treatment so they can learn more about the procedures we are performing. Our staff is always excited to educate patients on laser dentistry and the many benefits it provides for patients.
