## How Aging Has Changed

As times have changed, so has the way we live. Baby Boomers are a prime example of a new way of living. Unlike their parents, there is a large portion of the Baby Boomer population who have sloughed off the traditional idea of retirement. At the “ripe old age” of 65, many in this generation are just getting revved up; they continue thriving in their careers, or start new ones! They are running businesses, charities, or performing satisfying volunteer work. And the professional realm isn’t the only area in which older adults are staying active. Statistics indicate that life expectancy has increased over the past few decades. This may be one of the reasons why Baby Boomers are working longer, rather than settling into retirement when they may have expected they would. In addition to staying active in the workplace, we also see that older adults are still dating, still maintaining rich social lives.
