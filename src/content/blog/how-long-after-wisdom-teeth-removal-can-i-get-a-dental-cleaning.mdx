If you have one or more of your wisdom teeth removed, recovering completely could take up to two weeks. You may not be incapacitated and bedridden while you recover, but you should choose the food you eat carefully and follow your dentist’s instructions. Any other dental work will have to wait, but for how long? The amount of time you should wait will be determined by the kind of dental work you have planned and the number of wisdom teeth you had removed. In this article, we will discuss how long you should wait after wisdom teeth removal to get a dental cleaning.

## Recovering From Wisdom Tooth Removal

It could take as long as six weeks before the hole left by a [wisdom tooth removal](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/wisdom-tooth-extraction/about/pac-20395268) heals completely, but your swelling should be resolved and your stitches should have dissolved after about a week. You can speed up your recovery by rinsing with a saline solution two or three times each day. Just don’t rinse on the day of your surgery as this can prolong bleeding.

Your dietary intake should be limited to clear liquids like tea, apple juice or broth on the day of your wisdom tooth surgery, but you will be able to eat soft foods the following day. Stick to soft foods for at least a week. Rinse after each meal, and avoid strenuous exercise for at least a few days.

## Getting a Dental Cleaning

You should be able to brush your teeth gently a day or so after getting wisdom teeth removed, but you should wait at least a week before you visit your dentist for a cleaning. The dental hygienist will not have the room they need to do a thorough job if your mouth and gums are still swollen from wisdom tooth surgery, and the discomfort you will be feeling from having one or more wisdom teeth removed could turn the [dental cleaning](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-oral-health/what-happens-during-a-tooth-cleaning) process into an ordeal.

Waiting a week after wisdom tooth removal will also provide enough time for your stitches to dissolve naturally. If you get your teeth cleaned too early, the tools used by the dental hygienist could disturb your stitches. It may also take a week or so before you are able to open your mouth fully, which is something you will need to do during a professional cleaning. Some people recover from wisdom tooth removal more quickly than others, so you should consult with your dentist if you have specific questions or concerns.
