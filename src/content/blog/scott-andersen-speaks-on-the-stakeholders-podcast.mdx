Ep. 51: Scott Andersen

Host Andy Dosev is joined by our own, Scott Andersen, Director of Stakeholder Stewardship and Co-Owner of Artisan Dental with his wife Dr. Nicole Andersen.

In this episode, they discuss how accountability helps us all to grow and excel, how dental practices can make their impact in our world, what the future of society and business looks like and more!
