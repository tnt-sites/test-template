## Dental bridges explained

At Taylor Street Dental, our team works with patients to help them make an educated decision in regards to how to repair their smile. Our dentists provide a variety of options for patients to consider, including:

*   Dentures
*   Dental bridges
*   Dental implants

Dental bridges are a great option for patients who are seeking a restoration for one or more missing teeth in a row that is both permanent and affordable. Bridges are restorations made from dental crowns on each side fused to pontics, or false teeth, in the middle. They help “bridge the gap” left behind from missing teeth. The dental crowns on each side are bonded onto the adjacent teeth as an anchor, holding the pontics in place to restore the dental arch.

## How to care for dental bridges

It is important for patient to pay close attention to the underside of the dental bridge. This is where food particles can become stuck. Flossing is essential to remove food particles from underneath the dental bridge that can develop into a problem. Periodontal disease and tooth decay can still occur with dental bridges in place, so patients should also visit their dentist regularly for checkups to look for any signs of decay or disease that may develop even on the teeth underneath the crowns of the dental bridge, which are still susceptible.
