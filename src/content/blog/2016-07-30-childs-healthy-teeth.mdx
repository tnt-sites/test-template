If you are a parent, it’s likely that before your baby was born you were thinking about interviewing pediatricians. It’s true that most soon-to-be-parents understand that having a trusted doctor in place is an important part of planning for baby. It’s also true that a trusted dentist is vital to a child’s health care from his or her earliest days. Some parents believe that their child won’t need a dentist until he or she has a mouth full of permanent teeth. Not true. Dental care should actually start with their precious, toothless gums. And lifelong dental habits that protect teeth begin in a child’s earliest years. A healthy mouth and teeth are just as crucial to a child’s health as good nutrition and doctor visits. The American Academy of Pediatrics agrees, reporting that tooth decay is the most common chronic diseases found in children. Additionally, more than 40% of children in the U.S. have tooth decay by the time they’re in kindergarten. At Taylor Street Dental, we know that encouraging dental health for children goes hand-in-hand with their general health and wellbeing. And as our patients know, early dental visits teach a child that oral health is important, visiting the dentist can be a fun experience, and that tooth decay is a mostly preventable disease.
