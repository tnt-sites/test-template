## What to Do About Dark Lines Around My Crowns And Veneers

It's common to worry about dark circles and fine lines around your eyes. But it's unusual to see dark lines or blotches forming around your newly restored teeth. This may be a sign of tooth decay, gum disease or an even worse condition. It's important that you investigate the problem as quickly as possible and find a [dental professional](/) to help.

## Staining Around the Teeth

You're likely to see stains form around a crown or veneer that is not attached onto a tooth properly. Not brushing it properly may cause food particles to build up along the tooth's edges. This problem could damage the tooth by allowing the buildup of bacteria and plaque.

## The Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal (PFM) Method

This [blackline](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/qa/what-are-the-disadvantages-of-dental-veneers) could be a portion of metal that is peeking through. This occurs when a porcelain crown contains a metal base. In the past, metal was used to strengthen the attachment. However, the porcelain wears away as the gum line recedes, so the lower parts of the crown reveal the metal base.
