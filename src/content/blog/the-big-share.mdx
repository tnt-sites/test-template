We are happy to share that one of our favorite philanthropic partners, Community Shares and the Madison Community Foundation are introducing what they call the "Big Share" on March 3, 2015!

The Big Share is an online day of giving for 70 local nonprofits dedicated to building a fair, just community and protecting our environment.

The Big Share is a fun, easy, and flexible way to donate to and to learn more about organizations that help our community. The Big Share will introduce you to new causes and nonprofits and allow you to share the stories of the work people are doing to make the world a better place.

You can learn more at [www.thebigshare.org](http://thebigshare.org/)
