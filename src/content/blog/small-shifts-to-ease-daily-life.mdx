Join us for a virtual workshop to explore how you can make small shifts to find more ease in your daily life. We will move through a process of assessing our state of well-being, envisioning our future selves, and backmapping the steps we need to take to get there. This is not just an informational talk, you will leave with a plan to move yourself closer to the future you want.

Nichole North Hester is a well-being integration consultant and coach, former k12 educator, and yoga and mindfulness instructor. She believes in the power of small shifts to create real results. As the founder of [True North](http://www.beartruenorth.com), she works with individuals, families, teams, and workplaces to develop habits and create systems that support their well-being.
