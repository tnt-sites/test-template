[Artisan Dental Recycling Program](/recycling-program/) \- Double Points in October

We love Madison where so many people in our community, both patients and non-patients, put forth the extra effort to bring items in to be recycled and keep them out of the landfills. We have sent in nearly 4000 items to [Terracycle](https://www.terracycle.com/en-US/)! So thank you to all of you.

Terracycle is offering double points on items donated in October, so it’s a great time to drop off any items that you, your friends, neighbors, coworkers have. Double points translates into more dollars being donated to charities including Feeding America.

Donations are accepted during business hours, M-F 7:00am – 5:00pm.
