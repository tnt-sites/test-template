**Thanks For Your Participation -**

We are excited to share that we've had a very positive response to our Artisan Dental Recycling Program.  To date patients and members of the general public have recycled over 1,000 oral health care products.  Please invite family and friends to participate in the [Artisan Dental Recycling Program](/recycling-program/) by dropping off used toothbrushes, toothpaste tubes, mouthwash and floss containers during business hours.
