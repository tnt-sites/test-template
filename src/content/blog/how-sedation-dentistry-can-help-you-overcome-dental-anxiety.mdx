## Introduction to Sedation Dentistry on Dental Anxiety

Sedation dentistry uses medication to help you relax during dental procedures. There are different types of sedatives available, and your dentist will choose the best type for you based on your level of anxiety and the procedure being performed.

With sedation dentistry, you may remain awake or asleep during the procedure. The level of sedation will be determined by your dentist based on what is best for you and the procedure being performed.

If you have dental anxiety, sedation dentistry can help you get the dental care you need in a safe and comfortable environment. Talk to your dentist about whether sedation dentistry is right for you.

## Different Types of Sedation and How to Choose the Right Option

If you have dental anxiety, sedation dentistry can help you overcome your fear of the dentist and get the treatment you need. There are different types of sedation available, and your dentist can help you choose the right option for you.

**Oral sedation:** This type of sedation is taken in pill form an hour before your appointment. You will be awake during your appointment, but you will be relaxed and may not remember much of what happened.

**IV sedation:** This type of sedation is administered through a vein in your arm and takes effect quickly. You will be asleep during your appointment and will not remember what happened.

**General anesthesia:** This type of sedation is used for more invasive procedures, such as surgery. You will be asleep during the procedure and will not remember what happened.

Sedation dentistry can help you overcome your dental anxiety and get the treatment you need. Your dentist can help you choose the right type of sedation for you based on your level of anxiety and the procedure you need to have done.

## 5 Amazing Tips for Minimizing Dental Anxiety

Dental anxiety is a common problem that can make it difficult or even impossible to receive dental care. Sedation dentistry can help you overcome your anxiety and get the dental care you need. Here are the 5 tips:

1. Talk to your dentist about your anxiety. Dentists are trained to deal with anxious patients, and they can help you overcome your fears.

1. Try relaxation techniques such as deep breathing or visualization. These techniques can help you stay calm during your appointment.

1. Listen to calming music or bring a favorite stuffed animal to comfort you during the procedure.

1. Ask for numbing gel or topical anesthesia to lessen the sensation of the treatment.

1. Don't watch the procedure being done if it makes you anxious. Ask the dentist or dental assistant to cover your eyes with a drape or shield during the treatment.
