## **How do dental implants work?**

[Dental implants](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/emergency-dentistry.html) are titanium posts used by a dentist to provide a foundation for a crown, bridge, or [denture](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/dental-implants.html). They are inserted into the bone of the jaw, which makes them a strong and durable solution for those seeking a reliable and sturdy alternative to bridges and dentures. When the dental implant is inserted into the jaw, it stimulates bone growth through a process referred to as osseointegration and bonds to the bone as a natural tooth root does. This is what sets this restoration apart from the alternatives.

## **How long can I enjoy my dental implants?**

It is essential that you consider your options when selecting a tooth replacement solution for your smile. For many who are looking for the best in function and appearance, the dental implant is best. Because it bonds with the natural bone, it can last a lifetime with proper care. This makes it a popular choice with many of our patients who do not want the costs associated with replacing dentures and bridges over time.
