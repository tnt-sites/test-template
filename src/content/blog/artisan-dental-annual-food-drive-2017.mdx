We appreciate that we are an interwoven part of a larger local and global community. We are heartened by the opportunity to support organizations with owner and staff volunteer hours, free dental services, and/or ongoing financial contributions. /communitygiving/

In this spirit, we are happy to announce the Artisan Dental Annual Food Drive for 2017.

Artisan Dental patients and employees together with [Festival Food](https://www.festfoods.com/stores/madison) employees are invited to make a non-perishable food donation during business hours, M-F 7:00am – 5:00pm for the month of November. Food donated will be matched by Artisan Dental. We will have donation barrels located at Artisan Dental and Festival Foods.

All non-perishable food types are accepted. [Community Action Coalition](http://www.cacscw.org/index.php) suggests the following food types: canned tuna, canned chicken, peanut butter, canned fruit, vegetable oil and canned whole kernel corn.

Thank you for your generosity!
