## What is sleep apnea?

It’s a sleep-related breathing disorder that occurs when airflow is obstructed and the soft tissues at the back of your throat collapse (obstructive sleep apnea). Although rare, some patients have central sleep apnea, where the brain fails to send the right signals to the breathing muscles.

Whatever the type of sleep apnea, patients wake up abruptly gasping for air. The cessation of breathing happens for about 10 seconds, but these episodes may recur up to 100 times in a night! This fragmented sleep often leads to numerous symptoms like:

- Daytime fatigue
- Morning headaches
- Poor concentration during the day
- Irritability and mood changes
- Poor memory
- Depression

Sleep apnea is often characterized by chronic snoring as the air moves past the throat. Most sleep apnea patients aren’t even aware of the problem. Usually, your sleeping partner notices your chronic snoring and cessation of breathing, something that may not be a luxury for people who sleep alone.
