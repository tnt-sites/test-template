## Artisan Dental Wellness Series

June, 2014

*by Dr. Nicole Andersen, D.D.S.*

The Artisan Dental Wellness Series is a quarterly wellness and community building series, designed to foster whole person wellness, enjoyment and a sense of connection among all Artisan Dental stakeholders (patients, staff, suppliers and community)

Each quarter, Artisan Dental will sponsor a Wellness Series event, featuring an expert or experience designed to support greater levels of health and happiness.  All Artisan Dental patients, together with a friend are welcome to participate, free of charge, on first come, first reserved basis for each event.

You can learn about upcoming events by visiting this page of our website. Notices will be placed on our Facebook, Google + & Twitter pages. Artisan Dental patients will also receive an email reminder.

To reserve a place at an upcoming event simply email or call our office at:

info@artisandentalmadison.com or Ph. 608-467-8022

## Upcoming Events:

Cross Fit – August 26th – 6:00 p.m. – 7:00 p.m. James Madison Park (Near Basketball Courts in the Southwestern portion of the park)

CrossFit is an evidence-based fitness methodology that prescribes constantly varied, high intensity, functional movements. Our introduction to CrossFit will include a short overview if its history, philosophy and benefits. This introduction will be followed by a warm-up, moderate intensity workout and cool down instructed by Patrick Nottingham, owner of CrossFit Atavus and Artisan Dental patient. To learn more about CrossFit Atavus and Patrick by visiting https://www.crossfitatavus.com/.

We look forward to enjoying a health enhancing workout together by beautiful Lake Mendota.
