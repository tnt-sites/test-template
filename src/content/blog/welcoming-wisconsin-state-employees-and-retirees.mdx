We would like to extend a warm welcome to all Wisconsin State employees and retirees that are considering new dental care providers as part of the, "Its Your Choice Open Enrollment, 2016 " Oct. 5th -Oct. 30th.

As a Delta Dental PPO & Premier provider near the Capital Square, we would like to invite you to consider joining our growing patient family in 2016 when the dental insurance provided by the State of Wisconsin becomes administered by Delta Dental.  The **[“Why Artisan Dental”](/why-choose-artisan-dental/)** page of our website provides a description of the many ways we seek to enhance your oral and total health.

At Artisan Dental our commitment to providing exceptional quality dental care is central to our entire team’s efforts.  Dr. Andersen has been practicing general dentistry for over 17 years.  She has been practicing cosmetic dentistry, together with partial & full mouth rehabilitation for over 8 years.

Our approach is to first understand your needs and wishes and then customize a treatment plan that is comfortable and connected to your needs.

Our intention is that your care be convenient, with appointments starting as early as 7:00 a.m. including lunch hour appointments, thru 5:00 p.m.  We are centrally located eight blocks off the Capital at the corner of East Washington and North Livingston St.
