## Avoid Staining Foods and Drinks

Some foods and beverages are notorious for staining your teeth. Coffee, red wine, and berries are common culprits that’ll dull your newly whitened teeth. To minimize staining, try using a straw when you drink dark beverages and rinse your mouth with water afterward. You don’t have to give up your favorite treats entirely – just enjoy them in moderation and be mindful of their effects.

## Keep Your Teeth Clean

Good oral hygiene is essential for keeping your teeth white. Brush your teeth at least twice a day with a whitening toothpaste to remove plaque and surface stains. Don’t forget to floss daily to clean those hard-to-reach areas between your teeth. You’ll also want to schedule regular dental cleanings to prevent stains from setting in and keep your teeth healthy.

## Quit Smoking

If you’re a smoker, now is a good time to quit. Smoking is a major cause of tooth discoloration (and a host of other dental issues) and can quickly undo the results of your whitening treatment. By quitting, you’ll protect your smile and improve your overall health.

## Use Whitening Touch-Up Products

You can use at-home whitening products or touch-up kits to maintain your smile between professional treatments. Consult with your dentist first to make sure you aren’t overusing these treatments, which can make your teeth sensitive.
