When most people think about [dental fillings](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/restorative-dentistry.html#fill), the first thing that comes to mind is their appearance. However, these days, fillings aren’t made of the silvery, metal materials of the past and are now made using tooth-colored composite resin. But in addition to looking natural, they also come with many other practical advantages that you can enjoy. If you’re thinking about upgrading and replacing your old metal filling with a new tooth-colored one, here are some reasons to!

## They Bond Directly to Your Teeth

Unlike metal amalgam fillings, which simply sit on top of a cavity, composite resin is designed to chemically bond with your natural enamel. This creates a tighter, more secure seal that helps keep bacteria and food particles out, reducing the risk of further decay developing underneath or around the filling.

## They’re Mercury- and Metal-Free

Traditional silver fillings contain a mixture of metals, often including mercury. While these amalgam fillings are still considered safe, many patients still prefer to avoid metal restorations altogether. Composite resin is a biocompatible, metal-free material, making it a popular choice for patients who want a more natural approach to their dental care and no metals in their mouth.

## They Support Long-Term Oral Health

Because tooth-colored fillings preserve more natural tooth structure and create a strong shield against bacteria, they help set your smile up for long-term success. In fact, patients who choose composite fillings often find they need less extensive restorative work down the road, like crowns or root canals, since the tooth itself stays healthier for a longer time!

## They Blend Seamlessly with Your Smile

Because composite resin can be shaded to match the exact unique color of your surrounding teeth, tooth-colored fillings are virtually invisible once placed. This makes them an especially popular option for fillings in the front of the mouth, where a silver filling would otherwise stand out every time you laugh, smile, or begin speaking.

The bottom line is, tooth-colored fillings offer a combination of strength, durability, and a natural appearance that makes them an excellent choice for treating cavities!
