With a little help from the friendly dentists at Taylor Street Dental, porcelain veneers can provide a new sense of confidence every time you smile. Dental veneers, also known as porcelain veneers, are custom-made ceramic shells designed to fit seamlessly over damaged teeth, improving their appearance and improving their durability.

## **The Advantages of Porcelain Veneers**

Porcelain veneers offer several advantages over alternative treatments like crowns. These dental veneers can be applied within three visits and — with proper care and maintenance — can last indefinitely!

### Conceal Cosmetic Flaws

Porcelain veneers offer patients of Taylor Street Dental an excellent solution for masking several different kinds of dental imperfections. If your teeth are misshapen, misaligned, cracked, chipped, or stained, veneers can provide a natural-looking cover and give you a brighter, more confident smile. It also eliminates the need for more intensive procedures, saving you time and effort.

### Versatility

A significant benefit of porcelain veneers is their versatility. They’re used to address a variety of dental issues, including deeply stained teeth that are unresponsive to teeth whitening treatments. Veneers can also reshape misshapen or chipped teeth.

### Aesthetically Pleasing

The porcelain veneers at Taylor Street Dental are crafted in a specialized laboratory to ensure a precise fit and a natural appearance. The ceramic material used closely resembles the translucence of natural tooth enamel, resulting in a seamless blend with existing teeth. This makes the veneers nearly indistinguishable from natural teeth, allowing patients to look forward to a uniform smile. Dental porcelain is also highly resistant to stains, unlike natural tooth enamel. This means that your porcelain veneers will maintain their bright and vibrant appearance for an extended period.
