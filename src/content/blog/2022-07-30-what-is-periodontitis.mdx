## What is periodontitis?

Periodontitis is a chronic inflammatory disease that affects the tissues around your teeth. If left untreated, it can lead to tooth loss and the loss of gum tissue and jawbone.

## What causes periodontitis?

The leading cause of periodontitis is plaque, a sticky film of bacteria that forms on your teeth.

## What puts me at risk of periodontitis?

Some factors that may increase your risk of periodontitis include smoking, diabetes, and certain medications. Individuals experiencing hormonal changes, such as pregnant women, may also find themselves at a higher risk of developing periodontal disease.

## What are the symptoms of periodontitis?

Periodontitis often doesn't have any symptoms in its early stages. As the disease progresses, you may experience redness and swelling in your gums, bleeding when you brush or floss your teeth, and receding gums. You may also notice that your teeth appear longer than they used to because the gum tissue around them has started to shrink. If you have any of these symptoms, see the team at Taylor Street Dental right away.

## What is the best treatment for periodontitis?

Periodontitis can be treated with a deep cleaning, antibiotics, or surgery. With early diagnosis and treatment, you can help prevent tooth loss and other complications from occurring.
