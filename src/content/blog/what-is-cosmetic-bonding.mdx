## What is Cosmetic Bonding?

Teeth bonding is an effective method of correcting teeth problems such as gaps between teeth, chipped teeth, cracked teeth, discoloration, and decay while giving you a natural-looking smile.

The procedure entails placing a tooth-colored resin on top of the affected areas. Later, the area gets covered with a light hardening material to help cover the imperfection. The procedure is also painless, so the patient will not require anesthesia.

## Risks of Cosmetic Dental Bonding

1. **Allergic reaction** – In rare cases, some patients may react to the composite resin or tools used during the procedure.
2. **Infections –** Before beginning treatment, the dentist must ensure that all infections, debris, tooth decay, or anything else that can affect the tooth get removed. Any infection or debris left under the resin material will impact the natural tooth and may necessitate tooth extraction. To avoid infection problems, you should also get bonding services from an expert, such as Austin cosmetic bonding services.
3. **The bonding material can discolor** – The resin used in the bonding process is not stain-resistant. Initially, the resin can resist stains depending on how long the bonding is, but it becomes difficult to resist stains as it wears out. Furthermore, the bonding does not respond to teeth whitening procedures.
4. **The bonding material is not durable** – Compared to your natural tooth, the bonding material is not very durable. The resin used in the bonding process will eventually wear out or chip or crack.
