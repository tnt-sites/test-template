Enjoying a cup of coffee is one of life’s simple pleasures, but it can be a bit tricky when you’re wearing Invisalign. Coffee is notorious for staining teeth and can create an unpleasant odor if not properly cleaned after consumption. Fortunately, there are ways to enjoy your favorite coffee beverages without compromising the results of your Invisalign treatment. Here are four ways to enjoy coffee while wearing Invisalign:

### 1. Opt for Iced or Cold Brewed Coffee

Cold brew and iced coffees are excellent choices for individuals with Invisalign who want to preserve the condition of their teeth. Cold brew is brewed over time rather than with heat, resulting in a smoother, less acidic flavor. Iced coffee is made by brewing hot coffee and then cooling it down before pouring it over ice. Both options contain fewer staining components than hot coffee, allowing you to enjoy your favorite beverage without worrying about staining your Invisalign.

### 2. Drink Through a Straw

Using a straw for consuming drinks like coffee can aid in safeguarding your Invisalign from getting stained. By sipping through a straw, the liquid is steered away from your teeth and Invisalign aligners, lessening the chances of discoloration or harm. Opt for a reusable metal or glass straw over disposable plastic ones for a more eco-friendly choice.

### 3. Rinse Your Invisalign Aligners After Drinking Coffee

After consuming coffee, it is beneficial to rinse your Invisalign aligners to eliminate any leftover residue and staining agents. Just rinse them with lukewarm water briefly, followed by brushing with a [soft-bristled toothbrush](https://oralb.com/en-us/products/electric-toothbrushes/genius-series/) to remove any remaining residue. Make sure they are dry before reinserting them into your mouth.

### 4. Enjoy Dark Roast Coffee

Dark-roasted coffee beans, roasted for longer periods, contain fewer staining agents than lighter roasts, making them a great option for Invisalign wearers. Dark roast coffee typically has a richer, more intense flavor than its lighter counterparts, making it a perfect beverage to enjoy while wearing Invisalign.

## Additional Tips for Enjoying Coffee with Invisalign

Drinking coffee while wearing Invisalign doesn’t have to be a hassle. By following these tips and tricks, you can enjoy your favorite cup of coffee without compromising the condition of your Invisalign aligners. From iced coffee to dark roast varieties, there are plenty of ways to enjoy your coffee while ensuring your [Invisalign treatment](/dental-services/cosmetic-dentistry/invisalign/) remains effective.

### **Q: Can Invisalign be damaged by coffee?**

**A:** Yes, Invisalign can be damaged by coffee. Coffee is known to stain teeth and Invisalign aligners, so it’s essential to take proper measures to protect them from staining.

### **Q: What is the best type of coffee for Invisalign wearers?**

**A:** The best type of coffee for Invisalign wearers is cold brew or iced coffee, as they contain fewer staining agents than regular hot coffee. Additionally, dark roast varieties are also a good option, as they have fewer staining agents than lighter roasts.

### **Q: How do I clean Invisalign aligners after drinking coffee?**

**A:** To clean Invisalign aligners after drinking coffee, rinse them with lukewarm water for a few seconds, then brush them with a soft-bristled toothbrush to remove any excess residue. Make sure to dry them off before putting them back in your mouth.
