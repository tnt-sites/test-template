## Does Teeth Whitening Hurt?

Mild sensitivity or gum irritation is normal after teeth whitening services, but it usually resolves itself within a few days. For some clients, the sensitivity lasts longer. [Ten percent of patients report having moderate sensitivity, while four percent experience severe sensitivity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12198987/#:~:text=Approximately%2010%20percent%20of%20patients,sensitivity%20during%20home%20whitening%20treatment.) for up to two weeks. This discomfort is typically due to the peroxide in teeth whiteners. If you experience sensitivity, our dentists can help. They can limit the frequency of treatments, decrease the amount of peroxide used, or lower the concentration of the whitener.
