## Gum Disease Is Bad for Your Teeth

The health of your mouth has an inextricable link with the state of your gum tissue. Gum disease can lead to more than just a bad taste in your mouth. As the infection and inflammation continue, it starts to wear away at tooth enamel and bone around teeth, eventually resulting in cavities and an increased risk of losing teeth. Our [Austin Cosmetic Dentistry](/cosmetic-dentistry/) experts recommend regular flossing along with cleanings for a healthy mouth.

### Prevent Cavities With Regular Flossing

Most adults neglect dental hygiene because they don't realize how severe problems like [cavities](/cavities-and-fillings/) can get. When plaque builds up on teeth, bacteria multiply and release acids that eat away at the tooth enamel and cause decay. Without diligent dental care, this problem can develop into gum disease. If you notice any signs of gum disease, contact our Austin Cosmetic Dentistry offices right away.

That way, your [periodontal health](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4580771/) will always be under the care and protection of an experienced provider who will do their best to ensure your teeth remain healthy for years to come.

#### The Importance of Early Detection

Gum disease is a serious problem that calls for early diagnosis. Unfortunately, once the signs of gum disease appear in a patient's mouth, it can be too late. Some risks of delayed diagnosis of gum disease include dementia and heart disease.

If you notice any signs of illness in your mouth, such as bleeding from the gums or a bad taste in your mouth, it is essential to consult with Austin Preventative Dentistry. A [dental expert](/restorative-dentistry/) will examine your teeth and gums, keeping a close eye for any issues that may need treatment. Your toothbrush can help protect against gum disease, but regular visits are highly recommended for people at high risk.
