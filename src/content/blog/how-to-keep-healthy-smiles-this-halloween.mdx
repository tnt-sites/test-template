With Halloween just around the corner, you know that sweets will abound and your kids are going to enjoy as much of it as they can. This much candy and other treats are going to affect the health of their teeth and their smile. There are some steps you can take, as well as getting some help from [Austin cosmetic dentistry](/), to help keep those smiles looking good.

Eating too much candy and soda over [Halloween](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/tx--austin/halloween-parties/) and other holidays can cause dental problems such as toothaches, sensitive teeth, and cavities. The [World Dental Federation](https://twitter.com/fdiworlddental?ref_src=twsrc%5Egoogle%7Ctwcamp%5Eserp%7Ctwgr%5Eauthor) states that between 60 to 90 percent of school-age children have tooth decay, and almost 100 percent of adults do as well. Tooth decay, in various stages, can lead to significant pain. In children, it can hinder their learning in school and may result in poor grades.

## **Dental Veneers**

Cosmetic dentistry downtown has many ways to give you amazing smiles. One of them is [dental veneers](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-veneers#types). These thin porcelain covers for your teeth can change imperfect teeth into beautiful teeth very quickly. They can be used to hide many dental flaws including teeth that are crooked, chipped, cracked, discolored, or short, and they can also be used to close gaps between teeth.

## **Cosmetic Bonding**

When teeth are damaged, they can be repaired with cosmetic bonding at our cosmetic dentist downtown office. This material is white and it can be used to repair a chipped tooth or hide a cracked one. The material is put on the tooth in layers and then hardened and polished. It will look like a natural tooth when finished. This procedure is one of the least costly when it comes to repairing teeth.
