If you are looking for ways to replace your missing teeth, then [dental implants](https://www.hayscosmeticdentist.com/dental-implants.html) might have stood out as the gold standard. They are an effective solution that has helped millions of patients, but where should you go to get this service? Finding the right dentist can be tough with so many offices out there. Here are three qualities to keep an eye out for when making a confident choice.

## Special Training and Experience

Dental implants are recommended by patients and professionals alike, but some people aren’t eligible for them right away. You should look for a practice that will take the time to understand your oral and overall health; that way, you lower the risk of complications. A great way to ensure this is by looking at any special training and experience.

Finding dentists who have the knowledge and expertise about this treatment can help you feel confident knowing they are well-equipped to get you your full smile. During a consultation, you can ask about this to learn more about their specific credentials.

## Reviews and Smile Galleries

Before you head into your initial visit to a certain dental office, check online for reviews left by the community. First-hand testimonials can give you an idea of how they treat their patients and paint a picture of what you can expect. However, be sure you don’t focus on only the good or bad ones. Find a balance of those who are thorough.

Not only that, look on their website to see if they have a smile gallery. Before-and-after photos can hint at what your results might be like. That way, you can see the work they’ve done yourself.

## Advanced Dental Technology

As dentistry continues to evolve, you should find a practice that keeps up. Advanced technology helps treatments be more precise and efficient, so you can get the results you dream of. Dental implants only benefit from this, as they can be more accurately placed to give you the full smile you dream of.

You can ask about specific things that are used during this tooth replacement to get a better understanding of what the dentist uses. For instance, 3D cone beam CT imaging can take about 200 photos of your mouth at different angles to build a digital model of it.

This will help your dentist see things like bone density, nerve pathways, blood vessels, and more. All this information can give your provider a better understanding of your smile, so you get the restored grin you dream of.

In the end, finding the right dentist to get dental implants from doesn’t have to be a hassle. By keeping the three things above in mind while looking at a specific office, you can feel confident making a choice and get back your full smile.
