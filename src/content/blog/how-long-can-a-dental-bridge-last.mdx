## Caring for Your Dental Bridge

Taking proper care of your [dental bridge](https://aaid-implant.org/faqs/what-are-dental-implants/) is important to keep your smile bright for many years. A cosmetic dentist wants to help ensure your investment lasts, but caring for a bridge takes more care than natural teeth.

It is advisable to remove your bridge carefully when eating. A soft brush or mild cleaner gently sweeps away particles that brushing alone misses. However, never use abrasive tools - they could scratch the delicate material over time. Be gentle while scrubbing. Rinsing with cool water, not hot, after teeth cleaning prevents warping.

Thoroughly dry your bridge before storage to discourage bacteria growth. Also important - avoid prolonged soaking, unless your dentist advises otherwise. The retaining clips require as much protection from rust as your teeth. Regular professional cleanings are key to a bridge's wellness, just like brushing twice daily and flossing once between each meal.
