## What is Digital Imaging?

Digital imaging is a technology that allows you to take pictures of your patient's teeth and then review them on a computer screen.

This technology has made it possible for dentists to get more accurate results. The reason why digital imaging is so popular with dentists is that it helps them diagnose problems before they become serious issues.

Our [Austin dental digital imaging dentists](/dental-digital-imaging/) inform patients about their oral health, including:

• The state of their gums  
• The quality of their enamel  
• Whether or not their teeth are decaying or growing at different rates  
• The damage has been done by previous dental work.
