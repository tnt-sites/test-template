## Understanding Locked Jaw

The temporomandibular joint moves your jaw. In situations when it deviates from the right position, an individual will most likely suffer from speech difficulties as well eat food and even perform other activities throughout their regular schedule. There are several factors which can lead to this condition such as bad bite, grinding of teeth called bruxism, stress arthritis or injury. It can also be associated with a serious underlying medical condition requiring urgent intervention.

Age does not play a role in the probability of developing TMJ with women being more likely to have it as compared to men National Institute on Dental and Craniofacial Research. Approximately [65% to 85%](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5342624/)of adults will develop varying forms TMJ discomfort once in their lives. All these make it necessary for the client to seek professional help prevent any complications that may arise.

## Effects of a Locked Jaw

This condition, however, can yield several impacts that would otherwise be detrimental to the physique and mental composure as well. One of the more visible impacts is limitation to jaw movement which hurts and basically hampers mundane tasks such as talking or eating. This, in turn, may result in lowered quality of life when people are forced to avoid some food or activities simply because they feel uncomfortable doing it.

In addition, a rigid jaw also has important implications in regards to emotions. The presence of continuous pain and discomfort often results in elevated levels of stress, anxiety and irritability. This can interfere with everyday life, functioning and relationship work.

In addition, the pain and physical discomfort of a jaw may cause difficulty sleeping resulting to tiredness in feeling throughout the day. This can also increase emotional despair resulting from the disease.
