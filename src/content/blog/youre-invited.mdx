!Artisan Dental is sponsoring a free VIRTUAL webinar – “The Carbon Neutral Dental Office” – from 6:30 to 7:30 p.m. on Thursday, Nov. 18. Speakers include Scott Andersen, co-owner and director of stakeholder stewardship at Artisan Dental; Olivia Fussell, founder and managing director of [Carbon Credit Capital](https://carboncreditcapital.com/) and Robert Banner, senior project manager for [GreenTrees](https://www.green-trees.com/).  “We see carbon offsetting as an important element of a comprehensive plan to reduce our negative impact and enhance our positive impact, realizing our mission to serve all of the stakeholders connected to our business,” said [Scott Andersen](/scott-andersen/), Artisan Dental’s Director of Stakeholder Stewardship and co-owner of the practice with his wife, [Dr. Nicole Andersen](/dr-nicole-andersen/). “We feel we have both a responsibility and an opportunity to be good stewards of the environment we all enjoy and depend on.” Register by calling (608) 467-8022 or emailing [info@artisandentalmadison.com](mailto:info@artisandentalmadison.com).
