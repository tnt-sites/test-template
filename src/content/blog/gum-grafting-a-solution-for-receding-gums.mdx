## What is gum grafting?

Gum grafting is a surgical procedure that involves taking tissue from one part of the mouth and attaching it to the area where the gums have receded. The tissue can be taken from the roof of the mouth, or it can be taken from a donor source. The graft is then attached to the exposed tooth roots, which helps to cover them and protect them from further damage.

### Why is gum grafting necessary?

When gums recede, it can cause a number of problems. First and foremost, it can expose the tooth roots, which can lead to decay and other dental problems. It can also make the teeth more sensitive to hot and cold temperatures, which can make eating and drinking uncomfortable. Gum recession can also make the teeth appear longer than they should be, which can affect a person's smile and overall appearance.

### What are the benefits of gum grafting?

Gum grafting can help to restore the natural gum line and protect the teeth from further damage. It can also help to reduce tooth sensitivity and improve a person's smile. In addition, gum grafting can help to prevent the development of more serious dental problems down the line. Other benefits include:

### 1. Restoring the Natural Gum Line

When gums recede, it can lead to an uneven gum line, which can affect a person's smile and overall appearance. [Gum grafting](/vista-ca/gum-grafting/) can help to restore the natural gum line, creating a more even and aesthetically pleasing appearance.

### 2. Protecting Teeth

Exposed tooth roots can be more susceptible to decay and other dental problems, which can lead to more serious issues down the line. By covering the tooth roots with a graft, gum grafting helps to protect the teeth and prevent further damage.

### 3. Reducing Tooth Sensitivity

When tooth roots are exposed due to gum recession, it can make the teeth more sensitive to hot and cold temperatures. This can make eating and drinking uncomfortable, and can even affect a person's ability to speak. Gum grafting helps to cover the exposed tooth roots, reducing sensitivity and making eating and drinking more comfortable.

### What should I expect during the gum grafting procedure?

Before the procedure, your dentist will numb the area with a local anesthetic. If tissue is being taken from the roof of the mouth, a small incision will be made in the tissue and a small piece of tissue will be removed. If donor tissue is being used, it will be prepared and sterilized before being attached to the gum line.

To attach the graft to your gums, the dentist will make an incision at the grafting location to create a small flap. They will then place the new tissue onto the area and stitch it into place.

After the procedure, you may experience some discomfort and swelling for a few days. Your dentist will provide you with instructions on how to care for the area while it heals. In most cases, patients can return to their normal activities within a week or two.
