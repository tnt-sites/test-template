While resources exist for children through pediatric dentists or children’s hospitals, once they age out of pediatric care, it can feel next to impossible to find a dentist that can provide the care your loved one needs--especially sedation dentistry.

## Tailored Care for Unique Needs

What sets special needs dentistry apart is its personalized approach. Dentists specializing in this field are equipped with specialized training, empathy, and a profound understanding of various conditions, ensuring a comfortable and accommodating environment for patients with diverse needs.

One of the primary goals of special needs dentistry is to make dental visits stress-free and comfortable. The dental team focuses on creating a calming atmosphere and employs techniques such as gentle communication, desensitization strategies, and the use of sedation when necessary. This approach aims to build trust and confidence, ensuring a positive experience for every patient. Using our specialized certifications in sedation dentistry and our devoted sedation suite, your loved one can continue to receive the high quality care that they deserve.

## Collaborative Approach to Care

Collaboration is key in special needs dentistry. Our dental professionals work closely with caregivers, families, and other healthcare providers to create comprehensive treatment plans. Understanding the patient's medical history, specific needs, and preferences allows us to tailor treatments, schedule appointments at optimal times, and ensure necessary accommodations are in place.
