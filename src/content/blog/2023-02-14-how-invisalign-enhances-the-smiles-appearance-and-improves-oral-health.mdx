## The Benefits of Invisalign® Orthodontics

Not only do Invisalign® aligners look great, but they can also help to enhance the overall appearance of your smile. With just a few weeks of regular wear, many patients notice improvements in their oral health and confidence as well as their new smile begins to take shape. There are several benefits to Invisalign® that makes them highly desirable for our patients:

*   **Easy to use**. Invisalign® aligners are removable, which makes them very easy to clean and care for. As a result, they don't require the same level of upkeep that traditional braces do.
*   **Discreet and comfortable**. Most people won't even know you are undergoing orthodontic treatment when using Invisalign®. These clear plastic aligners are virtually invisible when worn over your teeth to straighten your smile! They're also smooth and flexible, making them much more comfortable than traditional metal braces.
*   **Effective at realigning teeth**. With Invisalign®, our team at Taylor Street Dental can realign your teeth over time to create the perfect smile you desire. We use this customized set of clear plastic aligners which gently apply pressure to the teeth and move them into the desired position.
