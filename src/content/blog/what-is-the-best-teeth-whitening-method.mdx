## What Is Teeth Whitening?

Whitening of teeth refers to lightening tooth color by eliminating discoloration and stains. Bleaching is a common method, where dental professionals use carbamide peroxide and hydrogen peroxide to destroy the molecules that lead to staining.

Other whitening methods are such as:  
Laser whitening- This process uses an intense pulsed light to remove stains  
Physical abrasion- This process involves washing the teeth thoroughly with a gritty material.

## Types Of Teeth Whitening

### Whitening your teeth is an excellent way to boost your self-esteem and confidence. The various types of whitening treatments are:
 - Professional in-office whitening treatments - DIY/ at-home kits - Over-the-counter products
 At-Home/DIY Kits

Some people prefer natural, DIY whitening products, like baking soda, activated charcoal, and coconut oil pulling. The problem with such treatments is the possibility of hurting your teeth and gums.

### Over-the-counter Products

You can find different whitening solutions like mouthwashes, strips, and gels at your local drugstore. Although they are easily accessible, they can hurt your gums if used incorrectly.
