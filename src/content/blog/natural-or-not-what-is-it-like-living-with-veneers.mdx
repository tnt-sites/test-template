Getting veneers is a big decision, so it’s only natural to want to know everything about them before you commit. One of the biggest questions many patients ask is, “What do veneers actually feel like in your mouth?” These enhancements are designed to look, feel, and function just like natural teeth. However, there are a few differences worth noting. Continue reading to learn what to expect when [your veneers](/dental-veneers-and-laminates/) are finally placed.

## The First Few Days

Right after placement, it’s normal for your veneers to feel a little different from your natural teeth. Your tongue, lips, and cheeks are sensitive to even small changes, so you might notice:

- A slight feeling of “thickness” compared to natural enamel
- Sensitivity to hot or cold foods
- Increased smoothness when your tongue swipes across
- Mild irritation around your gums

These sensations usually fade within a week or two as the various tissues in your mouth get used to the presence of porcelain.

## Day-to-Day Comfort

Once your mouth has adjusted, veneers should feel frictionless and comfortable. In fact, many patients say they forget they even have them after a while. This is because the shells are made specifically to fit your teeth, so they blend in exceptionally well.

You’ll be able to eat, talk, and laugh just as you did before. The only difference will be the confidence you’ll experience knowing your smile looks brighter and more polished.

## How Do Veneers Compare with Natural Teeth?

Porcelain veneers are made with non-porous materials, which means they resist staining better than enamel. They also feel more “slick,” which some patients are acutely aware of at first. But this isn’t necessarily a bad thing. Some patients find the smoother sensations enjoyable.

## When to Check in with Your Dentist

Veneers are incredibly successful as a treatment, rarely causing discomfort. However, if your veneers continue to feel bulky or cause bite problems after the adjustment period, schedule a visit. Your enhancements may simply need a slight adjustment, so everything feels perfectly natural.

There are few things as satisfying as looking in the mirror and loving what you see. Veneers are a comprehensive treatment that can completely transform your smile in a seamless, comfortable way. If you’re eager to experience all the positivity veneers can bring to your life, your dentist is just a phone call or email away.
