## **Invisalign for Straightening Teeth**

[Invisalign](//www.invisalign.com/treatable-cases) remains a way of treating crooked teeth without needing to use braces. If you have mild to moderate problems with your bite, our [cosmetic dentist](/what-is-cosmetic-dentistry-procedures/) downtown in Austin can improve your bite and the way your teeth look.

## **What are some of the dental issues Invisalign can treat?**

The Toothbar Austin [Cosmetic dentistry services](/services/) straighten your teeth if you have the following mild to moderate orthodontic issues, such as:

1. [An overbite.](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/qa/what-is-an-overbite)

8. [Generally crooked teeth.](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/guide/crooked-teeth-misaligned-bites#1)

## **How does your Downtown Cosmetic Dentist use Invisalign to Straighten your Teeth?**

When you use the Invisalign method of straightening your teeth, you wear clear trays that gradually move your teeth. You’ll wear these trays 24 hours per day, each day. The Invisalign company recommends that its clients wear trays for at least 22 hours per day.

The only time you need to remove your dental trays is when you perform your routine dental hygiene such as brushing and flossing your teeth. You’ll also need to brush and otherwise clean your dental trays to keep them clean, too.

Every several weeks you’ll need to return to your dental specialist for your next set of dental trays. Each set of trays moves your teeth a bit more until they reach their desired position.

## **The Invisalign Consultation and Treatments**

Your Toothbar downtown cosmetic dentist sets up a free consultation with you to determine the correct treatment for your situation. The dental professional takes a 3D iTero scan of your mouth, analyzes the general health of your teeth, and determines the bone structure of your mouth. You’ll get an excellent impression of the current position of your teeth. You’ll also get a good idea of the results of your Invisalign final results.

After this appointment, you’ll receive your first set of[Invisalign trays](//www.realself.com/invisalign). The first trays should be available in a few weeks. The more you leave your trays in your mouth, the better they’ll work to correct the position of your teeth. You should only take them out to eat and to clean your teeth. You’ll get new aligners about every six weeks.

Once your teeth straightening process is completed, you’ll need to wear a retainer to keep your smile straight.
