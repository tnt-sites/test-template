## How to Get Relief From Itching Gums

If your gums are feeling overly irritated, you must know how to find relief until you can see the dentist for Austin Preventative Dentistry. Thankfully, there are a few easy steps you can take to decrease your discomfort.

- If the itching is caused by irritating plaque, [brushing and flossing](https://www.uwhealth.org/health/topic/actionset/dental-care-brushing-and-flossing-your-teeth/uh1644.html) can bring quick relief. Brushing after each meal can prevent itching from occurring.
- Rinsing your mouth with warm saltwater can also bring relief. You can use this rinse two to three times a day until your symptoms stop.
- Should your itching gums be caused by an allergic reaction, taking an antihistamine can bring fast and lasting relief. If this is an ongoing problem, seek your doctor for testing.

## Common Causes of Itchy Gums

### 1. Gum Disease

Gum illness is one of the most common causes of Gum irritation. Almost half of Americans [aged 30](https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/research/data-statistics/periodontal-disease/adults) and up have some periodontal disease. This disorder, which ranges from minor inflammation (gingivitis) to severe disease (periodontitis), can produce swollen, painful, and irritated gums. [Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-and-oral-health/itchy-gums) provides more thorough information about gums discomfort and their causes.

### 2. Allergic Reactions

Itchy gums can result from allergies to certain types of food, dental materials, or oral hygiene products. It is essential to know what causes these allergies and how to avoid them to maintain healthy teeth. [Colgate](https://www.colgate.com/en-us/oral-health/gum-disease/why-do-you-have-itchy-gums) offers a closer examination of how allergies could affect your gum health.

### 3. Poor Oral Hygiene

Minimal brushing and flossing may lead to plaque accumulation around the gum line, resulting in itching. Regular dental exams and cleanings can help avoid this problem. [Shawnessy Dental](https://shawnessydental.com/7-reasons-why-your-gums-are-itchy/) explains why your gums may be itchy, stressing the necessity of proper dental care.
