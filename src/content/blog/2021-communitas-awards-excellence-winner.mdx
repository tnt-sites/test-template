We are grateful to be recognized as a 2021 [Communitas Award](https://communitasawards.com/) winner. The Communitas Awards recognize organizations and individuals who unselfishly give of themselves and their resources within programs involving volunteerism, philanthropy, ethical and sustainable business practices. We were nominated for this award based on our 2019 Impact Report created by fellow [Certified B Corporation](https://bcorporation.net/), [Evolution Marketing](https://greenmkting.com/). Hats off to Lisa and Michael Bauer at Evolution for all of the work on our impact reports over time!
