Dental sealants are thin coatings painted by a dentist on the occlusal surfaces of teeth. Dental sealants are typically painted on the back teeth, and statistics show they can prevent up to [80% of cavities](https://www.cdc.gov/vitalsigns/dental-sealants/index.html#:~:text=Dental%20sealants%20prevent%2080%25%20of,9%20in%2010%20cavities%20occur.). Although the number of kids with sealants continues to rise, the opposite is true for low-income families. These children are 20% less likely to get them and twice more likely to have untreated cavities. Usually, dental sealants are considered Austin [cosmetic dentistry services](/).

## **What Happens to Untreated Cavities?**

Cavities are among the most prevalent health issues worldwide, especially among kids. However, anyone else with teeth can get cavities. If cavities go untreated, they could escalate to issues such as severe pain, infection, or tooth loss. However, while treatment is available, it would be best to seek Austin preventative dentistry. Once you visit a dentist, one of the most likely recommendations is to get [dental sealants](https://www.mouthhealthy.org/en/az-topics/s/sealants). They can eliminate the need for alternative treatments, such as dental fillings or crowns, which can be expensive and invasive.

### **Who Should Get Sealants?**

Children and teenagers have a likelihood of developing decay in the pits and fissures of their back teeth. Therefore, they are eligible for sealants. Nevertheless, adults can also reap the benefits of sealants.

Ideally, children should seek Austin preventative dentistry services for sealants as soon as their permanent posterior set in. That way, these sealants can protect them from cavities through their most susceptible years of ages 6 to 14.

### **The Application Process**

The application of dental sealants is quick easy, with no pain. Before the procedure, the dentist thoroughly cleans the teeth to be sealed. The dentist will use an acid solution on the chewing surfaces to roughen them up. The dentist then goes ahead to rinse the teeth before painting the sealant. In some cases, the dentist may use a special curing light for better results. Additionally, the dentist has to use tooth conditioner to enhance the adhesive force of sealants.

### **Are Sealants Permanent?**

Sealants can serve you for up to 10 years. However, it would help if you saw your dentist regularly for checkups. The dentist can check for chippings, and if necessary, replace the sealants. It is essential to take good care of sealants. Like unsealed teeth, they call for [high-level dental hygiene](https://www.cdc.gov/oralhealth/basics/adult-oral-health/tips.html).
