Tooth erosion is often referred to as the silent epidemic when it comes to oral health. While the advances in oral health care today has come a long way, resulting to better quality of life, tooth erosion remains to be a common problem among individuals of all ages. We have even noticed a similar trend here at our [Massachusetts dental practice](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/meet-our-dentists.html), particularly with the increasing number of patients who complain about having sensitive teeth. Other signs that you may have tooth erosion include unexplained tooth discoloration, the appearance of sand-blasted or rounded teeth, transparency along the biting edges, and presence of cracks. **What Causes Tooth Erosion?** The best way to prevent tooth erosion from happening is to know what causes it. By and large, tooth erosion is a result of the following:

*   **Acidic foods and drink**s such as pickles, salad dressings, sports drinks, carbonated beverages,  and fruit juices. In addition, the sugar in acidic beverages may also increase one’s risk of tooth decay.
*   **Dry mouth syndrome**. Your saliva plays a huge role in neutralizing the acidity in your mouth hence tooth erosion may result from having dry mouth.
*   **Certain medications.** Tooth erosion may occur either due to the acidic nature of medications or its ability to cause dry mouth as an adverse effect.
*   **Health conditions that promote regurgitation of stomach acids** such as bulimia and acid reflux.
*   **Brushing immediately after consumption of acidic foods and drinks**.
