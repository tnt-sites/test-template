A Conscious Capitalism event brought to you by the Conscious Capitalism, Chicago Chapter with support from Envision IT, Wisconsin Sustainable Business Council, Dane Buy Local, DRM Transit, **Artisan Dental** and B Local Wisconsin.

Please join us for an evening of community, mindfulness, and learning with Dr. Daniel Friedland, MD presenting Leading Well from Within: A Neuroscience and Mindfulness-Based Framework for Conscious Leadership.

When: Wednesday June 6th in Madison, Wisconsin

Where: The Overture Center (2nd floor in the Promenade Hall)

Cost: $25 (non-members)

Itinerary: 6:00-6:30pm Gathering and networking

6:30-8:00pm Presentation and facilitated Q&A

8:00-8:30pm Wrap up and final connections

Leading Well from Within key points:

• Learn skills and practices to better navigate stress

• Cultivate your creative mindset as a high-performance conscious professional

• Lead with greater wisdom and a creative mindset

• Improve communication and collaboration

• Contribute to a healthier, energized, and actively engaged culture

Who is Dr. Daniel Friedland, MD?

Dr. Daniel Friedland is CEO of SuperSmartHealth, author, husband, and father. “Dr. Danny” is an expert on the science and practice of Conscious Leadership. He served as Co-Chair of Conscious Capitalism in San Diego and was a Founding Chair of the Academy of Integrative Health and Medicine; he understands the challenges of leadership and the growth it asks. He now spends his time helping today’s leaders and their organizations leverage neuroscience and mindfulness-based skills to multiply their positive impact in the world.
