## What Is A Dental Crown?

A [dental crown](https://www.healthline.com/health/what-causes-dental-crown-tooth-pain-and-how-to-relieve-it#when-to-seek-help) is a human-made cap that encompasses or covers a damaged tooth. Dental crowns are made either from porcelain, metals, or porcelain fused metals. Out of the three, porcelain is preferred due to its strength and durability. The reason why dentists install a dental crown is to restore the tooth size and shape. However, if proper precautionary measures are not considered, it could lead to dental crown pain.

#### What are the causes of Dental Crown Pain?

1. **Cavities and Tooth Decay:** Placement of dental crowns on teeth that show signs of dental caries may cause dental crown pain. Over time, the small black speck will develop into a cavity that damages the nerves. Pain arising from tooth decay is only corrected by removing the tooth or through a root canal. We at Austin [Preventative Dentistry](/preventive-dentistry/) center advocate that you frequently floss and brush at least twice per day to prevent tooth decay.
2. **Infection:** When [bacteria infect a tooth](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/tooth-abscess/symptoms-causes/syc-20350901#:~:text=Bacteria%20can%20enter%20the%20innermost,caused%20by%20a%20bacterial%20infection.), one presents with pain, inflammation, redness, and pus production. Dental crown tends to exacerbate pain associated with tooth infection as it presses down on the already damaged nerve. At Austin Cosmetic Dentistry, tooth infections are a thing of the past. Our dentist at Austin Preventative Dentistry recommends using antibacterial mouthwash to prevent tooth infection.
3. **Recessed and Sore gums:** Pain may emancipate from sore gums after a dental crown procedure. However, the discomfort should not be a cause of alarm as the ache is expected to reduce in two weeks. Additionally, dental crown pain could be due to a recessed gum line. A recessed gum line triggers gum diseases like gingivitis and facilitates plaque accumulation.
4. **Erroneous Dental Crown Installation:** Dental crowns with improper fit tend to press on the tooth nerve, causing pain. 5. **Dental Crown Fracture:** Bruxism is a medical condition characterized by frequent grinding of one’s teeth, especially at night. Bruxism, trauma, or an old dental crown can cause fracture.
