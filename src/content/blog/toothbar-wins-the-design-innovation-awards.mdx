In 2019, the [American Dental Association](https://www.ada.org/en) (ADA) gave Toothbar its “New Build” award as part of its [inaugural Design Innovation Awards program](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/toothbar-wins-the-new-build-award-from-the-ada-design-innovation-awards-300976027.html). The organization recognized Toothbar for the functionality, visual appeal, and innovative design of its new facilities. The prestigious award has added to Toothbar’s reputation in the world of Austin cosmetic dentistry.

## What The Award Entails

In giving Toothbar its [New Build award](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/toothbar-wins-the-new-build-award-from-the-ada-design-innovation-awards-300976027.html), the ADA praised the design, function, and aesthetics of the office’s physical space. As a reward, Toothbar will receive a cash prize and feature in future ADA publications.

### Why Toothbar Won

Toothbar’s innovative design reimagines the entire [dental experience](/express-services/), providing patients with a comfortable and relaxing environment. The lobby, with its floor-to-ceiling windows and modern aesthetic, creates a reassuringly professional atmosphere. While waiting for their appointments, patients can enjoy the massage chairs, bar, and snacks of the Toothbar Lounge. They can also request a blanket and pillows to get even more comfortable.

With free wifi and accessible charging stations, the Toothbar waiting area is perfect for today’s busy patients. Toothbar even provides a “brush bar” where patients can give themselves one last cleaning before meeting with the dentist.

Located in Austin’s thriving Seaholm District, Toothbar knows how to cater to a young, entrepreneurial clientele. The neighborhood’s workers are busy, and they don’t want visiting the dentist to feel like a chore. Toothbar provides them with a new-age dental experience that they can enjoy getting used to.

### How Toothbar Was Selected As Winner

The ADA gave Toothbar its first-ever New Build award because the facilities matched its stated criteria. The ADA was looking for appealing aesthetics, attractive color schemes, effective branding, and the utilization of new technology.

Toothbar was initially chosen alongside two other finalists from a larger pool of applicants. From there, attendees at the ADA FDI World Dental Congress in San Francisco voted for the winner. When all the votes had been tallied, Toothbar came out on top.
