[Special needs dentistry](/vista-ca/special-needs-dentistry/) is an essential branch of dentistry. It often focuses on meeting the needs of patients with special needs. Children with special health care needs (SHCN) deserve to receive quality care. But not all providers can address the additional challenges of working with them. If your child has special needs, it is vital to find a dentist trained to work with children with SHCN. Read on to find out more about **special needs dentistry**.

## Special needs dentistry

Children with SHCN have chronic physical, behavioral, developmental, or emotional conditions. Special needs children usually need extra support or time when seeing a provider. This can include children with cleft lip or palate and Down syndrome. Several other medical conditions also need special dental care and treatment. These include cerebral palsy and vision and hearing impairments.

### Oral conditions

Oral problems affect all children. But compared to the general population, special needs children have more oral health problems. Kids with special needs often have behavioral issues or impaired mobility. Complications such as these may make it hard for them to receive quality dental care. They may also increase the chances of developing oral health problems.

Several factors may contribute to poor oral health in children with special needs. These include oral conditions, physical limitations, and even medications. Some children with special needs are not able to clean their teeth. Others find it difficult to brush and floss properly. Tooth eruption may be delayed, inconsistent, or accelerated in kids with SHCN. Dental caries is also common in kids with developmental disabilities.

A kid with special needs may exhibit several signs when there is an oral health problem. These include grinding teeth, foul-smelling breath, or food refusal. Children may also prefer softer foods or have discolored teeth. Other signs include changes in behavior like touching in and around the mouth, jaw, and teeth. Parents and caregivers should watch out for these signs and symptoms.

### Pediatric dentistry

Kids should have their first dental visit before the age of 1. This is essential, especially for children with special needs. But many providers lack the knowledge or skills to provide adequate care to kids with SHCN. Some dentists usually do not like taking on patients that require extra care.

Fortunately, pediatric dentists are equipped to cater to the needs of these children. The providers can provide dental care that is tailored to the child’s comfort and tolerance levels. They can even recommend useful oral healthcare products. Dentists can also help them avoid dental problems like gum disease and tooth decay.

## Oral healthcare for kids with special needs

Proper oral care is crucial for the health of kids with special needs. Children’s special needs may make it hard for them to effectively care for their teeth. Sometimes, a child needs adult help to brush the teeth properly. It is vital for parents or caregivers to help with these daily tasks. Regular dental visits are also essential.
