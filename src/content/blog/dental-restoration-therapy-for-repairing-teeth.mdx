A [dental restoration](/vista-ca/dental-restorations/) is a great option when it comes to repairing teeth. Patients might wonder when it is necessary to have a restoration to restore the teeth. Understanding what other restorations are used for helps make a decision. Keep reading to learn more about the options.

## Getting veneers on the teeth

Veneers are not always used when a repair is required, but they can help hide the damage. They might improve the appearance of the teeth. For instance, if a patient has chips in their teeth, the veneers can work to hide them. Veneers are also great for discolored, misshapen, or crooked teeth. If the patient decides to get veneers, the dentist can customize the veneers for them. The dentist will match the shade to the surrounding teeth so that it does not look any different.

Patients can get a natural appearance with veneers on the teeth. It is a popular **dental restoration** for various dental issues. And if a patient cares for the restorations correctly, the veneers might last close to two decades. Patients will need to brush and floss their teeth often to keep them in great condition. Of course, if a patient gets their teeth whitened, the whitening should happen before the veneers are placed.

### Getting dental crowns

Dental crowns are a common type of restoration for repairing teeth. These can stop the tooth from eroding and allowing bacteria into the area. A crown is useful when the tooth is too damaged to stand on its own. Patients might get a crown after a root canal to protect what remains of that tooth. Or a crown might be useful for helping with chips or cracks.

One of the crowns’ benefits is that they can stop the tooth from completely cracking, requiring an extraction. If the tooth starts to break, it is not useful, and it might cause pain. Instead, the teeth need to last a patient’s lifetime. That means saving a tooth, not extracting it, is ideal. The dentist can use a _dental restoration_ to save the tooth. And if a patient cares for the teeth correctly, the crown could last three decades or more.

### Getting dental fillings

One of the more common dental restorations is a filling. A patient might get one if there is tooth decay present. Patients can choose from materials such as silver, porcelain, gold, or ceramic. Each one has benefits and drawbacks, so the dentist can help make the decision. A patient might also get glass ionomer, although it is not as expected.

Instead, consider talking to the dentist about when to repair the damage to your teeth.
