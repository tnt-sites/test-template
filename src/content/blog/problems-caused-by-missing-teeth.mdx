Here are some of the most common problems caused by missing teeth:

### Shifting of the Teeth

If you miss some teeth, the remaining ones will shift toward the gap. You might notice that the teeth on one side of your mouth are lower than on the other. You might also see that you have gaps between your teeth.

These gaps can make you self-conscious about smiling and eating in front of others. As you age, the gaps will become more noticeable and concern your Austin dentist.

### Changes in Facial Structure

It can cause changes in facial structure. This type of change in your facial structure may not be noticeable outside, but it can greatly impact self-confidence.

If you’re missing teeth, this can affect your ability to speak and eat. You may feel like you have to take extra time when speaking and chew food more thoroughly.
