We received an inspiring email from a student at Madison West High School working on a project in her health class with the subject “Raising Awareness of Diabetes and Its Complication”.  She stumbled upon our blog page [/diabetes-and-your-smile/](/diabetes-and-your-smile/) while searching about the topic on Google.

She asked us to share this page which has a complete list of resources about Diabetes:

[https://www.dietspotlight.com/diabetes-resources/](https://www.dietspotlight.com/diabetes-resources/) - Diabetes Resources: Tools, Guides, Stats, Tip Sheets

"November is Diabetes Awareness Month please help me with this campaign."

We are heartened to see this young woman take such initiative to spread the word about something she is passionate about and are happy to help her raise awareness.
