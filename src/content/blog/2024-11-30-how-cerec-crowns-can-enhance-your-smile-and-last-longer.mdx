[](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/shutterstock_2493521567-scaled.jpg)[CEREC crowns](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/restorative-dentistry.html#same) stand as a revolutionary solution in dental restorations, offering both aesthetics and durability. If you've recently upgraded your smile with these advanced ceramic restorations, you might wonder how to maintain their pristine condition. Proper care not only preserves their appearance but also extends their lifespan, ensuring you enjoy a brilliant smile for years to come.

## Regular Dental Hygiene Practices

Consistent oral hygiene is crucial for the longevity of your CEREC crowns. Brush your teeth at least twice a day using a soft-bristled toothbrush, ensuring you reach all areas around and above the crown to prevent plaque buildup. Fluoride toothpaste will help strengthen the crown and the surrounding teeth. Additionally, floss daily to remove debris between the teeth and crowns, reducing the risk of gum disease and tooth decay.

## Avoid Hard and Sticky Foods

While CEREC crowns are known for their durability, they are not indestructible. To avoid potential damage, steer clear of hard candies, ice, and other biting objects that can cause cracks or chips. Sticky foods should also be on your avoidance list, as they can pull at the crown and weaken its bond with your tooth. Opting for softer foods and cutting harder foods into smaller pieces can help maintain the integrity of your crowns.

## Use a Night Guard If Necessary

If you grind your teeth at night, wearing a night guard can provide significant protection for your CEREC crowns. Teeth grinding, or bruxism, can exert excessive pressure on your crowns, leading to premature wear or even breakage. A custom-fitted night guard from your dentist will shield your crowns and natural teeth from the forces of grinding, ensuring they remain intact and functional.

## Schedule Regular Dental Check-Ups

Regular visits to your dentist are essential for maintaining the health of your CEREC crowns. During these check-ups, your dentist can inspect your crowns for any signs of wear or damage and perform professional cleanings. These visits also provide an opportunity to address any minor issues before they become major concerns, contributing to the long-term durability of your crowns.
