## You Can Prevent Health Problems

Your smile is among the first things people notice about you, so taking care of your teeth and gums is important. Good oral hygiene can help control gum disease, tooth decay, and bad breath. Besides, it is important for your overall health. Poor mouth health has been linked to heart disease, stroke, and diabetes.

You can prevent health problems by brushing and flossing daily, visiting your dentist regularly, and eating a healthy diet. It would help if you also avoid smoking and using tobacco products. Consult your dentist if you have any concerns about your tooth health.
