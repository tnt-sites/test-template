[](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/emergency-dental-care-scaled.jpg)If you have a serious problem with your teeth or the soft tissue in your mouth, getting [emergency dental care](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/emergency-dentistry.html) can help ensure that your oral health is protected. However, you may not recognize that you have a dental emergency right away, and even if pain is present, you may be tempted to wait it out. Understanding what constitutes a dental emergency can help you avoid unnecessary pain and prevent further complications. Here are some of the most common dental emergencies.

## Trauma to the Mouth

Any injury to the mouth that results in a loose, broken, or knocked-out tooth constitutes a dental emergency. A broken crown or other restoration is often a dental emergency as well. These injuries need to be treated immediately to protect and preserve the tooth and avoid infection.

## Toothache

It may surprise you to learn that a toothache can constitute a dental emergency. Pain is your body’s way of signaling something is wrong. With intense or consistent pain, the signal could indicate issues such as nerve damage or infection.

## Gum Abscess

What looks like a pimple on your gum might actually be an infection called an abscess. An abscess can damage your teeth. Oral infections can also [spread to the surrounding tissues](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/symptoms-of-tooth-infection-spreading-to-the-body) and potentially even the rest of the body. The sooner you receive emergency dental care, the more likely you will avoid infection and preserve your tooth.

## Food Stuck Between Teeth

Though typically you will be able to dislodge food debris such as a popcorn kernel or apple skin between your teeth, there may be times when it is not possible to do so on your own. This is a dental emergency. Stuck food can be a hotbed for harmful bacteria that can lead to dental infection if not addressed. Additionally, food lodged between your teeth is often uncomfortable.
