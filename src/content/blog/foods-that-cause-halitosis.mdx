## How Food and Halitosis Are Linked Together?

There are some limited foods that cause halitosis by introducing strong odours, promoting bacterial growth, or causing dry mouth. Food affects your breath in the following ways:

- Bad breath is caused by sulfur compounds found in foods like garlic, onions, and spices that stay in your mouth and bloodstream.
- Sugary and starchy foods can encourage the growth of bacteria in your mouth, which can result in the production of odorous by-products.
- Saliva, which naturally cleans your mouth, can be decreased by caffeine, alcohol, and salty meals.

### Foods That Fight Halitosis

Thankfully, some certain food items actively fight bad breath. Add these to your diet chart:

- Apples, carrots, and celery. They stimulate saliva production and remove food particles.
- Parsley and mint contain natural odors-neutralized chemicals.
- Rich in antioxidants, green tea reduces the bacteria that cause halitosis.
- Yogurt and fermented foods improve gut health. These probiotic options positively impact breath.
- Hydration is a must. Staying hydrated washes away food debris and keeps saliva flowing.

## Oral Hygiene Routine To Support Your Nutrition

![Oral Hygiene Routine To Prevent Halitosis](../../assets/images/blog/Oral-Hygiene-Routine-To-prevent-Halitosis-300x188.png)

A Good yet balanced [oral hygiene routine](https://www.jnj.com/health-and-wellness/9-tips-to-boost-your-oral-hygiene-routine) is just as important in preventing halitosis as dietary choices.

To begin with, you have to brush your tongue as well as your teeth twice every day and even floss so often (2 times a day) to get rid of stuck food particles. Antimicrobial mouthwash is a great idea to eliminate bacteria that cause odor. Use a scraper to clean your tongue and get rid of bacteria & debris.

### Practical Tips for Fresh Breath

- Avoid tobacco and alcohol as both cause dry mouth and bad breath.
- Chew on gum that has no sugar. It stimulates saliva and freshens your breath.
- For an instant natural breath freshener, keep a nutritious snack on hand.
- If you have an important meeting or date, avoid eating garlic and onions. It’s important to limit odorous foods before social events.
