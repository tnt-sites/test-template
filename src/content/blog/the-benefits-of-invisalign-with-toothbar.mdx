For many years, metal braces were the only treatment option for misaligned teeth. Those dental devices are effective, but most patients find them to be uncomfortable and unsightly. They also require frequent trips to the dentist and constant daily maintenance. With Invisalign aligners from The Toothbar, you won’t have to worry about any of those drawbacks. Our team can help you come up with a long-term treatment plan that pulls your teeth back into alignment so that you can once again smile with confidence.



### The True Dangers of Malocclusion

Malocclusion is the medical term for misaligned teeth, and it is a much bigger problem than most people realize. In addition to being unsightly, a misaligned bite pattern will also increase your risk of many different [oral health complications](https://www.ahchealthenews.com/2018/04/04/crooked-teeth-hurt-self-esteem/) including dental fractures, tooth loss, periodontal disease, and tooth decay. Malocclusion can be a serious issue when left untreated, and that is why so many patients have turned to advanced restorative devices like Invisalign aligners.

### A Look at Invisalign

Our team understands just how frustrating malocclusion can be, and we are devoted to offering our patients some of the most effective treatments in the world. Invisalign aligners are nothing more than clear plastic trays that are practically invisible once they are in the mouth. Unlike traditional braces, these trays can be taken out at any time throughout the day. While patients should try to wear their Invisalign aligners for at least 20 hours a day, they can quickly remove them to eat a meal or brush their teeth.

### Coming Up With a Treatment Plan

Every patient has their own unique needs, and that is why we believe in personalized treatment plans. During your initial consultation at The Toothbar, one of our team members will completely inform you of all the pros and cons of Invisalign aligners. We will also need to take a careful look at your teeth and gums to make sure that you are a good candidate. Even though the success rates for Invisalign are very high, these devices aren’t for everyone. Once we have determined that you are a good candidate, we can take 3D images of your teeth in order to [create the custom aligners](https://www.invisalign.com/how-invisalign-works/how-custom-aligners-are-made).
