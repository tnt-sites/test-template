**Artisan Dental to Participate as a Panelist in B Corp Webinar- How to Tell Your Impact Story**

[Join](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/tZYpcOmqqjgqHdGXknaxmZ3v5qweNHWfP0_5) the [B Corp](https://www.bcorporation.net/en-us/) Marketers Network, Jul 14, 2022 at 12:00 PM, for a one-hour lunchtime panel plus Q&A on impact storytelling for marketers at purpose-led companies. Our own Director of Business Development, [Rochelle Guastella](/doctors-team/rochelle/), will be one of the contributing panelists. This event brings together marketing leaders within the B Corp community to showcase how we can distinguish ourselves from companies operating within the business as usual economy. Telling your impact story authentically and compellingly helps to elevate and grow not just your company, but our entire movement.

Attendees will learn about topics ranging from engaging youth, steering clear of “greenwashing,” and how to use impact metrics as a tool for quantifying your positive effect on the world. We’ll reserve ample time for Q&A after the discussion.
