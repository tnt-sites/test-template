**[WSBC](https://www.wisconsinsustainability.com/) (Wisconsin Sustainable Business Council)
[2022 AWARD RECEPTION & 15th ANNUAL CONFERENCE](https://www.wisconsinsustainability.com/2022-conference)**
*\-Green Bay, WI*
Tours & Award Reception - Tue, Nov. 15, 2022
15th Annual WSBC  Conference - Wed, Nov. 16, 2022

Sustainability has come far and still; we have a long way to go. In celebrating the 15th annual conference, we will reflect on how far we have come in the last 15 years, coupled with the (R)Evolution that is necessary to get us to a truly sustainable and just future. We invite you to join, reflect, learn, and discuss the challenges and opportunities you are facing in your business and what is needed to get you there! Only together, we can evolve to where we need to go.

We're proud to announce that [Scott Andersen](/doctors-team/scott-andersen/), co-owner and Director of Stakeholder Stewardship is a Sustainable Leadership – Executive Award Finalist and [Rochelle Guastella](/doctors-team/rochelle/), Director of Business Development, will be speaking at the conference.

Join us to form and nourish valuable connections and play your part in the (R)Evolution of Sustainability.
