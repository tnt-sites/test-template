Dentures are put in the mouth after tooth extraction. Many people are reluctant to commit to the entire process because it takes a lot more time. Permanent dentures cannot be put in the mouth immediately after tooth extraction. Before the dentures can be fitted, the oral tissues and gums must heal.

## What Are The Causes Of Tooth Extraction?

- Extraction might be necessary when the pulp is affected by severe decay. The bacteria which causes the decay might spread an illness. If so, root canal surgery could be used to treat the infection. However, your implant dentist could suggest an extraction in some severe cases.
- Periodontal disease can occasionally cause a tooth to become loose. When this occurs, a dental extraction may be an option.
- Your dentist in Littleton may advise extraction to stop the impacted tooth from harming nearby teeth. Extraction of an impacted tooth could reduce the risk of infection and overcrowding.
- It could be required to extract one or more teeth to get rid of overcrowding. Your implant dentist might suggest extraction when the patient needs to receive orthodontic treatment, and there isn't enough room for the teeth to move and realign.

## How Much Time Will It Take To Get Dentures After Removing The Teeth?

Depending on the type, a patient may need to wait a certain amount before getting dentures. A patient's custom-made dentures should fit three to six months after [tooth extraction](https://emedicine.medscape.com/article/82774-overview). The gum tissue has plenty of time to repair while you wait this long. This waiting period also ensures that the dentures will fit perfectly. During the waiting period, there is no need for concern regarding your gums. As the gum tissue heals, your dentist in Littleton will give you temporary immediate dentures. Once the tissues have fully recovered, your implant dentist will place the dentures in your mouth. After the tooth extraction, it typically takes six to eight weeks for dentures to be fitted. But every patient is different. Some people will need to wait for several months or more, while others just need to wait one month.

## What Are The Different Types Of Dentures Available?

- **Complete Dentures-**Your quality of life may suffer if most or all of your teeth are missing. All the foods you love and require for good health are no longer available. Without the support of your teeth, your facial muscles may appear droopy, and your speech may alter. You can regain your entire smile with complete dentures, allowing you to return to your regular life. We offer several complete denture alternatives designed to appear and feel as natural as possible. While getting used to wearing dentures can take some time, after getting your new smile, you can feel confident and at ease once more after a few months.
- **Partial Dentures-**Partial dentures are an affordable solution to restore your full smile if you only have a few missing teeth. These removable appliances maintain your tooth structure, so there is less chance of shifting by using a metal framework to fit with them and provide the chewing surface you require. Additionally, partial dentures give you a stunning smile that can increase your confidence.
- **Implant-Supported Dentures-**You won't have to be concerned about adhesives or slipping accidents when you have implant-supported dentures. Dental implants secure dentures, giving them a stable foundation for more natural function. Implant-supported dentures come in various designs; some are detachable, while others are permanently fixed.
