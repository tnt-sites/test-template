Individuals suffering from discolored teeth, uneven gums, or chipped teeth psychologically suffer as they lack self-confidence. ### Here are some dental cosmetic procedures that may help alleviate this problem:

- **Teeth whitening:** [Teeth whitening or teeth bleaching](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/teeth-whitening-and-bleaching#1) is a dental cosmetic procedure that aims at removing discoloration from teeth. A professional dental teeth bleaching procedure is considered more effective than the home kits and home remedies.
- **Dental Bonding And Dental Veneers:** Dental bonding is a strategy used to correct tooth chipping. In dental bonding, the dentist uses a composite resinous substance to cover the chipped part of the tooth. When looking for a more permanent approach, consider signing up for dental veneers. [Dental veneers procedure](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-veneers) involves binding porcelain shells directly on the tooth surface. It is more permanent and gives a better smile appearance. *Austin cosmetic dentistry* tooth crown procedure involves reconstructing the tooth's crown to protect the sensitive interior parts of a tooth. This procedure improves the shape and size of the tooth.
- **Misaligned Teeth And Missing Teeth:** When a tooth falls off, the gum area where the tooth was starts to degenerate because the gum is no longer functional. This causes the jawline and surrounding muscles to sag, which makes an individual appear older. However, with current technological advancement at Austin Cosmetic Industry center, dental implants are available. [Dental implants](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/dental-implant-surgery/about/pac-20384622) made are authentic and will reconstruct the teeth from the crown to the roots. Dental implants will aid in mastication and improves one's aesthetic appearance. Gaping and misaligned teeth often present a problem when brushing. This is because food particles hide in between the teeth where the toothbrush cannot reach. Therefore, such individuals opt to align their teeth using clear aligners as opposed to braces. Clear aligners are comfortable and affordable. Unlike braces, clear aligners are easy to remove and install; therefore, one is exempted from costly dental visits now and then when they want their braces to be adjusted.
- **Gum Contouring:** Individuals with uneven, receding, or gummy smiles should consider visiting a dentist for a[gum contouring procedure](https://www.healthline.com/health/gum-contouring#what-is-it). Gum contouring is a soft laser technique that cuts away part of the gum, making an individual's teeth look uneven or short. The procedure is quite painful and requires a few days to heal completely.
