## When to Consider Dentures?

Recent history has seen a shift away from the use of dentures as the first choice for most patients. Dentures were once the only choice for dental patients who were looking to maintain the integrity of their teeth. Dentures are a popular option for those who want to replace the teeth they have lost through injury or decay. Gum disease can cause issues with a loss of teeth, which can be treated using removable dental implants, according to the [American Dental Association](https://www.mouthhealthy.org/en/az-topics/d/dentures#:~:text=Dentures%20are%20removable%20appliances%20that,your%20appearance%20and%20your%20health.).
