### How Many Teeth Do Kids Have?

According to [Mount Healthy](https://www.mouthhealthy.org/en/az-topics/e/eruption-charts), a partner of the American Dental Association (ADA), kids start out with 20 baby teeth that erupt between 6-33 months of age. Children start to lose their baby teeth as early as between ages 5-6. The last baby tooth is lost between the age of 11 and 12. Their adult teeth start growing as soon as age 6. The last adult tooth grows in as late as age 21 with the molars. Austin Invisalign dentistry can help to aid any child or teen's teeth to grow straightly.

### Can I Start My Child's Tooth Care Regimen Before He/She Gets Teeth?

Yes! According to [WebMD](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/guide/dental-health-your-childs-teeth#1), infant tooth care will encourage proper speech development and overall nutrition.

Even before their teeth erupt from the gums, you can start a cleaning regimen by taking a clean damp cloth and using water to rinse the gums throughout the day. Austin preventative dentistry will give you more information on proper tooth care for your child as he or she ages.

As your child ages, you can rely on Austin invisalign dentistry to fix their teeth into place if they are growing crookedly.

### How Do I Clean My Child's First Teeth at Age 1?

To discourage swallowing, use a fluoride-free tooth and gum cleanser or toothpaste with an age-appropriate toothbrush to clean your child's teeth.

### When Can I Switch My Child to Fluoride Toothpaste?

Toddlers aged 2-3 have their full set of baby teeth grown. Transition your child to a kids' fluoride toothpaste during this age range to prevent cavities and properly clean their teeth in between meals.
