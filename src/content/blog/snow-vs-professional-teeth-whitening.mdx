## Snow vs. Professional Cosmetic Teeth Whitening

White teeth convey great daily habits and proper grooming. A bright smile draws people towards you and makes a positive first impression.

Bright teeth have helped people in the business world earn the trust of their colleagues because a great smile gives the appearance of trustworthiness, and provides a self-confidence boost.

## SNOW Teeth Whitening

Snow teeth whitening is a teeth whitening method that can be done at home.

It was created to attract those who were skeptical about home teeth whitening kits. The snow teeth whitening kit uses LED light technology to get rid of all sorts of stains, such as tobacco, tea, wine, and coffee.

The kit uses unique technology, a whitening serum, and an application to help you get your teeth whitened. The whitening serum is applied directly to the teeth.

Applying the serum directly has several benefits, such as being less messy, even coverage, and long-lasting. The ingredients used in the serum include peppermint oil, potassium nitrate, carbomer, sodium bicarbonate, deionized water, glycerol, carbamide peroxide, and [hydrogen peroxide](https://www.dentalhealth.org/tooth-whitening).

## Professional Cosmetic Teeth Whitening

Professional cosmetic teeth whitening is done at a dental office. Professional teeth whitening **produces results faster** than at-home remedies because the solution contains more peroxide.

[Teeth whitening](https://www.steelecroftdental.com/blog/6-benefits-teeth-whitening-treatment) at a dental office takes about one hour. The gum is protected from the peroxide using a protective gel or a thin rubber sheet.

**Whitening your teeth at home could lead to damage to your gums, gum receding, and also cause tooth sensitivity.** An [Austin dentist](/) will ensure a comfortable and safe procedure as they monitor the entire process from start to finish.



An orthodontist can customize the process to ensure that you [achieve your whitening preference](https://aedit.com/aedition/professional-vs-at-home-teeth-whitening) and can also target the most affected areas.
