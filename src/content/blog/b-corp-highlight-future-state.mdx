Congratulations to [Future State](https://futurestate.com/), our fellow B Corporation/Best For The World honoree in the Workers category! We are thrilled to share this honor with such an amazing company.

Future State is a consulting firm located in Oakland, California working with global companies and organizations to ensure people are the center of focus to achieve operational transformation and organizational change. They are passionate about the challenges their clients are looking to solve, like the transformation of the healthcare ecosystem, the curing of diseases that impact millions, and the establishment of a technology infrastructure that will allow access for all. They bring this commitment to their work every day, and count their clients among their friends and co-creators in making the world a better place.

Future State’s true definition of success: Benefits for people, purpose and the planet alongside profits.
