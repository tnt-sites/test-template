## Your Smile Matters: Finding the Best Help with Dental Implants

According to the National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research, approximately [2%](https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/research/data-statistics/dental-caries/adults) of Americans between 20 and 64 have lost all their teeth, and about [17%](https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/research/data-statistics/dental-caries/seniors#:~:text=Note%3A%20This%20survey%20applies%20only,and%20older%20have%20no%20teeth.) of senior citizens 65 or older are toothless.

Nonsmokers in this category but are otherwise healthy, with sufficient bone density and intact gums, are the most suitable candidates for dental implants since smoking might impede the healing process. A dental implant is a prosthetic tooth root made of titanium that dentists surgically attach to a patient's jaw to provide a solid foundation for cosmetic dentistry.

A dental implant is a long-term tooth replacement solution. In contrast to conventional dentures, which may require routine replacement or adjusting, dental implants are meant to last a lifetime with the proper upkeep.

## Signs You Ought to Consider a Dental Implant

### Broken, Infected, or Missing Teeth

A missing or damaged tooth has numerous [adverse effects](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4201933/), which is sufficient justification for considering dental implants. Similarly, a tooth infection or a broken tooth causes severe pain and discomfort. These may also cause difficulties when chewing since the teeth close to the gap are rarely stable after extraction due to damage to the jaws and roots. This can make chewing painful, turning eating into a challenging task.

### Gum Disease

Gums are exposed when teeth fall out, and this might lead to food particles being stuck in the gums and causing bacteria that might cause gum diseases. Also, the situation may worsen since cleaning around exposed gums is difficult.

### Jawbone Deformation

In the event of tooth loss, the jawbone is left empty and will degrade if it is not filled up as soon as possible. In some advanced cases, this might change your facial structure, giving you a sunken appearance.

### Change in Speech

Gaps in your teeth or slipping dentures can interfere with speech, especially if it is in the front teeth that help pronounce words.

### Feeling Insecure

When you meet someone for the first time, their teeth are one of the first things you notice. Feeling uneasy and insecure about your smile is enough to get a dental implant. The loss of teeth can be depressing, but it shouldn't stop you from pushing ahead when a natural-looking implant can quickly lift your spirits.
