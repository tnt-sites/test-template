Did you know that we are 1% for the Planet members? That means that at least 1% of our Artisan Dental Health Plan sales goes to supporting nonprofits focused on environmental issues.

[https://youtu.be/rZ0Stj6MWNU](https://youtu.be/rZ0Stj6MWNU)

1% for the Planet works to increase funding to the environment by leading a network of businesses, nonprofits, and individuals working together for a healthy planet. 1% for the Planet assists these businesses by assessing their priorities and connecting them with the most aligned nonprofit partners. All giving in the network goes directly from businesses to approved environmental nonprofits. On an annual basis, 1% for the Planet certifies donations by verifying sales data and donation receipts.

1% for the Planet members have given more than $150 million back to environmental nonprofits since 2002.
