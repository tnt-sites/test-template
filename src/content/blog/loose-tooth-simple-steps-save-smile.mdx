## Loose Tooth Statistics

Statistics show that loose teeth are relatively common in all age groups. Loose teeth are common in children but can also happen to adults. In Austin, the most recent data shows that 35% of children aged 6-11 have at least one loose tooth. The rate is even higher for adolescents aged 12-19, at 50%. And for adults aged 20 and up, the rate is still reasonably high, at 25%.

So, what does this mean for you? If you notice that your teeth seem looser over time, it's essential to see a dentist. They can help determine any underlying cause for your loose teeth and can also provide treatment to help prevent further tooth loss.

## Causes of Loose Tooth

It could [result](https://vitality-dental.co.uk/loose-tooth-simple-steps-to-save-your-smile/) from an injury, gum disease, or aging. If you're concerned about a loose tooth, it's best to consult with your Austin cosmetic dentist. They will be able to assess the situation and provide you with the best course of treatment.

Age is also a causal factor for loose teeth. As people age, their teeth can become loose due to resorption. Resorption occurs when the body breaks down and reabsorbs the bone and tissue that support the teeth. This can happen for various reasons, including gum disease, tooth decay, or even the natural aging process.

If you have loose teeth, visit Austin preventative Dentistry immediately to save your smile. Here are some simple steps to follow:

Schedule an appointment with your dentist. They can assess the situation and devise a plan of action.  
In the meantime, avoid chewing on hard foods or anything that could aggravate the tooth.  
Be sure to brush and floss gently around the area.  
If the tooth is completely loose, you can try to wiggle it back into place using clean hands.  
If you cannot save the tooth, your dentist may recommend getting a dental implant or bridge to replace it.

## Effects of Loose Teeth

Loose teeth can [cause](https://www.healthline.com/health/loose-tooth) pain and discomfort, especially when eating or talking. They can also make it difficult to chew correctly, leading to indigestion or other digestive problems. Loose teeth can be expensive to treat in terms of the cost of dental care and the lost productivity at work or school. In extreme cases, people with loose teeth may need to take time off work or school to recover from oral surgery. Loose teeth can cause anxiety and stress, as well as low self-esteem and body image issues. People with loose teeth may feel embarrassed or self-conscious about their appearance, which can impact their social life and overall quality of life.
