While [laser dentistry](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/dental-technology.html) isn’t a new technology, it has gained popularity in the last couple of decades compared to other methods. However, you may not be familiar with how laser dentistry works, its uses, and its benefits. Let’s take a closer look at laser dentistry so you can decide if it’s right for you.

## **Understanding Laser Dentistry**

Laser dentistry is used in teeth whitening, treating gum disease, finding and removing tooth decay, and more. The lasers use high-intensity light to remove things like plaque, tartar, and bacteria. The lasers can also be used to remove soft tissue like gums and hard tissue like teeth.

## **Benefits of Laser Dentistry**

Laser dentistry is often used in conjunction with traditional dentistry. However, lasers often have advantages over traditional tools.

### Minimal Damage

Dentistry often involves removing tissue of some form, whether it’s plaque, tartar, gums, or teeth. Lasers are far more precise than traditional tissue removal tools. Tools have to be placed inside a patient’s mouth, and getting a proper visual on the treatment site can be a challenge. Lasers don’t have this issue and can remove tissue with high accuracy. This leads to the smallest amount of damage necessary to complete the procedure.

### Bacterial Concerns

The mouth is teeming with bacteria, and that can be concerning during dental procedures where wounds may be made in the mouth. Lasers used in laser dentistry actually sterilize whatever area they treat. This is because the intensity of the laser immediately kills any bacteria that it hits.

### Recovery Time

Recovery is always a priority with dental work. That’s part of what makes laser dentistry so valuable. Laser treatments often recover quicker than traditional treatments because they cause the smallest amount of damage necessary, and the treatment area becomes sterilized.
