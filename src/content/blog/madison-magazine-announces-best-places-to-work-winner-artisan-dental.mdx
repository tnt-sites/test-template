Madison Magazine Announces Best Places to Work Winner - Artisan Dental!

We are thrilled! More than 100 local companies were nominated in [Madison Magazine’s](http://www.channel3000.com/madison-magazine/city-life/the-2017-best-places-to-work/623983581) annual Best Places to Work survey measuring six categories of engagement: communication and resources, individual needs, manager effectiveness, personal engagement, team dynamics and trust in leadership.

We LOVE working here and are excited to be selected.
