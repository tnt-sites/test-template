## Are Dental Implants Painful?

Toothbar administers local or general anesthesia during the implant procedure. Local anesthesia is injected into your cheek and gums to completely numb your mouth. And general anesthesia puts you to sleep during the implant procedure. With either type of anesthesia, you feel no pain during the actual implant procedure. Patients who’ve had the procedure often say it’s less painful than having a tooth extracted.

### Recover Time and Experience

You will likely experience some discomfort once the anesthesia wears off. You may feel discomfort at the implant site, in your chin or cheeks, or beneath your eyes. However, every patient is unique and recovery varies for each person. On average, you may feel discomfort for up to 14 days after the procedure. You will receive a prescription for antibiotics and painkillers to treat discomfort and combat swelling. At least fourteen days following the procedure, you should no longer feel any discomfort. That indicates the implant is healing as it should. Return to Toothbar for an examination if discomfort and/or swelling persists after 14 days.

### Four Tips for Reducing Pain and Swelling after Dental Implants:

1. Follow the after-care instructions provided by your dentist. Take medication  
as prescribed.  
2. Avoid spicy, hot, or crunchy foods for up to 10 days.  
3. Use a cold compress to help relieve pain or swelling.  
4. Keep follow-up appointments with your dentist.

### Possible Risks of implants

The [dental implant procedure](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/guide/dental-implants) has a 98% success rate. Complications can occur if you have uncontrolled diabetes or other disorders that affect healing. Immune system issues can cause problems as well.
