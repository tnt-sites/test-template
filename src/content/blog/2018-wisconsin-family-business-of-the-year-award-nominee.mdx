Wisconsin family businesses were honored at the fifteenth annual [Wisconsin Family Business of the Year Award](http://familybusinessaward.com/) banquet held at the Monona Terrace Community and Convention Center in Madison. Twenty three companies including Artisan Dental were nominated for the awards.

Congratulations to the worthy winners!

Don Johnson Auto Group, Rice Lake

Chapter 2, Inc., Lake Mills

Ayers Furniture Company, Ridgeway

Baker's Quality Pizza Crusts, Inc., Waukesha

Diversified Seed Producers, LLC, DeForest

Empire Screen Printing, Inc., Onalaska

Heck Capital Advisors, LLC, Rhinelander
