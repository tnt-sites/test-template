We are happy to share that [**Dr. Andersen**](/dr-nicole-andersen/) has again been named one of Madison's best dentists by her peers in **[top Dentist](http://www.usatopdentists.com/find-dentist/dentist-details.asp?dentist=nicole+m%2e+andersen&specialty=general%20dentistry&city=madison&state=wisconsin&num=1)** polls for 2016.  This is the sixth consecutive year Dr. Andersen has been named a topDentist.   Area dentists and oral healthcare specialists are asked to consider their peers years of experience, continuing education, manner with patients as well as the incorporation of new techniques and technology.

Past awards and standing in various dental academies (Academy of General Dentistry, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry) and the actual physical outcomes of their clinical work are also factored into the selection process.  Listings in [topDentists](http://www.usatopdentists.com/) can not be purchased and no payment is required to be listed.  As a result, inclusion in topDentists is considered an honor.

What is topDentists? [topDentists](http://www.usatopdentists.com/) was started in January 2007 with the intent of identifing the best dentists and oral health care specialists in the country. TopDentists is the only list of its kind, chosen by dental professionals themselves.
