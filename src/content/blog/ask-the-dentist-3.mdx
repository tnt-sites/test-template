Dr Allison ReimerIs there a right way to floss?-mouthhealthy.org&nbsp;Why yes, there is! Below are 5 Steps to a Flawless Floss:&nbsp;We know we should&nbsp;floss&nbsp;at least once a day, but not everyone knows the right way to do it. Use this step-by-step guide to find out how to properly floss your teeth and&nbsp;talk to your dentist about what types of oral care products will be most effective for you.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Break off about 18 inches of floss and wind most of it around one of your middle fingers. Wind the remaining floss around the same finger of the opposite hand. This finger will take up the floss as it becomes dirty.&nbsp;Hold the floss tightly between your thumbs and forefingers.&nbsp;Guide the floss between your teeth using a gentle rubbing motion. Never snap the floss into the gums.&nbsp;When the floss reaches the gum line, curve it into a C shape against one tooth. Gently slide it into the space between the gum and the tooth.&nbsp;Hold the floss tightly against the tooth. Gently rub the side of the tooth, moving the floss away from the gum with up and down motions. Repeat this method on the rest of your teeth. Don’t forget the back side of your last tooth.
