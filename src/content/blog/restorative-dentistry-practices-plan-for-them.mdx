Restorative dentistry is a broad dental service category that repairs damaged teeth. Knowing what restorative dentistry entails will help you decide if you need it.

## What Exactly Is Restorative Dentistry?

Restorative dentistry is any dental treatment whose primary goal is to restore or repair damaged oral components. This can cover a wide range of dental services, from fillings and bridges to implants.

Remember, though, that Austin Restorative Dentistry doesn't hide things like veneers or teeth whitening because they're viewed as just cosmetic.

## Most Common Restorative Dentistry Practices And How To Plan For Them

Although every patient's situation is unique, we typically do the following [Restorative dental procedures](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-and-oral-health/tooth-restoration). Whether your planned operation is aesthetic or restorative can save you thousands of dollars in unnecessary elective courses.

Our restorative dentists in Toothbar typically perform the following services:

• **Fillings.** Fillings made of metal amalgam or tooth-colored composite comprise the bulk of therapeutic dentistry procedures. If a tooth has become severely decayed, it can be utilized to restore its structure and prevent further damage.• **Crowns.** Dental crowns may be necessary if a tooth has sustained severe deterioration and cannot be restored with fillings (or caps, as they are sometimes referred to). As part of these repair procedures, a "cap" is made to fit snugly over the tooth and cover it completely, all the way to the gum line.

• **Bridges**. A missing tooth can be replaced with a bridge, which acts as a crown to conceal the gap. To "bridge" the void left by a missing tooth, dentists may often use a series of crowns fused and glued to the adjacent, healthy teeth on each side of the space.

• **Bonding**. Bonding is the most common method used to repair broken or chipped teeth. Bonding can effectively replace decayed or damaged enamel by shaping and adhering nanocomposites to the tooth's surface.

• **Implants**. [Dental implants](https://www.forbes.com/health/body/dental-implants-guide/) are an option to consider when the damage to teeth is severe. Here, natural teeth are surgically removed, and artificial ones are put in their place. A metal "root" or "post" bonds to your jawbone and serves as an anchor for these.

## Dental Procedures: What To Plan Beforehand

Although restorative dentistry can help with many dental problems, it's vital to have all your questions answered before making an appointment.

Always ask your Downtown Austin Dentist many questions so you may be prepared for your meeting and end up with the best solution for your teeth. You should also check with your insurance company to ensure that any proposed procedures are covered in the future.

This will protect you from unexpected fees for operations classified as cosmetic rather than medically necessary. There is no need to suffer financially or physically from dental problems if you plan for your care by reading up on available options and talking to your dentist.
