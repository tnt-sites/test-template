As you get prepared for your [dental implant](/dental-implants/) consultation, you may be feeling a mix of nervous excitement. You'll be able to see your candidacy for this treatment, but it's important you do your own learning. By asking the right questions, you can feel confident choosing your provider and understand the process from start to finish. Here are three that you can get your list started with.

## Do You Have Experience with Dental Implants?

While your dentist will be assessing your oral and overall health, it's important you know more about them as well. By inquiring about their experience, you can get a better sense of what to expect. Asking for before-and-after photos can help give you a visual!

You can also bring up whether they do the placement surgery themselves or through a local specialist. Regardless, you can feel confident knowing you stay with a team you trust or be in expert care every step of the way.

## Will I Need Preliminary Treatments?

Many people are great candidates for implants right away, but just as many will need some sort of preliminary treatment. Based on what your dentist finds in your oral health, they may need to treat gum disease first or give a bone graft to reinforce your jawbone.

Some may imagine that these are extra steps, but don’t let them get you disheartened! They will help you avoid complications down the line. Instead, see them as building blocks to a full smile that you feel confident in.

## How Can I Pay for My Dental Implants?

After all is said and done, you may be wondering what the overall cost will be. While implants are generally more expensive than other tooth replacement options, their benefits and longevity do make them a worthwhile investment. Regardless, it's normal to still be worried about the price.

To clear the air, simply ask about it! You may be able to learn more about whether your dentist takes your [dental insurance](/insurances-financial-options/) and how your benefits can be used to ensure you get the most out of them. If you don’t have it, then you can ask if they have a membership plan. These often have discounts on various treatments, but your provider can let you know more about whether it’s a good choice for you.

In the end, any question is a great one to ask! Start writing a list and bring it with you to your dental implant consultation; that way, you can feel confident deciding for your oral health.
