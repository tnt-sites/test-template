## What are Dental Crowns?

A dental crown is a dental restoration used to replace a tooth when it is missing, damaged, or decayed.

Crowns are made of different materials, including resin, metal, or porcelain.

They are used to improve the appearance of your smile, cover the tooth, and provide additional support and protection for the tooth's surface.

The goal of dental crowns is to restore the shape and function of [your damaged tooth](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5086281/).

### Types of Dental Crowns

There are a few different dental crowns, and some are better suited to certain situations. Traditional dental crown - A conventional crown is made of porcelain and is the most common type.

Traditional crowns are usually used to restore a single tooth, cover considerable damage to a tooth, or support a severely decayed tooth.

Composite resin crowns are hybrid crowns made of porcelain, porcelain-coated metal, and composite resin.

They're a great choice if you are allergic to metal. Composite resin crowns are also resistant to staining, staining, and cracking.

Metal crowns are durable metal materials like gold, stainless steel, or nickel.

They're an excellent choice for strengthening your teeth due to wear or injury and are generally more durable than porcelain crowns and ceramic crowns.

Ceramic crowns are made of a complex, durable material similar to porcelain.

They're an excellent option for patients who don't want to restore their front teeth. Zirconia crowns are made of a ceramic-like material of zirconium oxide.

Because zirconia crowns don't require any metal or porcelain in the restoration, they're also an excellent option for patients who are allergic or sensitive to metals. Zirconia crowns are translucent or opaque and feature a wide range of colors.

These crowns are made of a ceramic-like material resistant to stains, staining, and cracking.
