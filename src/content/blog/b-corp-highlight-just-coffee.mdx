Get to know fellow Madison B Corp - Just Coffee.

Artisan Dental is proud to be among companies with a similar vision of using business as a force for good. We applaud [Just Coffee](http://justcoffee.coop/), right here in Madison, for the great job that they are doing!

"*We became a B-Corp because we are proud of the work we do and have always felt the term "fair trade' only captures one aspect of how we operate. We are committed to excellence from bean to cup​ and are excited to be a part of a movement of other businesses who are also committed to that same level of excellence in their fields. We strive to do right by every person, place, ​and thing that​ our business comes into contact with and we believe this leads to better coffee in your cup.*"

Just Coffee - [B Corp Impact Report](https://www.bcorporation.net/community/just-coffee-cooperative)

“When we heard about the B Corp certification, it was like a lightbulb going off. It addressed all of the core things that we were trying to do.”
