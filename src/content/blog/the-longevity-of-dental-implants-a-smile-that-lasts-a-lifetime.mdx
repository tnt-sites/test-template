## The Science Behind Dental Implants

Dental implants are prosthetic tooth roots made of biocompatible materials, usually titanium. They are surgically placed in the jawbone to provide a strong and stable foundation for artificial teeth. This process, known as osseointegration, allows the implant to fuse with the bone, creating a solid anchor for crowns, bridges, or dentures. As a result, dental implants mimic the look and feel of natural teeth better than any other tooth replacement option.

## Longevity of Dental Implants

Implant Quality: At North County Cosmetic and Implant Dentistry, we use only the highest-quality implants, designed to withstand the rigors of daily use.

- Oral Hygiene: Maintaining good oral hygiene through regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups is crucial. Proper care helps prevent infections and gum diseases that can jeopardize the implant's stability.

- Lifestyle and Habits: Avoiding habits like smoking and excessive alcohol consumption, which can harm oral health, is essential for implant longevity.

- Bone Health: The density and health of your jawbone are vital for the success of dental implants. Patients with good bone quality have a higher likelihood of long-lasting implants.
