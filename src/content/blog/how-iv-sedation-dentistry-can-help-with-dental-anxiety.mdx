Going to the dentist can be particularly tough if you have dental anxiety, a low pain tolerance, severe tooth sensitivity, or a mobility condition. Many individuals who face these difficulties or who have a general aversion to going to the dentist can choose [IV sedation dentistry](/vista-ca/dental-anesthesia/) for their sessions. Continue reading to learn more about how IV sedation can help you relax and calm down during your next dental checkup or treatment.

## What is IV sedation dentistry?

Almost anyone who has had a dental filling would know about local anesthetic. These anesthetic injections will numb the treatment region to prevent discomfort during the procedure. For many patients, however, being comfortable will require more than an anesthetic alone. Receiving anesthetic shots might be distressing for patients with a general phobia of needles. Also, some have a weak gag response and are afraid of choking during treatment.

**IV sedation dentistry** is beneficial to patients in all of these scenarios. Sedation help patients stay calm before, during, and after their treatment, while the anesthetic inhibits pain signals from reaching the brain. Various sedative methods are available, and the dentist will work with the patient to evaluate which one is best for their condition and degree of anxiety.

### How does it work?

A tiny needle will be inserted into a vein on the patient’s hand or arm during the dental appointment. The needle is attached to an intravenous catheter, which allows the sedative to enter the circulation immediately. If the patient is scared of needles, the dentist can administer a numbing lotion to the region that the needle will puncture. However, not all patients are able to remain with the IV. The medication is given, and the needle is removed in this situation.

IV sedation creates a twilight state in which the patient is awake but extremely relaxed. Patients will not be unconscious and can respond to prompts. On the other hand, the process will seem to speed by, and time will fly past. For people who have severe dental phobia, IV sedation is the best option.

In both cases, the level of conscious sedation is the same. The dentist will regularly monitor the patient’s pulse and oxygen levels. Afterward, the patient’s blood pressure will be measured. Patients may also feel sleepy, so it is advisable to arrange for transportation home. It is also a good idea to take the entire day off for adequate rest.

### Who needs IV sedation dentistry?

Patients will need to speak with the dentist to know if _IV sedation dentistry_ will be recommended. However, certain categories of people benefit the most from dental sedation, including:

    - Patients who have a high level of dental sensitivity or a low pain threshold

    - Patients who have experienced trauma as a result of previous bad dental operations

    - Children who are frightened of dental procedures and find it difficult to sit still

    - Individuals who are apprehensive about sitting in a dentist's chair for a long period. This is particularly useful for more extensive treatments such as implant installation or full mouth reconstruction.

## Consider sedation dentistry

Patients can transform their dental experience with IV sedation dentistry. Even if you have had unpleasant or bad dental procedures in the past, going through the process again with a fresh mindset will reduce your overall anxiety. You can proceed with confidence, knowing that dental treatment may be relaxing and even enjoyable.
