## How Your Teeth Become Discolored

Your teeth perform complex functions and are often [discolored](https://www.healthline.com/health/tooth-discoloration) via two broad mechanisms.

The types of foods you love, beverages, and tar from cigarette smoke often stain as a form of extrinsic discoloration. You may have noticed that your teeth appear stained after certain meals, coffee, or red wine. Proper oral hygiene can manage discoloration from extrinsic sources. Proper oral hygiene involves proper and periodic brushing with a whitening toothpaste.

[Tooth discoloration](https://www.no1braces.co.uk/latest-news/5-benefits-of-having-whiter-teeth/) can also be from within the tooth. As you grow older, your teeth tend to get discolored. Certain medications like tetracycline antibiotics, infections, trauma to the teeth, and some childhood illnesses can also lead to discoloration. Water with high fluoride concentrations can also discolor your teeth. All these forms of intrinsic discoloration may necessitate that you seek professional teeth whitening services from a dentist.
