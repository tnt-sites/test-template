Cosmetic dentistry is a dental procedure that emphasizes enhancing your smile's attractiveness. They are designed to improve your teeth' balance, symmetry, and aesthetics.

However, the following steps won't necessarily work for everyone. Toothbar, an Austin cosmetic Dentist, can help you determine which technique will improve your smile the most.

Here are five common types of Cosmetic dentistry procedures that our Dentist in downtown Austin will outline for you before you undertake the procedure

### 1. [Teeth Whitening](/austin-teeth-whitening/)

Dark-colored foods and beverages (including coffee, tea, and berries) can cause tooth stains over time. Teeth whitening, also known as teeth bleaching, can assist in removing the surface stains that develop naturally.

The teeth are carefully cleansed to remove plaque, tartar, and food particles from the crevices. Cosmetic dentists may employ bleaching solutions like hydrogen peroxide to whiten teeth depending on the severity of discoloration.

Our Dentist in downtown Austin offers teeth whitening, one of the most sought-after cosmetic dental operations. As a result, you will get your smile back to its former glory without compromising your financial situation or oral health. Additionally, whitening products are readily available in retail outlets for simple at-home whitening.

### 2. [Inlays and Onlays](/cavities-and-fillings/)

Another name for inlays and Onlays is indirect fillings. They are created in a dental lab and are made of gold, porcelain, or composite materials. They are applied when there is either insufficient dental structure or mild to moderate decay on a tooth.

When the material is bonded inside the center of a tooth, the filling is referred to as an inlay, provided the [cusps](https://www.newmouth.com/oral-health/fractured-cusp/) are unharmed. However, if the cusp or a larger tooth section is broken, your dentist might use an Onlay to cover the tooth completely.

### 3. [Dental Veneers](/austin-dental-veneers/)

Dental veneers are tiny, individualized shells composed of composite or dental porcelain that overlay the front teeth. They can fix various cosmetic dentistry issues, such as gaps between teeth, chipping, stains, cracks, and crooked teeth. These tiny covers are glued to the teeth' front surfaces, altering the teeth' shape and color and preventing further decay or deterioration.

### 4. [Dental Implants](/dental-implants/)

Numerous Americans lose their teeth, most frequently due to tooth decay. [According to CDC reports](https://www.cdc.gov/oralhealth/fast-facts/cavities/), more than half (57%) of adolescents between the ages of 12 and 19 have tooth decay in one or more of their permanent teeth. They may result in pain and infections that make eating, speaking, playing, or learning difficulties.

The Toothbar, an Austin Cosmetic Dentist, works to raise awareness of the value of preventing and treating cavities in the Austin community. Our Dentist in downtown Austin can also replace lost or damaged teeth using dental implants. Artificial tooth roots made of titanium are put into the bone socket where a tooth once existed. The tiny titanium screw acts as a support for a new crown. Patients must also maintain strict oral hygiene during and after the implant treatment to remove plaque and food particles from the region.

### 5. [Dental Bonding](/austin-cosmetic-bonding/)

Discolored, irregularly shaped, or chipped teeth can negatively affect your confidence. To fix this, our Dentist in downtown Austin will apply a tooth-colored, putty-like resin that effectively conceals the decayed tooth's damage and creates the illusion of a brand-new, healthy tooth. It is then polished and sculpted to match the neighboring teeth and hardened with a laser or ultraviolet light.
