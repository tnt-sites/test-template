Patients in the area of Springfield, MA who are seeking a removable and economical solution for replacing teeth are encouraged to ask our team about the advantages of using [partial dentures](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/missing-teeth.html#denture).

## The Partial Denture

The partial denture is a popular choice for patients who want a simple to use solution that restores function and beauty to their smile, but will not empty the wallet. Additionally, many patients who have dental insurance may also be able to reduce the overall cost of their denture by applying their benefits. Partial dentures are made with a framework of acrylic and metal that snaps into the dental arch, utilizing the surrounding teeth within the arch for support. The dentists of Taylor Street Dental may suggest this restoration for someone who wants an immediate, economical solution while deciding on more permanent options possible down the road, such as a dental bridge or a dental implant.

## Obtaining a Partial Denture

First, patients need to consult with their dental team to determine if the partial denture is the right solution for their smile. Many find that they are a viable candidate and that the partial denture is the most affordable option available. Then, impressions are made and sent to a dental laboratory where the new restoration is completed. Patients return to the dental office to have their partial denture placed and to learn about care and use.
