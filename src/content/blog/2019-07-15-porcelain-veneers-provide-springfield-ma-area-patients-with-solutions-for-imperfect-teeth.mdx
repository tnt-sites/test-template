## Understanding the purpose of veneers

Veneers are essentially very thin facings of porcelain that are fabricated to match a patient’s existing teeth. These are bonded over the front of a tooth to change its appearance and disguise common aesthetic concerns, including:

*   Gapped teeth
*   Misaligned teeth
*   Crooked teeth
*   Short teeth
*   Chipped or cracked teeth
*   Permanently stained teeth

With porcelain veneers, the smile can be transformed quickly and easily without the need for extensive dental work.

## Who is a candidate for porcelain veneers?

Porcelain veneers are a wonderful option for patients who want to quickly and easily disguise problems that may appear on the front of the smile. These teeth, called the anterior teeth, are the first teeth others see when an individual smiles at others. With porcelain veneers, patients can dramatically enhance their smile or address subtle issues that may otherwise detract from their appearance. Determining candidacy for porcelain veneers starts with a consultation appointment with our team, in which they will be evaluated by our team to decide if veneers are the best option for patients.
