We all understand the significance of proper [oral care](http://www.thetoothbar.com/), but it may be challenging to develop the habit. Helping your kid develop excellent oral habits early will make it simpler for them to retain such practices as they grow. Educating your kid now will ensure that their smile remains bright and healthy in the future.

Dental [cavities](/cavities-and-fillings/) are the most prevalent sickness in kids, and studies reveal that over 40 percent of kids in the US have cavities. How can you get your young kids started on the pathway to good oral hygiene for life? Our professionals at [Austin Preventative Dentistry](/preventive-dentistry/) care about your children's dental health. We've put up a few pointers to help you get started instilling good oral practices in your kids.

#### **(1) Begin Early**

The earlier you can establish a proper dental habit for your child, the better. Toddlers have all 20 primary teeth by three years and can begin using fluoride toothpaste. Establishing a pleasant experience with oral hygiene at an early age will enable your child to develop such practices as they grow.

#### **(2) Set a Good Example**

Kids learn by seeing and imitating their parents. Having good oral habits yourself is usually the most significant thing you could do to assist your child in developing them. Your youngster will be inspired by your dedication to proper oral hygiene and will want to emulate it. Demonstrate to your youngster how to floss, brush, and use mouthwash. Simulating these habits has a [long-term impact](https://psychcentral.com/news/2018/05/27/modeling-behavior-for-children-has-long-lasting-effects#1) on children.

#### **(3) Make It Simple**

Add a stepping stool to the bathroom sink and put toothbrushes, toothpaste, and floss within reach to make it easier for your kid to develop excellent dental habits. The less difficulty your child faces in maintaining such behaviors, the simpler it'll be for them to do so. If flossing or brushing their teeth seems like a chore, they will less likely continue doing so.

#### **(4) Make Use of Child-Friendly Oral Care Products**

Oral products designed particularly for kids are the most significant way to begin educating your child about healthy dental practices. These products frequently contain cartoon characters, vivid colors, and pleasant flavors that keep the dental routine enjoyable. Some toothbrushes can light up and "speak" to the kid, encouraging them to clean their teeth for the appropriate length of time and maintaining a positive experience.

#### **(5) Have a Good Time at the Dentist**

Visiting the [dentist](https://www.cdc.gov/oralhealth/basics/childrens-oral-health/index.html) may be a terrifying experience for a kid, so having an excellent first experience with a dentist can help relieve your child's fear.
