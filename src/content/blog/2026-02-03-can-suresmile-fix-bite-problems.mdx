If you’re dealing with bite issues like an overbite, underbite, or crossbite, you may be wondering whether clear aligners can truly correct the problem. [SureSmile](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/orthodontics.html) is an advanced orthodontic system designed to address both cosmetic alignment concerns and functional bite issues. But how effective is it?

## What is SureSmile?

SureSmile is a clear aligner system that uses digital imaging and precise treatment planning to move teeth into better alignment. Similar to other clear aligner brands, SureSmile creates a customized series of trays that gradually shift your teeth over time.

Using 3D scans and advanced software, your dentist or orthodontist maps out each stage of movement to improve both the appearance and function of your smile.

## What Types of Bite Problems Can It Treat?

SureSmile can correct many common bite issues, including:

*   Overbite.
*   Underbite.
*   Crossbite.
*   Open bite.
*   Mild to moderate crowding or spacing.

By applying controlled pressure to specific teeth, aligners help reposition them into healthier alignment. In many cases, attachments (small, tooth-colored bumps) are added to improve grip and enhance movement precision.

## How Effective Is It?

The effectiveness of SureSmile depends on the complexity of your case and your commitment to wearing the aligners as directed. Most patients need to wear their aligners 20–22 hours per day to stay on track.

For mild to moderate bite issues, SureSmile can be highly effective. More complex or severe bite problems may require traditional braces or a combination of treatments. Your provider will evaluate your jaw alignment, tooth position, and overall oral health before recommending the best option.

## Benefits of Using Clear Aligners for Bite Correction

One of the biggest advantages of SureSmile is its discreet appearance. The aligners are clear and removable, allowing you to eat, brush, and floss normally. This makes it easier to maintain good oral hygiene compared to traditional braces.

Additionally, digital treatment planning allows for precise and predictable results. You’ll often be able to see a simulation of your projected outcome before starting treatment.

## Is SureSmile Right for You?

If you’re experiencing jaw discomfort, uneven tooth wear, difficulty chewing, or noticeable misalignment, a bite issue could be the cause. Scheduling a consultation is the best way to determine whether SureSmile can correct your specific concerns.

Yes, SureSmile can fix many bite problems, especially mild to moderate cases. With advanced digital planning and consistent wear, it can improve both the function and appearance of your smile. However, the best treatment option depends on your individual needs, so professional evaluation is essential for achieving long-lasting results.
