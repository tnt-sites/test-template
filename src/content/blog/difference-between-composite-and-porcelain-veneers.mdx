A cosmetic dentist can provide you with a variety of treatment options to restore your smile, allowing you to regain your self-confidence. If you have stained, chipped or cracked teeth, veneers might be the best thing to correct those issues. The custom-made shells are available in two types of material: composite and porcelain. Comparing the differences between them can help you decide which is right for you.

## What Are Veneers?

If you are curious about veneers, you might have questions about a cosmetic dentist near me to learn more. [Dental veneers](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23522-dental-veneers) are coverings for the front of the teeth to hide any imperfections such as cracks, chips, discoloration and more. They are custom-designed to fit the shape and size of your teeth to make them more attractive. They can also bring your pearly whites to a better state to prevent further damage.

Only certain people are good candidates for veneers. To be considered, you must have good oral hygiene and no serious issues involving gum disease or cavities. However, if you have those problems and address them first, you may be a good candidate for veneer treatment later.

There are two different types of veneers you can choose from: composite and porcelain. Our Austin cosmetic dentist at Toothbar can discuss your options and determine which type is more appropriate for you.

## How Do the Different Types of Veneers Compare?

The two types of veneers, composite and porcelain, share similarities. Both of them are used to correct a number of aesthetic imperfections in the teeth. However, they also have some differences that are worth comparing. Composite veneers are cheaper and made of a resin material. They’re good for covering up minor cosmetic dental issues and last around two to five years.

[Porcelain veneers](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/veneers) are thin shells that are affixed to the outside of the teeth. They are more natural-looking, durable and last for about 10 to 15 years, making them the more expensive option. However, because they last longer than their composite counterparts, for some, they might be a better investment.

## What Is the Process of Veneer Placement?

If you come to Toothbar for veneer treatment, the first thing the dentist will do is to remove a small amount of enamel to prepare your teeth. Then, they will take impressions so that a technician can create your custom veneers if you choose the porcelain type. If you choose composite, this step isn’t necessary.

When your veneers are ready for placement, the dentist will check their color and shape and ensure they fit before adhering them to your teeth. Once they’re in place, the dentist will check your bite and make adjustments as needed. After your procedure, you can resume your regular daily routine and eat and drink once the anesthesia has worn off.
