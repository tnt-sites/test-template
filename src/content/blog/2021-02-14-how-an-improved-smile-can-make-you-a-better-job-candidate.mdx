Finding a job during difficult times can make it more important than ever to make a positive, lasting first impression to potential opportunities. The first impression often seen by others is the smile. It is beneficial for you to outshine your competition by having an attractive smile and conducting yourself with confidence during your job interview. The best way to do this is to work with a dentist who provides cosmetic dentistry solutions to enhance the smile and restore self-confidence. A popular treatment to improve the appearance of the smile is the use of [porcelain veneers](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/cosmetic-dentistry.html#veneer).

## What is a porcelain veneer?

Porcelain veneers are aesthetic restorations that are custom-made for a patient. They are thin shells of porcelain that the dentist can bond onto the front of a natural tooth to disguise imperfections. This method of enhancing the smile is fast and easy, and with proper care, porcelain veneers can last many years before they need to be replaced.

## What can I expect with obtaining porcelain veneers?

First, patients are welcome to contact the team at Taylor Street Dental in Springfield, MA to find out if they are a viable candidate for porcelain veneers. Once patients decide to move forward with these restorations, the teeth being covered are prepared with the removal of a thin portion of natural enamel on the front of the tooth. Impressions are made and used to create the porcelain facings. The dentist ensures that the shape, size, and color of the veneer allows for aesthetic flow when placed. The porcelain veneer is bonded in place, and the process is complete!

## What can porcelain veneers disguise?

_Dental veneers are incredibly versatile as they can be used for covering a wide range of problems, including teeth that are:_

*   Unusually shaped
*   Permanently stained
*   Cracked or chipped
*   Spaced apart
*   Unusually short
*   Crooked
