## Benefits of Invisalign

The [benefits of Invisalign over braces](https://womenfitnessmag.com/the-benefits-of-invisalign/) are numerous. It is a comfortable, safe, flexible, and invisible approach to achieving straighter teeth. Fewer orthodontist visits are required, and the removable aligners allow you to enjoy all your favorite foods. In addition, treatment with Invisalign is often [faster than braces](https://dentalhealthsociety.com/invisalign/invisalign-vs-braces-which-is-faster/).

A beautiful smile is not only an aesthetic choice, however. Misaligned teeth can [cause health problems](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-and-oral-health/crooked-teeth). Their maintenance is more challenging, resulting in potential tooth decay or gum disease. Approximately 50% of U.S. adults over the age of 29 have some [indication of gum disease](https://www.cdc.gov/oralhealth/basics/adult-oral-health/index.html). Proper oral health remains essential, as “uncontrolled chronic disease is linked to [poor oral health](https://www.dshs.state.tx.us/dental/pdf/Adults-and-Oral-Health-Brief.pdf) outcomes and vice versa.”

The stigma of an imperfect smile can lead to emotional distress, and the physical realities associated with oral health deserve attention.
