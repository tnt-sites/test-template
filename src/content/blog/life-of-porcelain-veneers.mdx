Tooth decay and cavities constitute one of the prevalent medical problems that lead to hospital visits for adults, teenagers, and children. A toothache, increased sensitivity, visible holes, discoloration, and pain when biting, drinking, or eating cold or hot food comprise cavities' manifestations.

Tooth decay usually occurs due to the proliferation of specific strains of bacteria in the mouth, leading to plaque formation around teeth. The plaque acts as a biofilm that promotes more bacterial growth and de-mineralization of teeth leading to rotting and tooth loss. Individuals at high risk of exhibiting tooth decay are neonates, and older adults because of receding gums, insufficient fluoride intake, and decreased saliva levels.

### How Does One Extend The Life Of A Freshly Installed Porcelain Veneer?

Although porcelain veneers are durable, it is essential to know how to extend their life through several maintenance tips. Firstly, one should maintain good oral hygiene by brushing at least two times a day, aside from flossing once daily. Secondly, use a toothbrush with soft bristles to avoid damaging the veneer. One should avoid using toothpaste with abrasives ingredients and should settle for standard fluoride-containing pastes. Thirdly, you should prevent gum inflammation and ensure you maintain regular visits to the dentist to repair any damages.

Avoiding food like soda and coffee that stain teeth would prolong the life of your porcelain veneer. Additionally, one should do away with certain habits like smoking, clenching teeth, and unnecessary chewing on hard things.
