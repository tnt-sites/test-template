However, getting a bright white smile is quite complicated nowadays since there are a lot of options available. So, how can people in the Littleton area effectively whiten their smiles? Continue reading below!

##### Will using mouthwash work?

Although there are rinses that promise to have whitening agents, know that using the said product would not lift off stains. But for the [wellness of oral health](https://www.cdc.gov/oralhealth/basics/adult-oral-health/tips.html), patients are advised to rinse with an antibacterial mouthwash to protect the teeth against bacteria buildups.

##### How about pens and over the counter gels?

Products with peroxide content are indeed more effective than the ones without it; however, there are products with the said ingredient that cannot provide favorable results. If the agents do not come into contact with the teeth long enough, no outcome can be attained.

##### Can toothpaste help?

Depending on the ingredients, using whitening toothpaste could work at a certain degree. However, just like pens and gels, it needs to stay in contact with the teeth long enough to work effectively.

##### What if patients choose the in-office teeth whitening approach?

Of all the other means of having the teeth whitened, the one performed by the dentist is the most effective. Aside from the expertise of a professional, the agents used are also in high concentrations. As a result, ideal and quick results can be attained by the patient.

With the supervision of a dentist, patients can expect that hassles like irritations and sensitivity can be avoided as much as possible.
