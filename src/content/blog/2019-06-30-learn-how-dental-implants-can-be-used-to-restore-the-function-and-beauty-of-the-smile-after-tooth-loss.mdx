[Dental implants](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/dental-implants.html) are one of many choices available to patients in the Springfield, MA area who are seeking tooth replacement. These restorations are made of titanium metal, and the small posts are strategically placed into the bone of the jaw and restored over the top with crowns, bridges, or dentures. A dental implant is a smart choice for patients who are seeking permanent tooth replacement.

## How does a dental implant work?

The dental implant is a post that essentially functions as a tooth root. It is deeply seated into the bone of the jaw during oral surgery. This process stimulates osseointegration, which naturally grows bone around the implant to hold it firmly in place. The abutment on the top of the dental implant can be restored in many different manners. It can be restored with a dental crown to replace a single tooth, or a bridge or denture to replace many teeth. These versatile restorations are often chosen because of their strength and longevity.

## What re the benefits of dental implants?

_Dental implants are:_

*   Long-lasting
*   Natural-looking
*   Biocompatible
*   Strong and durable
*   Versatile
*   Easy to care for

## Who is a candidate for dental implants?

Any patient who has lost one or more teeth may be educated on the advantages of dental implants, but they may not always be a proper candidate. Patients with sufficient bone of the jaw are required, as dental implantation may be unsuccessful if there is not enough bone present for placement. Additionally, patients should have a smile free from periodontal disease and dental decay before they place the restoration, as these can also impact the success of placement. If patients do not have enough bone available for placement but are serious about placing dental implants, they may ask the team of Taylor Street Dental about the advantages of undergoing bone grafting in preparation for these restorations.
