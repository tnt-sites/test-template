We're not only a dental office. We are also a defender of the environment by purchasing 100% of our electricity through [MG&E Greener Tomorrow](https://www.mge.com/environment/green-power/gpt/) program. 100% of our total carbon emissions are offset through our partnership with fellow certified B corporation Native Energy. That's part of what makes us a BCorporation.

Embrace a better way of doing business.
