As you get used to your [Invisalign](/invisalign/) trays, you might have built a solid routine to follow at home. You’ve only become more effective and quicker at cleaning them and your teeth. That said, if you have a trip coming up, you might wonder whether your travels will affect your treatment. To help you avoid trouble, here are three tips you should keep in mind.

## Pack Your Dental Essentials & Extra Trays

When making your checklist, you might have several outfits planned. You’d want to look your best after all! But don’t forget about your dental essentials. Double-check whether you packed your travel toothbrush, floss, mini toothpaste, and mouthwash. That way, you can keep your teeth and gums free from plaque. Better yet, you’ll also keep your smile strong, so you avoid a dental emergency.

It’s also a good idea to look ahead on your Invisalign journey. You likely already know that you have to swap over to a new set of trays every week or so. If your trip lands on the days that you need to switch, then make sure you bring the right number of aligners. This will ensure you don’t linger on a pair for too long and cause delays.

## Keep Bottles of Water Handy

Whether you’re driving or flying, the restrooms you’ll have access to might not be up to your cleanliness standards. It’s easy to be grossed out by them and not want to use the sink. A great way around this is to have a couple of bottles of water with you. You can use them to wet your toothbrush and rinse your mouth after eating. So, you feel confident cleaning your mouth and staying hydrated.

## Don’t Slack on Your Aligner Routine

Once you reach your destination, you might want to sit back and relax without a care in the world. While you do deserve to wind down, don’t forget about your Invisalign trays. You should make sure you keep wearing them for 22 hours every day. That way, you avoid delaying your results. If you find you have trouble remembering, consider setting alarms and reminders. They will help your treatment stay on track without your aligners getting in the way of your trip.
