## What are the benefits of dental implants?

[Dental implants](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/blog/#) have a variety of advantages for patients to consider when thinking about their tooth replacement options. _Below are just a few of the many benefits that our team may mention during one's consultation visit and initial examination:_

*   **Dental implants will likely last for several years with proper care**. Longevity is one of the main reasons why patients choose dental implants. When patients take care of their smiles, they will enjoy their implants for a lifetime!
*   **Dental implants have an excellent success rate**. Because our team will evaluate you to ensure you are a good candidate for dental implants, the success rate of these restorations is extremely high.
*   **Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth**. Dental implants are placed into the bone of your jaw, which stimulates bone growth through a process known as osseointegration. This provides a strong and sturdy foundation for the implant to allow it to function better than dentures or bridges.
*   **Dental implants restore eating and speaking abilities**. Depending on the area where the natural tooth was lost, patients may experience changes in their abilities to speak or chew properly. Dental implants restore this ability to save patients the embarrassment and challenges that come with tooth loss.
*   **Dental implants maintain better jawbone health**. Jawbone health is improved with dental implants due to the bone growth that occurs with their placement. This prevents shrinking of the jawbone, which can cause adjoining teeth to shift or become loose.
