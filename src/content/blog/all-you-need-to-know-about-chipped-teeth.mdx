One of the hardest components of your body is the enamel, which is the hard outer covering of your teeth. It has limits, which is why if your teeth are exposed to extreme erosion or a strong hit, they may break. This may result in a rough tooth surface, which can negatively impact the appearance of your smile and demands immediate treatment. ## Why Do You Suffer From Chipped Or Cracked Tooth?

[Chipped teeth](https://www.sensodyne.com/en-gb/oral-health-tips/chipped-tooth/) can be caused by many factors. For your convenience, your Littleton dentist has produced a list of them.

Hard things, such as pencils, pens, hard candies, or ice, should not be chewed.

- Car accident
- Continuous teeth grinding or bruxism
- Taking part in physical activity without a mouthguard

## Which Teeth Are More At Risk?

If your teeth are weak, they are obviously at risk. Chipping is thought to be more likely in the second lower molar and teeth with large fillings. This happens because the second lower tooth is more sensitive to chewing pressure. You can consult your dentist in Littleton for additional information.

## What Are The Warning Signs Of Chipped Teeth?

Minor tooth chipping that isn't evident from the front of the mouth may go unnoticed. Periodontal inflammation surrounding the chipped tooth, pain while biting, and an uneven surface while running your tongue over your teeth, to mention a few, are all common indicators.

## What Are The Risk Factors For Chipped Teeth?

- Weaker teeth are regarded to be more susceptible to chipping than stronger teeth. Tooth decay and cavities can weaken your teeth by destroying your enamel, and large fillings can make them much weaker. [Teeth grinding can wear down your enamel](https://www.healthline.com/health/enamel-erosion), exposing your teeth, and acidic foods like fruit juices, coffee, and spicy foods can wear down your enamel, exposing your teeth. This is why it's critical to visit your Littleton dentist for a full examination every six months.
- Acid reflux and heartburn are two digestive illnesses that cause stomach acid to pour back into your mouth, damaging the enamel. Excessive alcohol consumption and eating disorders can result in frequent vomiting, which produces acid that can wear away at your enamel. If you have any of these issues, you should see your dentist in Littleton right away.
- Too much sugar can cause germs to develop in your mouth, and these bacteria can eat away at your enamel. As you become older, your enamel starts to erode. As a result, those over 50 are more prone to suffer from compromised enamel.

## How Will This Condition Be Diagnosed?

After a short inspection of your mouth, your dental care professional will be able to detect a chipped tooth. Your symptoms may be evaluated, and questions about the events that led to the chipping may be asked.
