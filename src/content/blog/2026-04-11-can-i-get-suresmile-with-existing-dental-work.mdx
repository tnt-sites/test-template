A popular orthodontic treatment that many people are choosing is [SureSmile](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/orthodontics.html#sure). You will need to wear thin trays for 22 hours every day to get the straighter teeth you want. This service is often effective, but you might be wondering whether it's a good fit for you if you have an existing restoration, like a crown, bridge, or veneers. Here's what you need to know and how clear aligners may still be possible.

## Should I Be Worried About My Crown with SureSmile?

Crowns are like caps for a tooth to give back its function and appearance, especially if it was severely damaged or decayed. Since it’s placed on a single structure, it typically doesn’t usually cause problems for a SureSmile treatment.

The trays you’ll get will be customized to your grin, so the fit isn’t an issue. Rather, it’s the pressure that may be worrying. Your provider will want to make sure that your restoration is secure to ensure nothing goes awry.

## How Well Does SureSmile Handle Veneers?

Veneers are thin, porcelain shells that are bonded to the front-facing parts of teeth to give a grin a more uniform and aesthetic look. Just like the point above, clear aligners can fare well against this cosmetic treatment.

Your dentist will make sure they are securely on and won’t budge. That said, you may need to have them adjusted once you finish orthodontics. Doing so can help make sure your smile stays balanced and gorgeous.

## Can I Straighten My Teeth with a Bridge?

A bridge is a tooth replacement that uses two teeth adjacent to the space to hold it up. Since this restoration relies on two things, it may cause some trouble for SureSmile. The structures supporting it won’t be able to move independently, and it’s not recommended to shift them as a unit. Instead, your treatment will need to be adjusted to avoid putting pressure on it.

## So, Can I Still Get SureSmile?

In the end, it’s still possible that you can get SureSmile if you have a restoration like a crown, bridge, or veneers. Your dentist will be able to let you know more about whether it’s a good choice for you through a consultation. That way, you feel confident knowing you will have a smooth journey to a straighter grin.
