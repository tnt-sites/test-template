Artisan Dental Featured in Brava Magazine Article [“Purpose before Profit”](http://bravamagazine.com/purpose-before-profit/)

A great piece written by Shelby Deering, it discusses the shift of people having a greater sense of consciousness around their choices, paying attention to the things that really matter and making sure that the companies that they choose to work for and do business with align with their personal ethics.

“The nationwide movement has found its way to Madison, changing corporations and small businesses alike and turning them into “Firms of Endearment,” coined by the 2003 book of the same name. Meet three local businesses that are embracing conscious capitalism, doing good while reaping good-for-business benefits.”

We’re honored to be featured along with 2 other amazing companies; [Envision IT](https://envisionitllc.com/) and [Ideas that Evoke](http://www.ideasthatevoke.com/).
