## What Is Jaw Botox?

Jaw Botox involves the precise injection of botulinum toxin into the masseter muscles - the large muscles along the jawline responsible for chewing and clenching. Overworked or enlarged masseter muscles can cause:

- TMJ (temporomandibular joint) pain
- Chronic teeth grinding (bruxism)
- Headaches or facial tension
- A widened or square jaw appearance

By relaxing these muscles, Botox provides both functional relief and aesthetic reshaping, making it a versatile option for patients who want more than a traditional dental solution.

## Therapeutic Benefits: Relief from Pain and Tension

Beyond aesthetics, many patients seek jaw Botox because of chronic discomfort. The Toothbar team frequently sees patients who grind their teeth at night or clench during stressful workdays. Left untreated, these habits can lead to:

- Worn-down enamel
- Cracked or fractured teeth
- Migraines and tension headaches
- TMJ disorders

By reducing the strength of the masseter muscles, Botox helps break the cycle of grinding and clenching, protecting your teeth while reducing pain. For patients who’ve tried night guards but still wake up sore, jaw Botox can be a game-changing option.
