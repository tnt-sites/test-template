A [natural smile makeover](/vista-ca/smile-makeover/) can repair issues and address specific concerns for individuals who are unhappy with the condition of their teeth. Having a beautiful smile can impact confidence, professionally and personally. It makes a positive first impression and can become one of a person’s most remembered attributes. People with a confident, bright smile are often perceived as more happy, trustworthy and intelligent.

## Dental reconstruction vs. a natural smile makeover

A full mouth dental reconstruction entails a series of dental procedures meant to restore not only aesthetics but also function. Patients who have these procedures often have advanced gum disease or several missing or damaged teeth. Root canals, tooth extractions and dental implants are among the measures taken to improve the mouth’s health.

A **natural smile makeover** combines several dental procedures to restore the appearance of a naturally healthy mouth. Factors taken into consideration as part of the evaluation include skin tone, facial appearance, hair color, lips, gum tissue, teeth and lips.

## Smiles reinvented

Each smile makeover is unique, based on the individual’s appearance and preferences. While teeth whitening can lighten and brighten discolored teeth, some patients may need additional treatments. Dentists focus on both functional and cosmetic elements, often combining any of the following procedures to meet patient needs.

### Crowns

A crown, also called a cap, restores the original size, shape and function of a tooth. Crowns may be made from a composite resin, ceramic, porcelain or metal alloy and can be used for several purposes:

    - Treating cavities too large for fillings

    - Restoring cracked or weakened teeth

    - Protecting or restoring teeth after a root canal

Crowns can also improve a smile by covering teeth that are misshaped or those that have severe discoloration and staining.

### Bridges

A bridge is created when two or more crowns are fused together, filling the gap left by one or more missing teeth. It can help maintain the shape of a patient’s face, prevent existing teeth from moving out of alignment and restore the ability to speak and chew properly. Depending on precise needs, several types of bridge can be used.

### Veneers

A dental veneer is placed over the visible part of a tooth to correct a broad range of dental issues, from chips or significant staining to filling the gap between the upper front teeth. Typically made from composite resin or porcelain, veneers can be matched to the color and texture of the adjacent teeth. The result is a _natural smile makeover_ that is durable and beautiful.

### Clear braces

A crooked smile can cause an uneven bite, which may lead to chipped or worn teeth, gum disease, muscle pain and jaw weakness. Alignment tray systems can straighten teeth, close gaps and help prevent future gum disease. Tooth realignment often gives patients a confidence boost, regardless of age.

## Conclusion

Dental bonding, inlays, onlays and dental implants may also be used to get the straight, beautiful pearly whites that people want in their smiles. Personalized treatment and planning can help maximize time and budget for a streamlined approach to a natural smile makeover.
