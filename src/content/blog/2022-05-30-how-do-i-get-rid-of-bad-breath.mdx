## What is halitosis?

Halitosis is bad breath that originates from the mouth. It can be caused by a variety of factors, including poor oral hygiene, plaque and tartar build-up on teeth, gum disease, dry mouth, food particles stuck in between teeth or braces, and even certain medications.

## How can my dentist help me?

If you are experiencing halitosis, your dentist can help you determine the cause and recommend the best solution. Depending on the underlying cause of your bad breath, your dentist may prescribe an appropriate treatment plan, such as:

*   improved oral hygiene habits (brushing and flossing twice a day)
*   daily use of a fluoride toothpaste
*   professional dental cleanings and exams every six months
*   treatment for gum disease
*   treatment for dry mouth
*   teeth whitening
*   medication adjustment if necessary

## How can I prevent halitosis?

_There are several things you can do to help prevent bad breath, including:_

*   brushing and flossing your teeth twice a day with a fluoride toothpaste
*   using interdental cleaners (such as floss or Picks) to remove food particles from between your teeth
*   rinsing your mouth with water after meals and snacks
*   avoiding tobacco products and alcohols Mouthwash is not a substitute for brushing and flossing
