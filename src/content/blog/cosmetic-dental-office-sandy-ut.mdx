## Ways to Enhance Your Smile with Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry gives you a wide range of options for improving your smile's esthetics. Some procedures can be completed fairly quickly, while others take longer. By the end of your treatment, however, you'll have a beautiful smile that you can show off proudly!

Here are a few ways cosmetic dentistry can benefit you:

1. Brighten your smile – Professional teeth whitening can eliminate unsightly stains and discolorations on your teeth to give your smile a dazzling appearance
2. Conceal minor tooth flaws – Veneers are ultra-thin porcelain shells we can bond to your tooth surface to conceal tooth flaws that detract from your smile's appeal. Using veneers, we can make stains, chips, cracks, and gaps in your smile disappear to give your smile a beautiful, unblemished appearance
3. Improve the size or shape of a tooth – Cosmetic crowns can be used to reshape malformed or irregularly sized teeth to give your smile a uniform appearance. Porcelain crowns blend in with your smile so naturally that no one can tell you've had crown treatment
4. Straighten crooked teeth – Through the use of metal braces or clear aligner therapy, we can straighten crooked or misaligned teeth, close gaps in your smile, correct bite issues, and eliminate problems with overcrowding
5. Replace missing teeth – Tooth loss issues can be resolved with implants, dentures, or bridges to make your smile whole and healthy again
