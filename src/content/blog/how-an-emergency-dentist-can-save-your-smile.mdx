Are you experiencing a sudden, throbbing pain in your tooth that won't go away? Nothing ruins your day quite like a painful toothache. But don't panic! Emergency dentists are here to save the day and your smile. In this blog post, we'll explore what emergency dentists do and how they can help alleviate your dental woes. Whether it's after hours or on the weekend, an emergency dentist is always ready to provide quick and efficient relief for any dental emergencies that come your way. So sit back, relax, and learn how these unsung heroes can be the saving grace of your oral health!

## **What Is An Emergency Dentist?**

If toothache is a regular occurrence, it's time to [see an emergency dentist](/?s=emergency+dentist). Emergency dentists are specially trained in dealing with toothaches and can provide relief quickly. They may also be able to prescribe medication to help ease the pain. In addition, your local hospital may have a dental service if you need an emergency dentist.

## **How Does An Emergency Dentist Help?**

When toothaches strike, there's no time to waste! Fortunately, emergency dentists are standing by to help. Here are a few ways they can help:

### **1. Remove the tooth pain as soon as possible:**

Emergency dentists will work quickly to remove any toothache pain. It means you'll be able to focus on getting better fast rather than worrying about dental procedures.

### **2. Put a temporary filling in the tooth:**

If the tooth is located in an easily accessible area, emergency dentists may prefer to temporarily fill the tooth instead of extracting it together. By doing this, they can avoid further damage and ensure that you receive treatment as soon as possible.

### **3. Perform an examination and provide a diagnosis:**

Since emergency dentists typically treat patients urgently, they must conduct an examination and provide a diagnosis as soon as possible. Doing so will help them determine if any other issues need to be addressed right away.

### **What To Expect From A Visit to An Emergency Dentist**

If you're experiencing a toothache, contacting an emergency dentist as soon as possible is important. After examining your symptoms and discussing your oral health history, the emergency dentist can recommend the best action to resolve your pain.  
The following are some key things to expect during a visit to an emergency dentist:

1. Oral X-rays may be necessary to determine the cause of your toothache and make a proper diagnosis.

1. If there is any indication that the tooth or teeth may be in danger of becoming loose or broken, they will be treated immediately with a dental implant or crown. Sometimes, if the tooth is deemed too damaged to save, it may need to be removed altogether.

1. The emergency dentist will work on relieving your pain with either medication or a dental injection. In some cases, they may also use a dental drill or laser to remove any pain-causing bacteria from within the teeth.

1. additional procedures such as root canal treatment or reconstruction surgery may also be recommended to alleviate your ongoing pain and restore your smile to its optimal state.

## **Tips For Preventing Toothaches In The First Place**

Toothaches can be a dreadful experience, but fortunately, you can[do a few things to help prevent them](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-and-oral-health/emergency-toothache-relief) from happening in the first place. Here are some tips from emergency dentists:

1. Keep your teeth clean and free of plaque. Tooth decay is caused by bacteria gathering on teeth surfaces, so keeping your teeth clean will help to prevent toothache. In addition, use a toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste daily, and ensure you eat enough fruits and vegetables to fight against harmful bacteria.

1. If you get a cold or the flu, get your teeth checked out by a dentist as soon as possible. Poor oral health can lead to toothache because it can increase the amount of pain sensitivity in your teeth.

1. Make sure you have all your dental insurance covered if you need treatment for a toothache. Unfortunately, many dental plans only cover preventive care, such as regular checkups and cleanings, which can help keep your smile healthy and pain-free.

1. If you experience pain in one or more of your teeth, talk to your doctor about whether an appointment at an emergency dentist is best for you. Emergency dentists have the tools and expertise to treat most types of toothaches quickly and effectively without surgery or invasive dental procedures.

### **How To Deal With A Toothache When You See An Emergency Dentist**

If you're experiencing a toothache, seeking emergency dental care as soon as possible is important. Here are a few tips to help you when you see an emergency dentist:

### **1. Make a List of the Symptoms:**

When diagnosing your symptoms, list everything that's been hurting your teeth lately. It will help the emergency dentist more easily determine which teeth are affected and whether there is any other underlying issue.

### **2. Rule Out Other Causes:**

Before seeing an emergency dentist, it's important to rule out any other potential causes of your toothache. For example, if you think an infection causes pain, antibiotics may be necessary to treat it. It can also be helpful to rule out some common dental issues before visiting an emergency dentist. For example, if you experience pain on one side of your mouth but not the other, this may be due to a problem with your bite or jaw alignment. In these cases, seeking professional dental care may be warranted before visiting an emergency dentist.

### **3. Consider Oral Surgery:**

If the toothache is severe or doesn't seem to resolve with treatment from an emergency dentist, consider oral surgery.
