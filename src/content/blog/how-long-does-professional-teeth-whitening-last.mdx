Pearly white teeth are beautiful and can make your smile shine. However, even a professional tooth-whitening treatment lasts for a finite duration. It has a lot to do with how deep the treatment penetrates into the tooth and the lifestyle and eating habits of the patient. Let's explore how long a professional teeth-whitening treatment lasts below:

## Toothpaste

Brushing your teeth with a peroxide- or charcoal-infused whitener can reduce surface stains and lighten up your smile in a matter of weeks. However, because the treatment doesn't penetrate into the teeth or break the molecular bonds of many varnishes, these treatments last mere months and produce lackluster results.

## Whitening Strips

Whitening strips are more effective than toothpaste and just as easy to use. By simply wrapping the strips around your teeth, you can achieve more dazzling results in a few days. Nevertheless, even these treatments fall short of professional methods and will fade away in just three or four months. Strips tend to cost much more than toothpaste but promise faster results.

## Whitening Foam with Trays

Foam whitening treatments are the in-between choice if you are torn between strips and toothpaste. Foam promises results in about two weeks but will fade faster than the brighter smiles you gain with strips and toothpaste.

## Whitening Pens

A whitening pen is easy to apply and can improve the look of your teeth in a matter of days. The drawback is that people who use pens often become dependent because the results also fade in a few days.

## Professional Bleaching Trays

The powerful bleaching solution prescribed by dentists can whiten teeth almost immediately. The effects outlast the retail treatments described above by sustaining the whiteness for six months to a year. These are an easy solution to obtain professional results without the hassle of constantly applying treatments.

## Laser Bleaching

Laser bleaching is a more effective option that penetrates deep into the microscopic textures of the tooth to whiten them. It provides lasting results that promise to keep your pearls looking polished for a year or longer. Although it is more expensive than bleaching trays, it is another option for immediate results.

## Dental Office Bleaching

This is the most durable treatment on the market. It is typically less expensive than laser treatments and outlasts all the other methods by years. An intensive bleaching performed exclusively in-office at Toothbar can last for up to three years.
