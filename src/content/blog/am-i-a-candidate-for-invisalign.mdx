[Invisalign braces and retainers](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/invisible-orthodontic-aligners) are not for everyone. Your dentist will order them just for your mouth so you get a tight fit. Adults and teens are often great candidates for Invisalign, but these appliances are not always recommended for younger children. A child's mouth is still growing and changing, often rapidly. Even a moderate change in the mouth or tooth size could interfere with an Invisalign appliance originally designed for adults. They might not fit snugly or pull the teeth toward the correct position.

Fortunately, Invisalign has addressed this issue and created appliances aimed at children. Like the adult and teen versions, they are only appropriate for certain candidates. Only your experienced dental professional can determine the best brace or aligner for your child.

These appliances are most effective for people with less severe problems with tooth crowding and spaces between their teeth. Those with severe issues, including overbites, crossbites, and underbites, will usually do better with traditional braces or a hybrid system. The same goes for those with complicated and serious tooth issues.

## Hybrid

Invisalign braces and retainers are meant for less severe dental alignment issues, which leaves some people out of this popular treatment. Fortunately, you may qualify for [hybrid treatment](https://www.jco-online.com/archive/2023/01/9-overview-hybrid-aligner-treatment/). Some patients' front teeth would benefit from Invisalign appliances, but perhaps their back teeth have a more serious issue. You may be able to wear clear braces on your front teeth and have more traditional wire braces on your back teeth. Your upper teeth may be relatively straight, while your lower teeth are out of alignment.

This combination of Invisalign with other methods takes many forms and depends on your specific needs. Hybrid appliances allow your dentist to customize a treatment plan that combines the most effective methods to give you an excellent result. The experts at the Toothbar can determine what combination of treatments will work best for you, giving you the smile you have always wanted.
