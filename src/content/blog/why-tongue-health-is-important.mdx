## Why tongue health is important?

When considering oral health, taking good care of teeth and gums are usually some of the first things that come to a person's mind. However, having a healthy tongue is key in [keeping a healthy mouth](/).

The tongue is the unsung hero of the mouth due to being multifunctional. Its functions include talking, swallowing, chewing, tasting, as well as sweeping away food and other debris.

There are many benefits to having a clean, healthy tongue. Due to the rough nature of the tongue's outer surface, it is easy for bacteria to collect and grow there. Prolonged collection of bacteria on the tongue's surface can lead to tooth decay, bad breath, and gum disease.

Cleaning your tongue at least twice a day as part of your daily oral health routine will help keep your tongue healthy while reducing the following issues:

1. Cutting down on number of bacteria that cause bad breath
2. Reducing the amount of plaque on the teeth
3. Enhancing the overall sense of taste
4. Creating a clean-feeling mouth

[The Cleveland Clinic](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/what-your-tongue-can-tell-you-about-your-health/) addresses the difference in the appearance of a healthy tongue and an unhealthy tongue. A normal, healthy tongue will be pink in color and covered in tiny bumps called papillae.

Any sort of discoloration, formation of sores or bumps, lumps, or the onset of pain should be immediately addressed by an oral health professional.
