As we start to enter into the gift giving season, why not give yourself the gift of a healthy, beautiful smile?

Our doctors are happy to offer a complimentary visit so you can find out if you are a candidate for Invisalign® or SureSmile® [clear aligners](/services/invisalign-suresmile-clear-aligners/) and get all your questions answered.

-   We are now able to take a digital 3D scan of your teeth, eliminating the need for messy, uncomfortable impressions of the past.
-   Clear aligners allow you to maintain a professional image throughout your treatment period.
-   Clear aligners can be removed to brush/floss, and to eat/drink, making good oral health easier to maintain.
-   It's a great time to use remaining insurance benefits or FSA dollars before you lose them.

If you are interested in straighter teeth, right now may be the ideal time to begin your treatment. Call or request an appointment online to get started today!
