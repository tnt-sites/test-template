Artisan Dental is proud to be Certified™ by Great Place To Work® for the fourth year in a row. The prestigious award is based entirely on what current employees say about their experience working at Artisan Dental. 84% of employees at Artisan Dental, LLC say it is a great place to work compared to 57% of employees at a typical U.S.-based company.

Great Place To Work® is the global authority on workplace culture, employee experience, and the leadership behaviors proven to deliver market-leading revenue, employee retention and increased innovation.

❝*Great Place To Work Certification is a highly coveted achievement that requires consistent and intentional dedication to the overall employee experience,” says Sarah Lewis-Kulin, the Vice President of Global Recognition at Great Place To Work. She emphasizes that Certification is the sole official recognition earned by the real-time feedback of employees regarding their company culture. “By successfully earning this recognition, it is evident that Artisan Dental stands out as one of the top companies to work for, providing a great workplace environment for its employees.*❞

Artisan Dental wouldn’t be a great place to work without our teams dedication, service, empathy and compassion.

This year, Artisan was able to support several community organizations. We were fortunate enough to volunteer time with More Smiles WI to help serve the community through this non-profit organization! MSW is a clinic that is open full-time and employs a dental team to provide care. MSW still utilizes and relies on volunteerism to help offset the costs of providing charitable care for their patients.

Artisan Dental is honored that our team continues to be present for each other and our patients!
