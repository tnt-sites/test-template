Springfield, MA – Taylor Street Dental and Dr. David I. Peck are proud to announce the addition of Mahmoud Homsi, DMD to their dental practice at 41 Taylor Street. Dr. Mahmoud Homsi graduated in 2012 from Damascus University School of Dentistry in Syria before practicing dentistry and advancing his clinical expertise in Istanbul, Turkey. In 2015, Dr Homsi came to the U.S. to further his dental education. Four years later, he earned his DMD degree from Boston University School of Dental Medicine. He received his degree with high honors and graduated second in his class. Dr. Homsi was awarded the Prestigious Omicron Kappa Upsilon award for his high standards of scholarship and potential for continued professional growth. He was also awarded the Geriatric Dentistry Award for exceptional clinical care for older adults and the Leadership Award for his service to the GSDM chapter of the Student National Dental Association. Dr. Homsi believes strongly in the value of continuing education in delivering the highest quality of treatment to his patients. He is certified in Invisalign treatment for teeth alignment and delivery of Botox and dermal fillers. In his spare time, Dr. Homsi loves to explore cities and restaurants around the world. His favorite city is Boston. He is married to his wife, Alaa, who obtained her PharmD degree in 2018 from the University of Charleston School of Pharmacy. They currently live in Windsor, Connecticut. Dr Peck established his practice on Worthington Street more than 34 years ago. In August 2015, he moved to the historic Stacy Building on Taylor Street. He completely refurbished the building with the latest state-of-the-art technology and equipment, in addition to adding new dentists and associates. Dr Homsi is welcoming new patients. Appointments can be made by calling (413) 781-7645. More information can be found online at www.mygreatsmile.com. The office is located at 41 Taylor Street, 4th Floor, in downtown Springfield. Parking is validated at the Taylor Street Garage.
