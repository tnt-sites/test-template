## Why DIY options can be harmful to the smile

Our team sees many patients in and around the Springfield, MA area who have read on the internet about certain strategies for whitening the smile. Below are some of the DIY attempts that patients might have tried to achieve results:

*   Activated charcoal. Activated charcoal used to be used on individuals who had been poisoned, but there is no scientific proof that using it to whitening the teeth is effective.
*   Oil pulling. Individuals who have tried whitening their smile with oil pulling will rinse the mouth with coconut oil to brighten the teeth. Though it may not be harmful, there’s no evidence of it whitening the smile.
*   Whitening strips. Over-the-counter whitening strips are readily available to patients at the local drugstores. Unfortunately, many patients are unhappy with the results because they do not provide the whitening they expect.
