**The Carbon Neutral Dental Office Recording Now Available**

Artisan Dental recently sponsored a free VIRTUAL webinar – “The Carbon Neutral Dental Office” which is now available for viewing on our YouTube Channel. Speakers include Scott Andersen, co-owner and director of stakeholder stewardship at Artisan Dental; Olivia Fussell, founder and managing director of [Carbon Credit Capital](https://carboncreditcapital.com/) and Robert Banner, senior project manager for [GreenTrees](https://www.green-trees.com/).  “We see carbon offsetting as an important element of a comprehensive plan to reduce our negative impact and enhance our positive impact, realizing our mission to serve all of the stakeholders connected to our business,” said [Scott Andersen](/scott-andersen/), Artisan Dental’s Director of Stakeholder Stewardship and co-owner of the practice with his wife, [Dr. Nicole Andersen](/dr-nicole-andersen/). “We feel we have both a responsibility and an opportunity to be good stewards of the environment we all enjoy and depend on.”
