Researchers in Australia recently published findings linking the consumption of sugar-free beverages and candies  to the erosion of tooth enamel.  While we might ordinarily consider sugar-free drinks as being free from concerns associated with beverages containing sugar, the presence of other types of acids additives in sugar free drinks is leading to the adverse affect on teeth.  You can learn more about research at this **[*link*](http://www.oralhealthcrc.org.au/sites/default/files/Dental%20Erosion%20Briefing%20Paper_FINAL2015.pdf)****.**
