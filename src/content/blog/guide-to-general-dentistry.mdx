## What is General Dentistry?

General dentistry is the branch of medicine that deals with the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of oral diseases and disorders. It includes a wide range of procedures such as dental implants, teeth whitening, Invisalign braces, and more. General dentists are also responsible for educating patients on proper oral hygiene habits, such as brushing and flossing.

## Types of Procedures Performed by a General Dentist

There are many different types of procedures that can be performed by a [general dentist](/dental-services/general-dentistry/). Some of the most common procedures include:

**Cavity fillings:** This is a common procedure that is used to treat cavities. The dentist will remove the decayed portion of the tooth and then fill the cavity with a filling material.

**Root canals:** This procedure is used to treat an infection that has reached the root of the tooth. The dentist will remove the infected tissue and then seal off the root canal.

**Tooth extractions:** This procedure is used when a tooth is too damaged to be saved. The dentist will remove the tooth and then close up the empty socket.

**Dental implants:** This procedure is used to replace missing teeth. The [dentist](/) will place a metal post in the jawbone and then attach a replacement tooth to the post.

## How to Care for Your Teeth and Gums?

It is important to take care of your teeth and gums to prevent cavities, gum disease, and other dental problems. Here are some tips on how to care for your teeth and gums:

1. Brushing your teeth correctly is important: Use a pea-sized amount of toothpaste on your toothbrush. Aim the toothbrush at a 45-degree angle towards the gum line and use gentle circular motions. Brush the front, top, and back of your teeth. Spit out the toothpaste after brushing.

2. Flossing is also important in order to remove plaque and bacteria from between your teeth that your toothbrush cannot reach. Use about 18 inches of floss and wind it around each middle finger, leaving about an inch of floss to work with. Glide the floss up and down against each side of every tooth. Don’t forget to floss behind your very back teeth!

3. Eating a healthy diet helps keep your teeth and gums healthy as well. Eat plenty of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean protein, and low-fat dairy. Avoid sugary drinks and snacks as well as sticky foods that can cling to your teeth and cause cavities.

4. Visit your dentist regularly for professional cleanings and checkups. They can spot problems early on and help you keep your smile healthy!

## Common Conditions Treated by a General Dentist

There are a variety of conditions that can be treated by a general dentist. Some of the most common conditions include:

**Tooth decay:** Tooth decay is one of the most common conditions treated by a general dentist. This condition is caused by bacteria that break down the tooth enamel, resulting in cavities. A general dentist can treat cavities by drilling out the decay and filling the cavity with a dental filling.

**Gum disease:** Gum disease is another common condition treated by a general dentist. This condition is caused by plaque and tartar buildup on the teeth, which can lead to inflammation and bleeding of the gums. A general dentist can [treat gum disease](/blog/how-can-you-treat-gum-disease-at-home) by cleaning the teeth and removing any tartar buildup. They may also recommend specific oral hygiene products to help control plaque and tartar buildup.

**Toothaches:** Toothaches are another common condition that can be treated by a general dentist. This condition is usually caused by an infection or tooth decay. A general dentist can treat a toothache by diagnosing the underlying cause and providing treatment accordingly.
