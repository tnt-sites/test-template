Do you get up close to your mirror and focus on flaws in your teeth? Are you taking extra time brushing your smile, but you don't notice it getting any brighter? You aren't alone, as many people want to get perfect pearly whites. To answer your question of "How do I fix my smile?" is with the help of [cosmetic dentistry](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/cosmetic-dentistry.html). Continue reading to learn about some treatments to get a beautiful smile and how simple lifestyle changes can also help.

## Professional Teeth Whitening

A reliable way to get rid of stains is to visit your dental office for an in-house teeth whitening treatment. A high-quality bleaching agent is placed on your teeth by your dentist, which is both safe and effective. That means that you can get a brighter smile without having sensitivity afterward.

Your dentist gives you an oral examination beforehand to ensure that you have no cavities or gum disease, so you can leave with noticeable results and a wide grin.

## Dental Veneers

These thin porcelain shells are effective in covering many flaws at once, like discoloration, cracks, and minor gaps. They give your teeth a new appearance while being durable and long-lasting. Veneers can last over a decade with proper care, letting you enjoy a confident grin for years to come.

## Metal-Free Restorations

Not only can cosmetic dental work enhance your smile, but some restorative treatments can as well. For instance, if you have a severely cracked tooth, it can not only affect the appearance of it but the function as well! Getting a [CEREC crown](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/restorative-dentistry.html#same) can help restore your bite and confidence by giving you a solution the same day you come in for an appointment.

If you have gaps, then [dental implants](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/dental-implants.html) can help fill in the empty spaces in your grin. You’ll be able to enjoy the benefits that come with a full set of teeth while having it blend in seamlessly!

## Tips for Your Daily Life to Enhance Your Smile

All these treatments need to have a healthy mouth as a foundation. Changing some lifestyle habits can not only enhance your smile but also improve your oral health and candidacy for these services! Here is what you can do:

*   **Follow Proper Oral Hygiene**: Brush twice and floss once daily, and you can avoid cavities that can make your teeth look darker. This also includes routine checkups with your dentist to catch any issues that can lead to a more serious dental problem that can affect your grin.
*   **Don’t Use Tobacco**: Both chewing and smoking tobacco can stain your teeth, so quitting this unhealthy habit can keep your teeth from discoloring quickly.
*   **Don’t Chew Hard Objects**: Biting pen caps, ice, and other similar things can lead to chips or cracks.
*   **Limit Dark-Colored Beverages**: Coffee, tea, and red wine can stain your teeth when you drink them excessively. Instead, enjoy a cup occasionally, or follow sips with a drink of water to lessen discoloration.

When it comes to sprucing up your smile, there are many kinds of treatments that can do just that. Depending on what you want to fix, you can find the service that benefits both the aesthetics of your teeth and your oral health!
