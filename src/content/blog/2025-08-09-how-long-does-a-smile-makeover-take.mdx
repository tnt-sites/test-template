While looking at different cosmetic dental treatments, you may have created a list of ones you think you’ll benefit from. After talking to your dentist, they’ll create a [smile makeover](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/cosmetic-dentistry.html#make) that helps you tackle the flaws that bother you. However, just how long will it take to get to the results you want? Keep reading to learn more about what you can expect this plan’s timeline to be.

## How Long Will My Smile Makeover Take?

Many people choose a smile makeover because it helps consider things such as your time constraints and final budget. Since everyone will have different treatments in theirs, here is what you can expect for individual services that are commonly used:

*   **CEREC Crowns:** This uses a handy piece of technology that allows your dentist to place crowns within one visit. It takes about a couple of hours total as they need to prepare your tooth, and a machine will craft the restoration in around 30 minutes.
*   **Tooth Replacements:** Bridges may take two appointments with a few weeks in between, as a dental lab has to sculpt it out of a high-quality material. Dental implants usually take several months as it takes time to fuse with your jawbone.
*   **Clear Aligners:** In most cases, it can take about 12 months or longer to gently move your teeth into a healthy alignment.
*   **Veneers:** This one takes two appointments that are a couple of weeks apart.
*   **Simple Treatments:** Teeth whitening, dental bonding, and gum recontouring are likely to take one visit, which may be about an hour long.

## What Affects My Smile Makeover Timeline?

The cosmetic services that your dentists offer do take some time, but it’s because they ensure your smile stays healthy while receiving them. That also means it has to be a good foundation before beginning the makeover. If you have any dental problems that can cause complications, such as gum disease, they need to be taken care of first.

Some patients may be able to get multiple treatments done simultaneously, but it depends on which ones you have. This may shorten your overall timeline! Additionally, it’s important to stick to any instructions given to you by your dentist when undergoing services like dental implants or Invisalign. There may be some delays if you find yourself with some complications during them.

## The Best Way to Get a Timeline Estimate

In all, knowing how long it will take to get the pearly whites you want takes a consultation with your dentist. They will look at your teeth and oral health to see what preliminary work needs to be done before beginning your smile makeover. If it takes a bit longer than you expected, don't feel discouraged, as you will be able to show off a renewed grin in no time!
