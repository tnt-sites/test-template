We are excited to share with you that [Artisan Dental](/certified-b-corp/) recently became Wisconsin’s 1st dental practice to become a certified B corporation and the 2nd dental practice in the United States.  Called "the highest standard for socially responsible business by Inc. magazine, B Corporation certification is to sustainable business what USDA organic certification is to food or what LEED certification is to buildings.  There are currently 2,200 certifed B corporations across 50 countries in over 130 industries.  Household names of certified B corporations include [Patagonia clothing](http://www.patagonia.com/search/?q=b%20corp&lang=en_US), [Ben & Jerry's ice cream](http://www.benjerry.com/about-us/b-corp), [Method cleaning products](https://methodhome.com/benefit-blueprint/), and [Stonyfield yogurt](http://www.stonyfield.com/bcorp).  The certifed B corporation designation is awarded by the non-profit B Labs.
