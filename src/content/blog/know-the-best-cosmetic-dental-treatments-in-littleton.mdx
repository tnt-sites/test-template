## Invisalign

Clear plastic aligners called Invisalign are worn over the teeth to gently move the teeth to the desired positions. Each set of clear aligners is personalized to alter your smile using cutting-edge 3D imaging. A fresh set of aligners is applied after every few weeks to bring you closer to having the perfect smile. The trays fit gently over your teeth without using any metal or hardware. Invisalign is practically unnoticeable and can be taken out when you eat or brush your teeth. They also help to avoid the problems that people face in the case of wearing traditional braces. Schedule a visit with us if you want to achieve a perfect smile with the help of Invisalign.

## Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are like a dental façade for your teeth. Thin porcelain shells are used to cover any flaws in the front and sides of your teeth. Nearly any defect can be concealed behind veneers to give you a smile you've always wanted. They can straighten teeth, make them appear equal or longer, fill gaps, or cover crooked teeth. If you wish to modify just one tooth or all of your visible teeth, you can alter the appearance of your smile in roughly two weeks with this treatment option.

You need to know that dental veneers don't correct dental flaws but just help to cover them. The first step in the process is identifying the teeth that require veneers and taking impressions of those teeth so that the veneers can be made. A tiny portion of enamel is removed to create room for the extra width of the veneers because the shells will be directly bonded to the teeth. The veneers can be attached in a single appointment once the teeth have been cleaned and the veneers have come back from the lab.

## Dental Bridges

Missing teeth might affect your confidence in your smile and how your teeth function. Why not use a dental bridge to fill in the gaps in your smile? Cosmetic dentistry may restore your smile in just a few weeks. A dental bridge is among the quickest long-term remedies for missing teeth. Unlike partial dentures, these dental devices are fixed in place and won't slip or shift while you eat, laugh, or talk. Dental crowns are affixed to the natural teeth on either side of the gap to create the typical bridge. The bridge's support structure, which holds the false teeth in place, is where the crowns are joined. The adjacent teeth are treated to suit these crowns for the purpose of preparing a dental bridge. The bridge is made using impressions and imagery to guarantee a flawless fit in your bite.

## White Fillings

Composite fillings can be used for more than simply filling dental cavities. This substance can be used to fix chips or fissures in teeth while perfectly matching the color of your natural teeth. Additionally, it can be utilized to correct cosmetic issues. Most composite resin fillings or bonding can be completed in one appointment, making it a convenient and inexpensive way to improve the appearance of your smile. Dental composite resin comes in a wide variety of forms that can be used for bonding and filling. Most are attached to the teeth in putty form and are made of plastic, glass, and ceramic materials. Most resins need a specific light to dry into a hard substance. The surface can be polished and smooth after it has solidified to make it fit the tooth. When used in dental bonding and fillings, the composite resin is incredibly resilient and can last for many years.
