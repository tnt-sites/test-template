While most people associate orthodontics with children and teens, the [American Association of Orthodontists](https://www.aaoinfo.org/_/frequently-asked-questions/) reports that about one in five patients are adults. ## Gradual Changes Over Time

Correcting misaligned teeth requires a professional touch. The staff at the Toothbar have the equipment, training, and experience to treat our patients properly. If you are looking for [Invisalign in downtown Austin](/invisalign/), we are ready to help you.

All [orthodontic appliances](https://www.aaoinfo.org/orthodontic-treatment-options/) work by applying pressure on teeth over time. Traditional braces do this with wires, brackets, and elastics. These devices are cemented to the patient’s teeth throughout the treatment. While metal braces were the standard for many years, they also have drawbacks such as limiting the patient’s diet, making dental hygiene difficult and simply being uncomfortable.

The Invisalign process is different. Instead of permanent braces, patients wear a series of clear aligners during the treatment. At your first appointment with a cosmetic dentist in downtown Austin TX, the doctor will examine your mouth to see whether you are a good candidate for Invisalign. It is important to note that in some situations, metal braces are still the best solution for a straight smile.

The next step for Invisalign in downtown Austin is a three-dimensional scan of your teeth. This scan will be used to create your first clear aligner. This aligner will make a small adjustment to the position of your teeth over a few weeks. You will then receive a series of aligners that will make small corrections until the process is done.

### Convenience and Comfort

Our Austin cosmetic dentistry office [loves using Invisalign](/blog/the-benefits-of-invisalign-with-toothbar) because it is much more convenient for our patients. Especially for adult patients, the clear aligners are less noticeable during professional and social interactions. Because they can be removed, patients do not need to change their diets. They can simply take the appliance out during meals. This also means that patients can carry out their normal dental hygiene routine without interruption.

Because the aligners are specifically designed for your mouth, the Invisalign process often leads to a shorter treatment period than traditional braces. There are usually fewer office visits and fewer potential problems with the appliance. It is wonderful to see a patient’s smile improve with as little drama as possible.
