## Dental Implants

It’s impossible to not get excited about dental implants. [Implants](/dental-implants/) are a permanent solution to replace missing teeth and are now the [standard of care](https://www.nytimes.com/2009/11/17/health/17brod.html). They are an effective solution and offer all the expected advantages.

Like real teeth, they require the same level of care. Although implants don’t decay, daily brushing and flossing, as well as regular dental checkups, are an imperative part of a regular program of dental care.

While your [dental cleanings will require some additional consideration](https://www.deardoctor.com/inside-the-magazine/issue-21/dental-implant-maintenance/), we will make sure your implants get the expert care they need in order to last a lifetime.

Until relatively recently, the available solutions to the problem of missing teeth didn’t address all the issues associated with the problem. Considering the extent of the number of people affected in the U.S. alone, 35 million, the best that could be done for patients was to offer some kind of bridge.
