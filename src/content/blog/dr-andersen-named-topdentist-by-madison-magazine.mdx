We are delighted to share that [Dr. Andersen](/dr-nicole-andersen/) was named one of Madison’s best dentist by her peers in a

[topDentist poll](http://www.channel3000.com/madison-magazine/home-lifestyle/top-dentists-2015/34442058) published in the August 2015 issue of [Madison Magazine](http://www.channel3000.com/madison-magazine/home-lifestyle/top-dentists-2015/34442058).

How are topDentists selected?

Area dentists and oral health care specialists are asked to consider their peers years of

experience, continuing education, manner with patients, as well as the incorporation of

new techniques and technology.  Past awards and standing in various dental academies

(Academy of General Dentistry, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry) and the

actual physical outcomes of their clinical work are also factored into the selection

process.  Listings in [topDentists](http://www.usatopdentists.com/) can not be purchased and no payment is required to be

listed.  As a result, inclusion in topDentists is considered an honor.

What is topDentists?

[topDentists](http://www.usatopdentists.com/) was started in January 2007 with the intent of identifing the best dentists

and oral health care specialists in the country. TopDentists is the only list of its kind,

chosen by dental professionals themselves.
