If you are missing multiple teeth or just a single tooth, you know just how serious that can be. It can leave you feeling quite self-conscious, for example, making you hesitant to smile, laugh, yawn, or even talk with your mouth open very wide. Eating your favorite foods or certain foods when you are missing many teeth can be particularly difficult. A major dental health issue to consider is the position and condition of your remaining teeth, as a single gap may cause your other teeth to shift out of place. Additionally, your jawbone can be affected by a missing tooth, as there may be some level of bone loss and diminished bone density in the jaw where the tooth gap is located. For these and other reasons, people have come to visit our [Hartford cosmetic dentist](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/blog/who-are-good-candidates-for-dental-implants/) to learn about implant dentistry options. Dental implants are sturdy posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone and gum tissue in order to support a replacement tooth, dentures, or dental bridges. Since not everyone is an ideal candidate for dental implants, it is important that you undergo a full dental implants evaluation and consultation. The best candidates for dental implants are people who have healthy gums and a sturdy jawbone. Since the dental implants are placed directly into these mouth structures, it is imperative that their health be solid. People who do not have sufficient bone density in the jaw can potentially undergo a bone grafting surgery to improve their ability to get [Hartford dental implants](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/dental-implants.html). If you are not a good candidate for dental implants, your dentist will be able to devise another option that is best suited to meet your needs. This may involve simply getting regular dentures or dental bridges, which will be quite helpful for you and your needs.
