## Cleaning Tips for Invisalign

### Clean Your Invisalign Trays Every Day

When cleaning Invisalign trays, you should use only lukewarm water and mild soap or toothpaste. Gently brush the Invisalign trays with a soft toothbrush in order to remove any food or bacteria that may have accumulated on them. You should also rinse Invisalign trays thoroughly before putting them back in your mouth.

### Clean Your Teeth and Invisalign Trays After Eating

It’s important to clean your Invisalign trays after every meal or snack. This will help prevent food from getting stuck in the Invisalign trays and causing bacteria to form. You should also brush and floss your teeth after eating, as this will help keep Invisalign and your teeth clean.

### Use a Retainer Cleaner

If you’re looking for a deeper clean, then using a retainer cleaner is the way to go. These products are specifically designed to clean Invisalign and other orthodontic retainers. Follow the instructions on the packaging carefully and make sure to rinse Invisalign thoroughly after using a retainer cleaner.

## Prevention Tips for Invisalign

### Avoid Eating Sticky and Chewy Foods

When wearing Invisalign, it’s best to avoid foods that are sticky or chewy as these can easily get stuck in the Invisalign trays. Examples include gum, caramel, and taffy. If you do eat these types of foods, make sure to clean Invisalign and your teeth immediately afterward.

### Avoid Hard or Crunchy Foods

It’s also best to avoid hard or crunchy foods when wearing Invisalign. Examples include candy, chips, and nuts. These types of foods can easily damage Invisalign trays, so it’s best to steer clear of them.

### Don't Smoke

Smoking is extremely harmful to your oral health and Invisalign trays. The tar and nicotine in cigarettes can stain [Invisalign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Align_Technology) trays and cause discoloration, so it’s best to avoid smoking altogether.
