**Use Whitening Strips?**  
The human body is bound to age with time, and this is visible in most parts of the body, including our teeth. This necessitates a teeth whitening procedure to conceal the yellow dentine tissue and preserve the white smile.

**How Do Teeth Whitening Strips Work?**  
Whitening strips are a common form of whitening a person’s teeth to get that dazzling smile. These strips are thin, flexible, and made of plastic, which has a whitening gel made of [peroxide on its surface](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jerd.12519). It is recommended that they easily fit the form of your teeth to assist in lightening the shade of your teeth gradually.

**The Science Behind the Whitening Strips**  
The active ingredients frequently found in the tooth whitening strips include hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide that, through oxidation, wears out stains and reveals a natural [color of teeth](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jerd.12519).

Research has indicated that as many as forty percent of the adults living in the United States of America bear discontentment with the shade of their teeth, and this has resulted in them seeking measures to whiten their teeth.

The American Dental Association conducted a survey, and it was discovered that 80 percent of people within the age group of [18 to 49 years](https://mytowncenterdental.com/teeth-whitening-is-the-most-popular-procedure-for-cosmetic-dentists/) requested whiter teeth, which shows the people’s interest in the bleaching of their teeth.
