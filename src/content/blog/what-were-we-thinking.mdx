Why did Artisan Dental become a Certified B Corp?

Since our inception in 2014, our [mission and vision](/philosophy/) has been to create an integrated prosperity that includes all stakeholders connected to our business. These stakeholders include our patients, our team members, the suppliers who support us and the communities and the planet we all share.

We became a Certified B Corp to spread awareness, create good and ignite worldwide change; to be a part of the global movement of people using business as a force for good, and to work together with companies all over the world that are creating exceptional positive social and environmental impact.
