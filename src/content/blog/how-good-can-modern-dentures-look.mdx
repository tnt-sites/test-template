If you’ve already passed that point, and several of your teeth need to be replaced, the [American Academy of Periodontology](https://www.perio.org/consumer/full-mouth-implants) assures you that dental implants can replace all of your teeth. Your downtown cosmetic dentist has experience in the most up-to-date dental implant procedures.

At the downtown Austin dentistry, Toothbar, a dental exam showing the condition of your teeth and a general discussion of your health and your goals will determine if a tooth implant is right for you.

Dental implants do not move or slide around in your mouth. **Once an implant procedure is completed, your new tooth is esthetic, functions and looks just like your natural tooth.** The Austin cosmetic dentist, [Toothbar](/blog/how-are-teeth-implants-done), fixes or places a dental implant into your mouth for each extracted tooth. A temporary cover for the implant looks and functions like a natural tooth. Over a few weeks, the permanent implant gradually anchors into your jawbone structure.

Then the [Austin](https://www.austintexas.org/) cosmetic dentist attaches a special extension to the implant that will secure a customized tooth crown to the permanent implant. Because the implant integrates with your jawbone, it maintains the health and shape of your mouth. This keeps your beautiful smile intact.
