    heading: Procedures We Use to Create Your New Smile
    anchorName: ""
    subtext: |-
      Dr. Andersen will create a custom treatment plan to enhance your smile and give it the appearance you’ve always wanted. The procedures she uses to create your new smile might include:

      #### Porcelain Veneers

      Porcelain veneers are thin porcelain coverings that are bonded to the front of your teeth to change their shape, size and/or color. Porcelain veneers can usually be completed in two visits and can make a dramatic change to your smile.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/2-1.jpg)

      #### Dental Crowns

      A dental crown is used when the tooth needs cosmetic changes and structural repair. A crown can also be used on top of an implant post to replace a missing tooth.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/3-1.jpg)

      #### Teeth Whitening

      Teeth whitening can be a quick way to markedly enhance your smile. It is an easy solution for people who want to improve the color of their teeth with minimal procedures. Remove stains from coffee, smoking or other common causes with a painless teeth whitening treatment at Artisan Dental.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/4-1.jpg)

      #### Dental Bonding

      Dental bonding is a tooth-colored dental resin that can be used to correct flaws such as chips and minor cracks in a tooth. It can also be used to change the size, shape or color of your teeth, but is not as durable and long-lasting as veneers.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/5-1.jpg)

      #### Laser Gum Sculpting for a Gummy Smile or Uneven Gum Line

      Uneven gum lines and gums that are too large around your teeth can cause your smile to look unattractive, and in some cases can affect your overall oral health. For most gum reshaping procedures the doctor uses a dental laser as a minimally-invasive way to sculpt the gum tissue.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/6-1.jpg)

      #### Invisalign® & SureSmile® Teeth Straightening  

      Effective clear aligner treatment designed to fit your lifestyle. Clear aligners are designed to orchestrate each step of your smile transformation with precision.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/7-1.jpg)

      #### Dental Implants

      Dental implants are considered the modern solution for replacing missing teeth. A titanium post is inserted into your jaw and topped by a crown, which looks, acts and feels just like a natural tooth. We are usually able to provide all stages of your implant treatment in-house.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/8.jpg)

      #### Dental Bridges

      A dental bridge is a series of connected crowns that is used to replace one or more missing teeth. Although they have certain disadvantages compared to implants, bridges are a suitable option for replacing missing teeth in certain circumstances.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/9-1.jpg)

      #### Full Mouth Restoration

      A full-mouth restoration can handle decayed, cracked or damaged teeth to full health and function and allow you to smile again with confidence. Dr. Andersen provides comprehensive dental services and has the skills necessary to make your full-mouth restoration successful. She is experienced in handling extensive cases, and is partner to one of the country’s best aesthetic dental labs to make sure the work you have done is not only strong and durable, but also looks aesthetic and natural.
