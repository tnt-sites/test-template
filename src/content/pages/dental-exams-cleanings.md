        html: What to Expect During Your Dental Exam
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <p><span>During your initial dental visit and regular check-ups, our dentist and hygienist will perform a comprehensive exam designed to identify potential concerns early. This allows us to address issues before they require more intensive treatments. Here’s what your exam will typically include:</span><b></b></p>
          <ul><li aria-level="1"><b>Diagnostic X-Rays (Radiographs)<br><br></b></li></ul>
          <p><span>X-rays are an essential tool for detecting hidden issues such as:</span></p>
          <ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Tooth decay between teeth or under restorations</span></li><li aria-level="1"><span>Bone loss related to gum disease</span></li><li aria-level="1"><span>Cysts, tumors, or other abnormalities</span></li><li aria-level="1"><span>Root positioning for orthodontic evaluations</span></li><li aria-level="1"><b>Oral Cancer Screening<br><br></b></li></ul>
          <p><span>Our team will check your face, neck, lips, tongue, throat, and gums for any signs of oral cancer. Early detection is critical in successfully treating oral cancer, and regular screenings can provide peace of mind.</span><b></b></p>
          <ul><li aria-level="1"><b>Gum Disease Evaluation – </b>We assess the condition of your gums and supporting bone structure for signs of periodontal disease. Catching gum disease early can prevent further bone loss and keep your teeth securely in place.</li><li aria-level="1"><b>Examination of Tooth Decay – </b>Using specialized tools, we’ll carefully inspect every tooth surface to identify decay or weakened areas that need treatment. Early detection of cavities can prevent further damage and the need for more invasive procedures.</li><li aria-level="1"><b>Assessment of Existing Restorations – </b>Our team will check any crowns, fillings, or other dental restorations for signs of wear or damage to ensure they continue to protect your teeth effectively.</li></ul>
      - type: subheading
        html: What to Expect During a Professional Dental Cleaning
      - type: prose
        html: <span>Even if you brush and floss regularly, professional cleanings are essential to maintain healthy teeth and gums. Cleanings are typically performed by one of our Registered Dental Hygienists, who are skilled in removing the buildup that regular brushing can’t eliminate. Here’s what your cleaning appointment will include:</span>
      - type: prose
        html: <ol><li><b>Removal of Calculus (Tartar) – </b>Calculus is hardened plaque that forms when plaque is left on the teeth for an extended period. It can build up both above and below the gumline and cannot be removed through regular brushing or flossing. Our hygienist will use specialized instruments to remove this buildup to prevent further gum irritation and infection.<br><br></li><li><b>Removal of Plaque – </b>Plaque is a sticky, bacteria-filled film that continuously forms on your teeth. When not removed, the bacteria in plaque produce toxins that irritate the gums, leading to gingivitis and, eventually, periodontal disease. Professional cleanings target and eliminate plaque in hard-to-reach areas.<br><br></li><li><b>Teeth Polishing – </b>Once the plaque and calculus are removed, we’ll polish your teeth to remove stains and give them a smooth, clean surface. Polishing also helps prevent plaque from reattaching to the enamel, keeping your teeth looking their best.<br><br></li><li><b>Professional Flossing and Fluoride Application (Optional) – </b>After polishing, your hygienist may floss between your teeth to ensure all remaining debris is removed. For added protection, fluoride treatments may also be recommended to strengthen your enamel and help prevent cavities.</li></ol>

          <p><span>Regular dental exams and cleanings are the cornerstone of preventive dentistry. Here’s why scheduling these visits every six months is critical to your oral health:</span></p>

Taking care of your mouth can positively impact your general well-being.</span></li></ul>
