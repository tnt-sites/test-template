      #### Information:

      At Artisan Dental, our team provides comprehensive treatment for individuals and families. When you make an appointment through us, we will care for all aspects of your oral health to help prevent the onset of dental issues such as cavities, gum disease, and other issues of the jaw and mouth. Our oral cleaning sessions can help identify problems beforehand, address those issues, and provide comprehensive treatment plans that prevent dental issues from becoming worse.

      Artisan Dental has an excellent team of dentists, assistants, and hygienists that are dedicated to providing you with the highest level dental care. Under the direction of leading dentists, including Dr. Andersen, Dr. Dayal, Dr. Reimer, and Dr. Rehermann, our combined levels of experience and professionalism are here for you in whatever you need to keep your teeth and gums healthy.

      Our regular cleanings are considered a vital part of our care because through these exams, we’re able to [detect oral diseases early on and treat them effectively](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/taking-care-your-teeth-and-mouth). Regular dental cleanings allow us to keep your mouth healthy and help us monitor all aspects of your oral health to the highest degree of care we can provide. During your dental exam, we work to understand all aspects of your health, including:

      -   **Your Medical History:** During your exam and cleaning, we look at all aspects of your medical history, medications, and family history to discuss how they may be affecting your oral health and any oral disease you may have.
      -   **Your Dental History:** Any previous dental work or undetected issues will be discussed with you during your cleaning to help us provide more comprehensive plans that work with you.
      -   **Habits and Lifestyle:** Oral habits, including brushing and flossing, are [an essential part of your oral care](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/001957.htm). We work to help provide better ease of access to dental services that can be better integrated into your lifestyle to improve your oral health.
      -   **Pricing and Treatments:** Through discussing financing options and treatment plans, we provide personalized attention to the needs of our patients.

      Our cleaning sessions at Artisan Dental can provide you with that clean feel you need for healthy teeth. This includes removing any signs of plaque and tartar, addressing bacteria along the gum line, observing signs of discoloration throughout the teeth, and assessing the health of your jawbone in relation to your tooth alignment. All this occurs just through regular cleaning with us!

      ### Choose Artisan Dental in Madison, Wisconsin Today!

      Having good oral hygiene is the backbone of a healthy smile. Our professional dental cleanings work to keep your mouth healthy long-term and ensure that you and your family have clean teeth free from plaque and food debris. For more information, contact our dental team to arrange an appointment and keep your teeth healthy for years to come. Through an appointment with us, our team members will discuss with you financing options, scheduling plans, and treatment options we have available to bring you the most out of your dental care.
