      #### About Dr. Andersen:

      Artisan Dental is the product of Dr. Andersen’s vision, training and values.

      Dr. Andersen graduated from the University of Iowa with a B.A. in Biology and a D.D.S. degree from the College of Dentistry. She has been in private practice since 1998.

      Continuing education and staying connected with the newest techniques, products, and equipment is essential to a journey of excellence.  Dr. Andersen’s passion for learning has resulted in her devoting hundreds of hours to higher learning, including hands-on clinics with some of the best instructors in the world.

      Learn more about the specific post-graduate continuing education courses and hands-on clinics Dr. Andersen has participated in below, including studying under the direction of Dr. Newton Fahl Jr., Fahl Center, Dr. Pete Dawson, The Dawson Academy, Dr. Pascal Magne, Interdisciplinary Dental Education Academy, Dr. Arun Garg, Implant Seminars, Frank Spear, Spear Education and Dr. David Hornbrook of the Hornbrook Group.

      Dr. Andersen graduated with honors from the Hornbrook Group’s distinguished national clinical training program in Chicago, IL. This hands-on program in cosmetic dentistry focuses on smile design and enhancement using the latest state-of-the-art technology and materials. Nationwide, only one percent of dentists graduate from this type of advanced training institute.

      Based upon reviews by local dentists and specialists Dr. Andersen was selected as a “**Top Dentist” from 2011 – 2026.**

      Dr. Andersen married Scott Andersen in 2002, and they are the proud parents of two children. In her free time, she enjoys reading, walking her dogs, and spending time with family.
