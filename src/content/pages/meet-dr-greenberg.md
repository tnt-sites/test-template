Now, excited to join Taylor Street Dental, he looks forward to working alongside one of his favorite mentors, Dr. David Peck.

      Dr. Greenberg remains up-to-date with developments in dentistry through continuous education, focusing particularly on general and cosmetic dentistry and dental surgery. His expertise covers various procedures, including tooth extractions, root canals, restorative crown and bridge treatments, removable dentures, and dental implants.

He enjoys getting to know his patients, understanding that each individual and their smile are unique. Whether addressing cosmetic concerns, restorative needs, or dental emergencies, Dr. Greenberg strives to establish genuine connections with his patients, providing them with excellent, tailored care.
