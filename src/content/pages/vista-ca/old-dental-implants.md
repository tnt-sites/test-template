  label: Definition of Dental Implant Terminology
  contentSections:
  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
    text: Definition of Dental Implant Terminology
    level: h3
    size: md
  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list
    items:
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Abutment
      text: An abutment is a component that attaches to the dental implant so a professional can place
        a dental crown to provide patients with an artificial, aesthetically pleasing and fully-functional
        smile.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Bridge
      text: Multiple replacement teeth that are fixed in place via attachment to dental implants, natural
        adjacent teeth, or a combination of the two.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dental Crown
      text: A crown is an artificial tooth, usually consisting of porcelain, which covers the top of the
        implant to provide people with an aesthetically pleasing and fully-functional tooth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dental Implant
      text: A dental implant is an artificial tooth root that is placed into your jaw to hold a replacement
        tooth or bridge. Dental implants may be an option for people who have lost a tooth or teeth due
        to periodontal disease, an injury, or some other reason.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Endosteal (endosseous)
      text: Endosteal is a type of dental implant that a professional places in the alveolar and basal
        bone of the mandible that transcends only one cortical plate.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Eposteal (subperiosteal)
      text: Eposteal is a type of dental implant that conforms to whichever edentulous surface of an alveolar
        bone is superior.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Implant-Supported Bridge
      text: An implant-supported bridge is a dental bridge that professionals fix in place with the use
        of dental implants inserted in the jaw to create a sturdy set of artificial teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Osseointegration
      text: Osseointegration is the process in which a titanium dental implant fuses with the surrounding
        bone over several months after an oral health professional places the implant in the jaw.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Periodontal
      text: Literally "around the tooth"
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Resorption
      text: Resorption is the process in which the body absorbs the calcium from the jaw since there are
        no tooth roots to cause the necessary stimulation and proceeds to use the calcium in other areas.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Transosteal (transosseous)
      text: Transosteal is a type of dental implant that includes threaded posts which penetrate the superior
        and inferior cortical bone plates of the jaw.
