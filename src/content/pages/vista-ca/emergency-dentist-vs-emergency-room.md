      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Facial Cellulitis
        text: Facial cellulitis is a condition in which an infection from an abscess or plaque in the

      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Impacted Wisdom Tooth
        text: An impacted wisdom tooth is a tooth that does not erupt from the gums properly that can
          cause pain and become infected without professional extraction.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Irreversible Pulpitis
        text: Irreversible pulpitis is when the pain in the tooth occurs spontaneously or continues to
          hurt after the patient removes the hot/cold substance that was causing pain. Normal pulpitis
          will cease hurting directly after removing what is causing the pain.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Lateral Luxation
        text: Lateral luxation is the displacement of a tooth that moves it in the wrong direction while
          it is still attached to the gums.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Pericoronitis
        text: Pericoronitis is the inflammation of soft tissues surrounding a tooth that does not fully
          erupt from the gums and causes pain.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Tooth Avulsion
        text: Tooth avulsion is when the tooth completely displaces from the socket due to an accident
          or other health issues. We can replant the tooth if the patient seeks immediate care.
