        inside of your mouth, which makes it difficult to see signs of oral cancer until the disease has

        of minutes. It works by acting like a protective barrier so food particles and plaque cannot become
        trapped, thereby reducing your risk of developing cavities.

    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: Root canals.
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: If the center of your tooth is infected, you most likely need a root canal. The obvious sign
        of this is if you have tooth sensitivity or a toothache. To perform a root canal, we will create
        a small hole in the tooth to remove the infected area prior to cleaning the area, seal it up with
        a rubber-like material, and then use a filling or dental crown to restore and protect the tooth.
        We will numb the area to be sure you feel comfortable during the procedure. It will last slightly
        longer than treating a cavity, but the process is fairly similar. This is one of the general dentistry
        services we perform to save an infected tooth and prevent the need for it to be extracted.

      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Cosmetic Dentistry
        text: Cosmetic dentistry is generally used to refer to any dental work that improves the appearance
          (though not necessarily the function) of a person's teeth, gums and/or bite.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Decay
        text: Tooth decay is when the enamel of the tooth begins to decay and cause erosion from plaque
          and tartar on the teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dental Caries
        text: Dental caries are also known as cavities and result from a lack of proper oral hygiene leaving
          plaque that forms tiny holes in the teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dental Checkup
        text: A dental checkup is an appointment that involves cleaning the teeth, identifying any signs
          of infection and removing said signs of infection at least once every six months in the office.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dental Filling
        text: A dental filling involves restoring the structure of the tooth by using metal, alloy, porcelain
          or plastic to fill the tooth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dental Prophylaxis
        text: A dental prophylaxis is a professional and detailed cleaning that involves the removal of
          plaque, calculus and stains from the teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dental Sealants
        text: Dental sealants contain a resinous material that we apply to the chewing surfaces of the
          posterior teeth to prevent dental caries.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dentist
        text: A dentist, also known as a dental surgeon, is a doctor who specializes in the diagnosis,
          prevention, and treatment of diseases and conditions of the oral cavity.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Gingivitis
        text: Gingivitis is the inflammation of gum tissue that results from plaque, other infections
          in the mouth and poor oral hygiene.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Preventive Dentistry
        text: Preventive dentistry is the dentistry that focuses on maintaining oral health in order to
          prevent the spread of plaque, the formation of tartar and infections in the mouth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Tartar
        text: Tartar forms when plaque builds up on the surface of the teeth and calcifies into a hard
          surface that is much more difficult to remove and will require professional treatment.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Tooth Enamel
        text: Tooth enamel is the protective visible outer surface of a tooth that contains the dentin
          and dental pulp.
