  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
    text: The Procedure
    level: h3
    size: md
  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
    text: When patients visit our North County Cosmetic and Implant Dentistry offices, we will take an
      impression of their teeth to create the veneers in a lab. The veneers will match the size, shape,
      and color of your teeth, taking a week or more to create.
  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
    text: In order to place dental veneers, we will first prepare your tooth by removing a portion of
      the enamel. This helps the veneer lay flat and in line with your gum tissue. After preparing the
      tooth, we will secure a temporary veneer using removable glue. While wearing a temporary veneer,
      it is important to brush your teeth gently and avoid eating any foods that may push it out of place.
      Since it is secured by removable glue, it can be moved fairly easily.
  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
    text: When your permanent veneers are ready, you will come back into our North County Cosmetic and
      Implant Dentistry offices so that we can remove the temporary and ensure that the new veneer is
      a perfect fit. It is typically attached using a water based solution to see how it looks. If any
      adjustments need to be made, it will be done at this time. Otherwise, the tooth will be cleaned,
      and the dental veneer will be bonded to the tooth. Patients will enjoy how the results look. They
      are instant, and you can start flashing your smile right away.
  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
    text: After the Procedure
    level: h3
    size: md
  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
    text: Your teeth will typically be sore for a few days after the preparation phase and after placing
      the permanent veneers. During this time, you can take pain medication to help you stay comfortable.
      Patients can also benefit from getting rest and using a cold compress to reduce any swelling. We
      recommend that patients watch what they eat for a few days and stick with foods that are soft. While
      there is a short recovery time when getting veneers, the procedure is entirely worth it because
      in the end, you will have a beautiful smile.

      title: Allograft
      text: An allograft is a graft of tissue from a donor within the same species as the patient that
        is not a genetic relative.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Alveolar Ridge
      text: The alveolar ridge is the bony ridge for one of the jaw ridges located on the roof of the
        mouth or on the bottom of the mouth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Autograft
      text: An autograft is a graft of tissue from one portion of an individual's body to another.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Bleachorexia
      text: Bleachorexia is when people continue to abuse teeth whitening solutions without consulting
        professionals, causing significant damage to their enamel and gums in the process.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Cap Splint
      text: A cap splint is a plastic or metallic device that professionals may use to treat mandible
        or maxillary fractures and cover crowns of teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Cosmetic Dentistry
      text: Cosmetic dentistry is generally used to refer to any dental work that improves the appearance
        (though not necessarily the function) of a person's teeth, gums and/or bite.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Crown Lengthening
      text: Crown lengthening is a surgical procedure that increases the extent of a tooth structure for
        restorative or esthetic purposes.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dental Crown
      text: A crown is an artificial tooth, usually consisting of porcelain, which covers the top of the
        implant to provide people with an aesthetically pleasing and fully-functional tooth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dentin Hypersensitivity
      text: Dentin hypersensitivity is when the enamel wears down on the tooth enough that the dentin
        faces exposure and will cause great levels of pain when a person applies pressure to the tooth
        or drinks hot/cold beverages.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dentist
      text: A dentist, also known as a dental surgeon, is a doctor who specializes in the diagnosis, prevention,
        and treatment of diseases and conditions of the oral cavity.
