        most convenient and affordable way to address the problem. Therefore, find out if a dentist offers
        restorative solutions before starting the procedure.

  - _component: page-sections/builders/custom-section
    label: What are the requirements to qualify for a dental implants procedure?
    contentSections:
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: What are the requirements to qualify for a dental implants procedure?
      level: h2
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: At North County Cosmetic and Implant Dentistry, we place dental implants in patients for a
        variety of reasons. One of the key, qualifying factors is health. If you have any other oral health
        problems, these will need to be addressed first. Since this is a surgical procedure, it is important
        to start the process with healthy teeth and gums. The other qualifying factor is bone density.
        We will take X-rays in our Vista dental office to determine if your bone is dense enough for secure
        implant placement. If it is not, we can discuss other options such as bone grafts.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: How long does the process take?
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: That depends on how many implants you are having placed. It can take up to an hour to place
        each metal implant. This is why if you are having a lot of teeth replaced, you may need to visit
        us several times. Once the metal post has been implanted, you will need to wait a couple of months
        for the process of osseointegration to take place. This is where the metal and bone fuse together
        to provide a sturdy, synthetic root system for your new tooth. You will not feel anything while
        this is taking place but waiting is important to ensure that your final tooth is as durable as
        a natural one would be. When ready, we will attach the new crown (tooth) to an abutment that is
        secured to your implant. This appointment will be much shorter.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: How will the process of receiving dental implants impact my job or taking care of my kids?
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: When you are scheduled for the surgical portion of the procedure, you will need to take a
        couple of days off of work. Ideally, we recommend that you visit our dental office at the end
        of the week so that you can recover over the weekend. Your gums will be sensitive and sore for
        a few days but if you have the procedure on Thursday, you should be highly functional at work
        on Monday. As for taking care of children, you can certainly do so after the procedure but we
        do recommend getting some rest the day of so that your body can focus on recovery.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: What is the recovery like?
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: At North County Cosmetic and Implant Dentistry, we recommend that you prepare for recovery
        by putting soft ice packs in the freezer, buying ibuprofen, and soft foods that you can eat. For
        a few days, your gums will be sore so the ice and ibuprofen will help to reduce any swelling while
        eating soft foods will help to prevent discomfort. After a few days, you should begin to feel
        better and start to eat regular foods though you should still avoid anything sharp. Since there
        is a delay between when the metal implant is placed and when the new tooth is attached, we often
        provide patients with a temporary denture to wear so that their smile appears intact while waiting
        for the procedure to be completed.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: What are the final results like with a dental implant compared to a bridge or dentures?
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: Be sure to ask about the final results and what you can expect for your particular situation.
        Scientifically, an implant is the most secure and durable tooth replacement solution that you
        can find because it derives strength from the jawbone. By comparison, a bridge relies on your
        other teeth to hold the bridge in place and dentures are removable which means that they can come
        loose.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: What costs are involved with an implant procedure?
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: Initially, you will have the pre-operative expense of examinations and X-rays, which most
        insurance companies will cover. The actual procedure will have the expense of placing the implant
        and the anesthesia that is used to keep you comfortable. Finally, there is a cost associated with
        creating and placing the tooth portion of the implant (dental crown). We can give you an exact
        breakdown of the cost, something that you should request from every dentist.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: Do I have to pay for this in cash or are there financing options?
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: Unless you can afford to pay for the procedure in cash, it is important to visit a dentist
        that offers financing solutions or payment plans since this is a more costly, restorative solution.

    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: Definition of Dental Implant Terminology
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list
      items:
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: 3D CT Scan
        text: A 3D CT Scan involves using a computed tomography scanner to create a 3D image of one’s
          teeth, mouth, jaw or other body part for examination.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Abutment
        text: An abutment is a component that attaches to the dental implant so a professional can place
          a dental crown to provide patients with an artificial, aesthetically pleasing and fully-functional
          smile.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dental Implant
        text: A dental implant is an artificial tooth root that is placed into your jaw to hold a replacement
          tooth or bridge. Dental implants may be an option for people who have lost a tooth or teeth
          due to periodontal disease, an injury, or some other reason.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dental Implant Coatings
        text: Dental implant coatings help to anchor the surrounding bone more efficiently and fight bacterial
          infection better than in the past.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dental Implant Connectors
        text: Dental implant connectors are also known as abutments and connect the implant screw to the
          dental crown.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Edentulous
        text: Edentulous is a term that refers to an individual who is lacking tooth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Endosteal (endosseous)
        text: Endosteal is a type of dental implant that a professional places in the alveolar and basal
          bone of the mandible that transcends only one cortical plate.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Eposteal (subperiosteal)
        text: Eposteal is a type of dental implant that conforms to whichever edentulous surface of an
          alveolar bone is superior.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Endosteal Implants Types
        text: An endosteal implant is one of the more common types of dental implants that professionals
          place in a patient’s mouth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Implant-Supported Bridge
        text: An implant-supported bridge is a dental bridge that professionals fix in place with the
          use of dental implants inserted in the jaw to create a sturdy set of artificial teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Oral Surgeon
        text: An oral surgeon has the necessary skills and completes the proper training to treat a variety
          of injuries or diseases in the mouth, neck, face or jaw.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Osseointegration
        text: Osseointegration is the process in which a titanium dental implant fuses with the surrounding
          bone over several months after an oral health professional places the implant in the jaw.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Periodontist
        text: A periodontist is a specialist that studies and treats diseases and conditions that affect
          the supporting structure of teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Periodontal
        text: Literally "around the tooth"
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Prosthodontist
        text: A prosthodontist is a dental professional who goes through special training to focus on
          the design and placement of artificial teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Resorption
        text: Resorption is the process in which the body absorbs the calcium from the jaw since there
          are no tooth roots to cause the necessary stimulation and proceeds to use the calcium in other
          areas.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Subperiosteal Implants
        text: Subperiosteal implants are dental implants that consist of a metal framework that dental
          professionals attach under the gum tissue.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Transosteal (transosseous)
        text: Transosteal is a type of dental implant that includes threaded posts which penetrate the
          superior and inferior cortical bone plates of the jaw.
