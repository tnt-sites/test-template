    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list
      items:
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Alveolar Bone
        text: The alveolar bone is the bone surrounding the root of the tooth that keeps the tooth in
          place.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Clasp
        text: A clasp is a device that holds a removable partial denture prosthesis to the teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Denture Base
        text: The denture base is the part of the denture that connects the artificial teeth with the
          soft tissue of the gums.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Edentulous
        text: Edentulous is a term that applies to people who do not have any teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Periodontal Disease
        text: Periodontal disease is a condition that causes inflammation of the gingival tissues and
          membrane of the teeth, leading to tooth loss without professional treatment.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Pontic
        text: Pontic is another term for an artificial tooth on a fixed partial denture.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Rebase
        text: Rebase is the process of refitting denture prosthesis by replacing the base material.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Reline
        text: Reline is when a professional resurfaces the surface of the prosthesis with a new base material.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Resin/Acrylic
        text: Resin and Acrylic are resinous materials that can be components in a denture base.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Stomatitis
        text: Stomatitis is the inflammation of the tissue that is underlying a denture that does not
          fit properly. It can also result from other oral health factors.
