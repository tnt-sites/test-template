    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: Dental crowns.
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: When a tooth is missing a large portion of its enamel, a dental crown is the best type of
        restoration because it is a cap that completely surrounds the tooth. This way, you can bite down
        with full force and not worry about being uncomfortable or damaging the tooth further. This is
        most frequently used after a root canal or when the tooth is severely cracked or chipped. Fortunately,
        they now come in all-ceramic and ceramic-on-metal in addition to the traditional all-metal material.
        Patients getting crowns for restorations can have natural looking teeth after the fact.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: Dental veneers.
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: If a tooth is only slightly damaged with a small chip or crack, a veneer can be used to restore
        it. A veneer is a thin shell that is placed on top of a natural tooth and frequently used for
        restorations of the front teeth. The process of placing a veneer is similar to that of a dental
        crown only with less tooth preparation. If you live in Vista, schedule an appointment so that
        we can discuss both options with you.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: Tooth-colored fillings.
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: At North County Cosmetic and Implant Dentistry, we use tooth-colored fillings as a way to
        restore teeth that have had cavities. Once we remove the decayed area of the tooth, a filling
        is necessary to replace the missing enamel. Traditionally, this was done using silver amalgam
        but now we can complete dental restorations using materials that look natural so that when you
        smile, no one can tell that you had cavities.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
      text: Bondings.
      level: h3
      size: md
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text

        is popular when restoring baby teeth. Children, for example, do not need a dental veneer on a
        tooth that is going to fall out in short order. With this in mind, we can keep a baby tooth in
        place and ensure that it is functioning properly by restoring it using a bonding procedure. We
        can use a tooth-colored material so that it looks completely natural.

    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: In many cases, the patient wears temporary dentures while deciding how they want to permanently

        which cosmetic dentistry procedure to use.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: Dental bridges are popular for dental restorations. Patients can use a bridge to replace a
        tooth without undergoing surgery. In this procedure, a dental crown is placed on the two teeth
        that surround the missing one. The crowns are used as anchors to hold a gum-colored bridge in
        place that the new tooth is secured to. The entire bridge is created in a local dental lab and
        will look completely natural by blending in with the surrounding teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
      text: Patients may prefer to use dental implants instead of other treatment options. An implant
        is by far the most durable option for replacing a missing tooth. In this procedure we will implant
        a metal post under the gums and secure it to the jawbone. The bone and metal fuse together to
        create a synthetic root system that the new tooth is secured to. With this type of dental restoration,
        patients can maintain an active lifestyle without worrying about the impact on their new tooth.

    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list
      items:
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Allograft
        text: An allograft is a graft of tissue from a donor within the same species as the patient that
          is not a genetic relative.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Alveolar Ridge
        text: The alveolar ridge is the bony ridge for one of the jaw ridges located on the roof of the
          mouth or on the bottom of the mouth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Autograft
        text: An autograft is a graft of tissue from one portion of an individual’s body to another.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Bleachorexia
        text: Bleachorexia is when people continue to abuse teeth whitening solutions without consulting
          professionals, causing significant damage to their enamel and gums in the process.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Cap Splint
        text: A cap splint is a plastic or metallic device that professionals may use to treat mandible
          or maxillary fractures and cover crowns of teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Cosmetic Dentistry
        text: Cosmetic dentistry is generally used to refer to any dental work that improves the appearance
          (though not necessarily the function) of a person's teeth, gums and/or bite.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Crown Lengthening
        text: Crown lengthening is a surgical procedure that increases the extent of a tooth structure
          for restorative or esthetic purposes.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dental Crown
        text: A crown is an artificial tooth, usually consisting of porcelain, which covers the top of
          the implant to provide people with an aesthetically pleasing and fully-functional tooth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dentin Hypersensitivity
        text: Dentin hypersensitivity is when the enamel wears down on the tooth enough that the dentin
          faces exposure and will cause great levels of pain when a person applies pressure to the tooth
          or drinks hot/cold beverages.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dentist
        text: A dentist, also known as a dental surgeon, is a doctor who specializes in the diagnosis,
          prevention, and treatment of diseases and conditions of the oral cavity.
