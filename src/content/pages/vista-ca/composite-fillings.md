              Cosmetic dentistry is generally used to refer to any dental work that improves the appearance

          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Decay
            text:

              and tartar on the teeth.
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Dental Caries
            text:
              Dental caries are also known as cavities and result from a lack of proper oral hygiene leaving
              plaque that forms tiny holes in the teeth.
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Dental Checkup
            text:
              A dental checkup is an appointment that involves cleaning the teeth, identifying any signs
              of infection and removing said signs of infection at least once every six months in the office.
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Dental Filling
            text:
              A dental filling involves restoring the structure of the tooth by using metal, alloy, porcelain
              or plastic to fill the tooth.
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Dental Prophylaxis
            text:
              A dental prophylaxis is a professional and detailed cleaning that involves the removal of
              plaque, calculus and stains from the teeth.
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Dental Sealants
            text:
              Dental sealants contain a resinous material that we apply to the chewing surfaces of the posterior
              teeth to prevent dental caries.
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Dentist
            text:
              A dentist, also known as a dental surgeon, is a doctor who specializes in the diagnosis, prevention,
              and treatment of diseases and conditions of the oral cavity.
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Gingivitis
            text:
              Gingivitis is the inflammation of gum tissue that results from plaque, other infections in
              the mouth and poor oral hygiene.
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Preventive Dentistry
            text:
              Preventive dentistry is the dentistry that focuses on maintaining oral health in order to
              prevent the spread of plaque, the formation of tartar and infections in the mouth.
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Tartar
            text:
              Tartar forms when plaque builds up on the surface of the teeth and calcifies into a hard surface
              that is much more difficult to remove and will require professional treatment.
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
            title: Tooth Enamel
            text:
              Tooth enamel is the protective visible outer surface of a tooth that contains the dentin and
              dental pulp.
