  label: Definition of Denture Terminology
  contentSections:
  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading
    text: Definition of Denture Terminology
    level: h3
    size: md
  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list
    items:
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Alveolar Bone
      text: The alveolar bone is the bone surrounding the root of the tooth that keeps the tooth in place.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Bruxism/TMJ/Soreness
      text: Bruxism/TMJ soreness is the pain that follows excessive and unconscious teeth grinding and
        jaw clenching.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Clasp
      text: A clasp is a device that holds a removable partial denture prosthesis to the teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Denture Adhesive
      text: Denture adhesive help the dentures remain in the mouth and prevent them from falling out when
        the wearer laughs, speaks or eats.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Denture Base
      text: The denture base is the part of the denture that connects the artificial teeth with the soft
        tissue of the gums.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Denture Slippage
      text: Denture slippage is when dentures fall out of place or become loose since the jawbone beneath
        the gums lack stimulation and begin to diminish.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Edentulous
      text: Edentulous is a term that applies to people who do not have any teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Pontic
      text: Pontic is another term for an artificial tooth on a fixed partial denture.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Periodontal Disease
      text: Periodontal disease is a condition that causes inflammation of the gingival tissues and membrane
        of the teeth, leading to tooth loss without professional treatment.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Poor Fitting Dentures
      text: Poor fitting dentures are typically loose dentures that do not fit properly and fall out when
        the individual is eating, speaking or laughing.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Rebase
      text: Rebase is the process of refitting denture prosthesis by replacing the base material.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Reline
      text: Reline is when a professional resurfaces the surface of the prosthesis with a new base material.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Resin/Acrylic
      text: Resin and Acrylic are resinous materials that can be components in a denture base.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Speaking Difficulties
      text: Patients who do not seek proper dental treatment lose teeth will find that it becomes difficult
        to speak properly and may need artificial teeth to speak clearly again.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Stomatitis
      text: Stomatitis is the inflammation of the tissue that is underlying a denture that does not fit
        properly. It can also result from other oral health factors.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Xerostomia
      text: Xerostomia is also known as dry mouth, which is a condition where the salivary glands do not
        make enough saliva to keep the mouth moist.
