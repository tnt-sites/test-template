      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Bleachorexia
        text: Bleachorexia is when people continue to abuse teeth whitening solutions without consulting
          professionals, causing significant damage to their enamel and gums in the process.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Carbamide Peroxide
        text: Carbamide peroxide is a solution that contains hydrogen peroxide and can help to whiten
          teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Cosmetic Dentistry
        text: Cosmetic dentistry is generally used to refer to any dental work that improves the appearance
          (though not necessarily the function) of a person's teeth, gums and/or bite.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Deeper Teeth Stains
        text: Deeper teeth stains are stains that require professional oral health services and can result
          from certain foods, beverages or improper oral hygiene.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dentin Hypersensitivity
        text: Dentin hypersensitivity is when the enamel wears down on the tooth enough that the dentin
          faces exposure and will cause great levels of pain when a person applies pressure to the tooth
          or drinks hot/cold beverages.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dentist
        text: A dentist, also known as a dental surgeon, is a doctor who specializes in the diagnosis,
          prevention, and treatment of diseases and conditions of the oral cavity.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Hydrogen Peroxide
        text: Hydrogen peroxide is a chemical compound in teeth whitening solutions that patients use
          to whiten the shade of their teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Relief ACP
        text: Relief ACP stands for amorphous calcium phosphate, which helps to relieve the pain in sensitive
          teeth in a gel form.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Stained Dentin
        text: Stained dentin can result from certain foods and beverages with a lack of proper brushing.
          Dental professionals can provide whitening treatments to brighten the teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Surface Enamel
        text: The surface enamel is the hard outer shell that protects the dentin and pulp of the teeth.
          The enamel is the surface that people see when one smiles.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Surface Teeth Stains
        text: While surface teeth stains can be inevitable with age, certain foods and beverages will
          only promote discoloration and faded teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Teeth Whitening Concentrate
        text: Teeth whitening concentrate is the bleaching agent that whitens the shade of teeth, which
          is more effective from a professional and not a general store product.
