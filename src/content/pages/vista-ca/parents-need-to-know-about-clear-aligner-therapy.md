  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list
    items:
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Aligner Trays
      text: With Clear Aligner Therapy treatment, the patient will receive a series of aligner trays and
        swap out each one for the next one in the series every two weeks in order to gradually straighten
        the teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Blue Compliance Indicator (Clear Aligner Therapy Teen)
      text: The blue compliance indicator is a small blue dot on the aligners that will fade from blue
        to clear in order to indicate if the patient is wearing the aligner for the proper amount of time
        and where the patient is in the Clear Aligner Therapy process.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: ClinCheck® Software
      text: ClinCheck® software allows professionals to map out the straightening process the patient’s
        teeth will go through with Clear Aligner Therapy in great detail.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Gum Line
      text: The gum line is the line in the mouth where the teeth and gums meet. If a patient struggles
        with gum recession, then the gum tissue around the teeth may begin to wear away.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Incisal Ridges
      text: Incisal ridges are the portion of the crown of the tooth that makes up the incisal portion
        and can be a direct cause of overbite if they are extended too far.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: iTero Element® Scanner
      text: An iTero Element® scanner allows professionals to create a 3D image of the patient’s teeth
        in minutes and predict what it will take to move the teeth into proper alignment.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Malocclusion
      text: Malocclusion is the condition in which the upper and lower teeth do not meet properly when
        the bite is closed. Conditions of malocclusion can include overbite, underbite, crossbite and
        open bite; all of which are treatable with Clear Aligner Therapy.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Overbite
      text: An overbite is a type of malocclusion that occurs when the upper teeth jut out over the lower
        teeth, covering them and causing other issues.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Polyurethane Resin
      text: Polyurethane Resin is a USP Class VI medical grade, high molecular weight compound that makes
        up the aligners.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: SmartForce Attachments
      text: SmartForce attachments are small attachments that professionals place on the patient’s teeth
        before placing aligners to help move the teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Smart Track® Material
      text: Smart Track® material is in aligners to increase comfort, improve control of movement, increases
        the speed of treatment and applies a gentle force to the teeth over time.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Smile-Scan
      text: Smile-Scan analyzes the way a patient smiles by measuring the position of the mouth and eyes
        before giving a score.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Vivera® Retainers
      text: Vivera® retainers are a type of retainer that consists of clear plastic material and helps
        to maintain orthodontic corrections after a treatment.
