  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/heading

    level: h3
    size: md
  - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list
    items:
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Cosmetic Dentistry
      text: Cosmetic dentistry is generally used to refer to any dental work that improves the appearance
        (though not necessarily the function) of a person's teeth, gums and/or bite.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Crown Lengthening
      text: Crown lengthening is the process to expose more of the tooth by removing gum tissue in order
        to place a crown over the tooth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Decay
      text: Tooth decay is when the enamel of the tooth begins to decay and cause erosion from plaque
        and tartar on the teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dental Caries
      text: Dental caries are also known as cavities and result from a lack of proper oral hygiene leaving
        plaque that forms tiny holes in the teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dental Checkup
      text: A dental checkup is an appointment that involves cleaning the teeth, identifying any signs
        of infection and removing said signs of infection at least once every six months in the office.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dental Filling
      text: A dental filling involves restoring the structure of the tooth by using metal, alloy, porcelain
        or plastic to fill the tooth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dental Prophylaxis
      text: A dental prophylaxis is a professional and detailed cleaning that involves the removal of
        plaque, calculus and stains from the teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dentist
      text: A dentist, also known as a dental surgeon, is a doctor who specializes in the diagnosis, prevention,
        and treatment of diseases and conditions of the oral cavity.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Dental Surgery
      text: Dental surgery involves a large variety of procedures to help treat, remove, correct, add,
        change or repair teeth.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Full Mouth Rehabilitation
      text: Full mouth rehabilitation is a customized treatment that strives to optimize health and features
        of the entire mouth with multiple procedures.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Gingivitis
      text: Gingivitis is the inflammation of gum tissue that results from plaque, other infections in
        the mouth and poor oral hygiene.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Gum Contouring
      text: Gum contouring allows patients to change the position of the gum line in a patient's teeth
        by raising or lowering the line.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Tartar
      text: Tartar forms when plaque builds up on the surface of the teeth and calcifies into a hard surface
        that is much more difficult to remove and will require professional treatment.
    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
      title: Tooth Enamel
      text: Tooth enamel is the protective visible outer surface of a tooth that contains the dentin and
        dental pulp.
