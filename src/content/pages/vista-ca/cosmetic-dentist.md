    - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list

      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Allograft
        text: An allograft is a graft of tissue from a donor within the same species as the patient that
          is not a genetic relative.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Alveolar Ridge
        text: The alveolar ridge is the bony ridge for one of the jaw ridges located on the roof of the
          mouth or on the bottom of the mouth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Autograft
        text: An autograft is a graft of tissue from one portion of an individual’s body to another.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Bleachorexia
        text: Bleachorexia is when people continue to abuse teeth whitening solutions without consulting
          professionals, causing significant damage to their enamel and gums in the process.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Cap Splint
        text: A cap splint is a plastic or metallic device that professionals may use to treat mandible
          or maxillary fractures and cover crowns of teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Cosmetic Dentistry
        text: Cosmetic dentistry is generally used to refer to any dental work that improves the appearance
          (though not necessarily the function) of a person's teeth, gums and/or bite.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Crown Lengthening
        text: Crown lengthening is a surgical procedure that increases the extent of a tooth structure
          for restorative or esthetic purposes.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dental Crown
        text: A crown is an artificial tooth, usually consisting of porcelain, which covers the top of
          the implant to provide people with an aesthetically pleasing and fully-functional tooth.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dentin Hypersensitivity
        text: Dentin hypersensitivity is when the enamel wears down on the tooth enough that the dentin
          faces exposure and will cause great levels of pain when a person applies pressure to the tooth
          or drinks hot/cold beverages.
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/definition-list/definition-list-item
        title: Dentist
        text: A dentist, also known as a dental surgeon, is a doctor who specializes in the diagnosis,
          prevention, and treatment of diseases and conditions of the oral cavity.
