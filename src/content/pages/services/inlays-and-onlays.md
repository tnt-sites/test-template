    heading: What are Inlays & Onlays?
    text: An inlay or onlay is a type of restoration that is placed either inside of
      the cusps of a tooth or on the top of these cusps. This restoration is
      similar to a crown, but it is smaller in size and requires less drilling
      of the tooth. Dr. Adkins is able to perform both inlay and onlay
      procedures to restore beauty and integrity to your smile. This option is
      most ideal for patients with deep cavities, fillings or those who have
      broken or cracked the upper portion of a tooth.
