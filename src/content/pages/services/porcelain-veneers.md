    heading: What Can You Expect With the Porcelain Veneer Procedure?
    text: >-
      The first step is coming in for a consultation with Dr. Adkins. We will
      examine your teeth and determine if you are a good candidate for porcelain
      veneers. Next, the front of your teeth will be buffed slightly.
      Impressions, or molds are taken and sent to a local lab where the
      porcelain veneers are made for you. You’ll come back into the office to
      have the veneers placed. The veneers are smoothed and filed to perfect the
      fit and make them more comfortable. With proper care, you can expect the
      veneers to last for about 10 years. After this time, you can have the
      veneers replaced with brand new porcelain to again perfect the look of
      your smile.
