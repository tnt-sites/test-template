    heading: What is Periodontal Scaling and Root Planing?
    text: Periodontal scaling and root planing is typically done on patients who
      have gum disease. The cleaning is performed by a licensed hygienist who
      works to remove hardened plaque and calculus from both the upper surface
      of the teeth as well as in the gum pockets that have formed around each
      tooth. When you have gum disease, large pockets begin to form around the
      teeth, which harbor bacteria and plaque. This needs to be removed using a
      specialized dental cleaning known as a scaling and root planing.

    text: >-
      You will first receive local anesthetic to numb one side of your mouth.

      around the teeth and in the gum pockets. The pockets are rinsed and
      cleaned with medication. The roots of the teeth are then smoothed to
      prevent bacterial adhesion. You will then be scheduled to come back in and
      have the other side of your mouth professionally cleaned. The procedure
      takes less than an hour in our office and is a great step to preventing
      the progression of gum disease.
