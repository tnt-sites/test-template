    heading: What are Extractions?
    text: Extractions are done when one or more teeth need to be completely removed.
      Extractions may be done on teeth that are severely decayed, loose,
      cracked, broken, or fractured. The procedure may be done on baby teeth
      that are having difficulties falling out themselves. We may recommend
      having one or all of your wisdom teeth removed if they are impacted or do
      not have enough room to come up through the gums.

    text: >-
      You will first receive anesthetic or sedation of your choice. For more

      a time, we may suggest and recommend general anesthesia. Dr. Adkins will
