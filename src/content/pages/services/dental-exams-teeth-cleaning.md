    heading: Why are Dental Exams and Professional Cleanings Needed?
    text: A cleaning is specifically done to remove hardened plaque and buildup from
      the teeth. Over time and even with the best brushing techniques, plaque
      can harden on the surface of teeth, making it impossible to remove without
      the help of a hygienist. After a thorough cleaning, the hygienist will
      measure the gum pockets around each tooth to identify signs of gum disease
      or gingivitis. Dr. Adkins will then perform an exam to look for cavities,
      loose restorations, intra-oral problems and oral cancer.

      The first step of your preventative care appointment is the cleaning. The
