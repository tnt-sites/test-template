        html: What Does the Procedure Involve?
      - type: prose
        html: For teeth that require crowns, treatment often begins with a root canal if the decay has reached the tooth’s inner pulp or root system. During this procedure, the infected tissue is removed, and the root canals are thoroughly cleaned and sealed to prevent further infection. A stainless-steel post may be placed to provide additional support. Once the tooth is prepared, a custom crown—crafted to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth—is fitted over it, restoring both form and function.
      - type: prose
        html: If you’re missing one or more teeth, a bridge may be the ideal solution to close the gap. Bridges are secured by anchoring a prosthetic tooth (or teeth) to the adjacent healthy teeth, often using crowns for support. Most patients prefer permanent bridges that are cemented into place, providing a stable and natural-looking restoration. With regular checkups at Toothbar, you can avoid painful infections and abscesses, keeping your smile healthy and complete.
