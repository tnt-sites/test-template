      Dr. Elwira Tyra grew up in both Poland and Connecticut. She graduated from Poznan University of Medical Sciences in 2015 where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree, followed by a dental residency program at the same university. She moved back to the United States and completed her general practice dental residency at Berkshire Medical Center in Pittsfield, MA. She went on to work as an associate dentist through Community Health Programs for a year. Choosing to advance further in her career and education, she chose to attend Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental medicine, where she graduated with honors, earning a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in September 2020.
    subtext: |-
      Dr. Tyra has a long history of giving back to her community through volunteerism in both Poland and Massachusetts. She has set up dental workshops, fundraisers, blood drives and more. While at Boston University, she received recognition for her role as Outreach Chair in the BU chapter of Alpha Omega International Dental Society.
