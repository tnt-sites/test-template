      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <p>Taylor Dental Care is committed to making our website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We want every patient to be able to learn about our services, contact our office, and request an appointment with ease.</p>
      - type: heading
        html: Our Standard
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <p>We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people who are blind or have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, or have motor, speech, cognitive, or other disabilities.</p>
          <p>Steps we take include:</p>
          <ul>
          <li>Writing text alternatives for meaningful images.</li>
          <li>Using text and background colors with sufficient contrast.</li>
          <li>Structuring pages with clear headings so they work with screen readers.</li>
          <li>Making menus, links, and forms usable with a keyboard.</li>
          <li>Providing written transcripts alongside our treatment videos.</li>
          <li>Reviewing the site with automated and manual accessibility checks.</li>
          </ul>
      - type: heading
        html: Third-Party Content
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <p>Some features of our website are provided by other companies, such as our online appointment forms, embedded videos, and maps. We choose these providers with accessibility in mind, but we do not control their content and it may not fully meet the standard above. If any of these features gives you trouble, please contact us and we will help you directly.</p>
      - type: heading
        html: Need Help or Have Feedback?
