      - type: prose
        html: Yes! Professional whitening treatments, like those offered at Toothbar, are not only effective but also safe for your teeth. Some people worry that whitening will weaken their enamel or cause permanent sensitivity, but this isn’t the case. During the whitening process, hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide gently penetrates the enamel to lift stains. Any sensitivity you might experience is temporary, caused by minor dehydration of the teeth, which resolves naturally within a day or two.
