        contentSections:
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
            text: |-
              Many people wanting brighter smiles hesitate to schedule a cosmetic consultation because they’re concerned that professional teeth whitening procedures might hurt. Thankfully, that’s usually not the case! In fact, this popular service is considered non-invasive because it’s incredibly straightforward and there’s no need for needles or numbing.

              With that said, it’s possible to develop temporary sensitivity after your appointment because the bleach applied to your enamel expands the microscopic pores located there, allowing sensations, like hot or cold temperatures, to travel more easily to the tender roots inside. Any associated tenderness usually resolves on its own after a few days and can often be safely reduced with over-the-counter medications, such as acetaminophen or ibuprofen.
