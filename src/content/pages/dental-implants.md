    heading: What Are Dental Implants?
    subtext: Dental implants are small titanium posts that are surgically placed beneath the gumline to restore the root structure of missing teeth. Due to the biocompatible nature of the implants, they’re able to fuse with the jawbone through a natural process called osseointegration, allowing them to act as both an unwavering support system for restorations as well as provide the bone with necessary stimulation and blood flow to keep it from deteriorating with time.

    heading: The 4-Step Dental Implant Process
    subtext: |-
      *   **Initial Consultation** The first step of every dental implant treatment is an in-depth consultation with one of our implant dentists in Springfield. They will examine your mouth and help you decide whether dental implants are right for you.
      *   **Dental Implant Surgery** Following your approval to move forward with the procedure, our team will surgically place your dental implants within your mouth beneath the gumline.
      *   **Osseointegration & Abutment Placement** Over the following three to six months after your surgery, your dental implants will fuse with your bone through a process called osseointegration. Once that is completed, our team will secure small, metal abutments to the tops of the implants, which will anchor the restorations in place.
      *   **Attaching the Implant Restorations** Finally, when our team has received your prosthetic from our dental laboratory, we’ll secure it to the abutments, anchoring it firmly within the mouth.
