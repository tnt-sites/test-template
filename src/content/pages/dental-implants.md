      - type: prose
        html: "The dental implant process involves several carefully coordinated steps:"
      - type: prose
        html: <strong>Consultation and Exam:</strong> We begin with a thorough dental examination, including 3D imaging to assess the structure of your jawbone and plan your treatment. If a tooth extraction is necessary, we’ll perform it during this stage.
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <ul>
          <li><strong>Implant Placement:</strong> A small titanium or zirconia screw is surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as an anchor for the future crown. If additional bone support is needed, a bone graft may be performed before placing the implant.</li>
          </ul>
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <ul>
          <li><strong>Healing Period:</strong> After the implant is placed, your jawbone will need time to fuse with the implant through a process called osseointegration, which typically takes a few months.</li>
          </ul>
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <ul>
          <li><strong>Abutment Placement:</strong> Once the implant is stable, an abutment is attached to the post to connect the crown.</li>
          </ul>
      - type: heading
        html: Titanium vs. Zirconia Implants
      - type: prose
        html: Although the process requires multiple visits over several months, each step is essential to ensuring a long-lasting and secure implant. The result is a restoration that looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth.
      - type: prose
        html: Toothbar offers both titanium implants and zirconia implants, giving you options based on your needs and preferences. Titanium is widely used due to its strength and ability to fuse well with bone, but some patients may experience sensitivity or an allergic reaction. Zirconia implants are a metal-free alternative that reduces the risk of allergic reactions or corrosion. Both materials are durable and offer similar long-term success, but zirconia may offer additional aesthetic benefits for patients concerned about metal visibility through the gumline.
