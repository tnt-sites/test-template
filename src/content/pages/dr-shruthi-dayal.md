      Dr. Dayal is honored to join the Artisan Dental team in providing high quality comprehensive dental care.

      Dr. Dayal graduated from the University of Wisconsin-Madison in 2011 with a B.S. in Genetics. 

      Upon completion of her bachelor degree Dr. Dayal spent a year as an elementary school English teacher in Pyeontaek, South Korea. She went on to receive her D.D.S from New York University College of Dentistry in 2016.

      After receiving her D.D.S Dr. Dayal continued her training and education as a general practice resident at New York Presbyterian Weill-Cornell hospital in Manhattan working in a hospital- based clinic with rotations in oral surgery, pediatrics, anesthesia, and urgent care. As part of her training Dr. Dayal also worked alongside dental oncologists seeing patients in the Head and Neck Cancer department at Memorial Sloan Kettering.

      Dr. Dayal looks forward to creating quality relationships with her patients based on her ability to diagnose, treat, and educate them about their oral health. “I feel lucky to have chosen a dynamic and influential profession that requires long-lasting relationships with my patients to keep their smiles healthy and beautiful. I truly believe in the healing power of dentistry and its ability to change lives.”

      Dr. Dayal is truly passionate about her career, and is on a mission to change the public perception and fear about Dentistry. “I’m here to prove that dentistry doesn’t have to be surrounded by anxiety and fear.”

      When she’s not fixing smiles, Dr. Dayal enjoys reading, camping, and hiking. She is also an avid food connoisseur who enjoys trying new restaurants and recipes.
