          <p>Chapel Hill Dental Arts is actively working to improve digital accessibility across our website in response to WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines and ADA-related best practices for public-facing businesses.</p>
          <h2>Current Accessibility Work</h2>
          <ul>
          <li>Reviewing keyboard access, headings, forms, links, images, and color contrast</li>
          <li>Providing accessible names for maps, social links, navigation controls, and interactive elements</li>
          <li>Publishing our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices as HTML while retaining the source PDF as a download</li>
          <li>Publishing a dedicated website Privacy Policy, separate from the HIPAA Notice</li>
          <li>Providing dedicated footer links to this ADA efforts page, our Accessibility Statement, Privacy Policy, and HIPAA Notice</li>
          <li>Reviewing older pages and third-party widgets for barriers that require additional remediation</li>
          </ul>
          <h2>Ongoing Work</h2>
          <p>We continue to review interior pages, downloadable documents, forms, navigation, and third-party embeds. Remaining items are tracked and remediated on a rolling basis.</p>
          <h2>Need Help?</h2>
          <p>If you encounter a barrier on our website, call <a href="tel:+17323650123">(732) 365-0123</a> or use our <a href="/contact/">contact form</a>. We will respond as promptly as possible and provide the information or assistance you need.</p>
