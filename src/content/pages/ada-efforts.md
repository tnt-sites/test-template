    <p>Artisan Dental is actively working to improve digital accessibility across our website in response to WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines and ADA-related best practices for public-facing businesses.</p>
    <h2>Recent Improvements</h2>
    <ul>
    <li>Skip navigation link for keyboard and screen-reader users</li>
    <li>Improved form labels on appointment and contact forms, including third-party verification fields</li>
    <li>Accessible names for maps, social links, and mobile navigation controls</li>
    <li>Descriptive alternative text for key footer and affiliation images</li>
    <li>Color-contrast remediation for gold buttons, headings, and text on photo backgrounds</li>
    <li>Homepage YouTube embeds configured to load with closed captions enabled</li>
    <li>HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices published as HTML (PDF remains available as a download)</li>
    <li>Footer links to this ADA efforts page, our Accessibility Statement, Privacy Policy, and HIPAA Notice</li>
    </ul>
    <h2>Ongoing Work</h2>
    <p>We continue to review interior pages, downloadable documents, blog pagination, and third-party embeds. Remaining items are tracked and remediated on a rolling basis.</p>
    <h2>Need Help?</h2>

We will respond as promptly as possible and provide the information or assistance you need.</p>
