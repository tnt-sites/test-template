      - type: heading
        html: Current Accessibility Work
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <ul>
          <li>Reviewing keyboard access, headings, forms, links, images, and color contrast</li>
          <li>Providing accessible names for maps, social links, navigation controls, and interactive elements</li>
          <li>Publishing our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices as HTML while retaining the source PDF as a download</li>
          <li>Publishing a dedicated website Privacy Policy, separate from the HIPAA Notice</li>
          <li>Providing dedicated footer links to this ADA efforts page, our Accessibility Statement, Privacy Policy, and HIPAA Notice</li>
          <li>Reviewing older pages and third-party widgets for barriers that require additional remediation</li>
          </ul>
