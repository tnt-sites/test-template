      #### **Patient Care Coordinator**

      I have been in the dental field since 2016. I’m so grateful that the dental world found me. I have always been teeth obsessed and loved getting my teeth cleaned. Dentistry isn’t always cosmetic and has great overall health benefits as well. My favorite thing is seeing the outcome and confidence that people leave with after each appointment. Sometimes going to the dentist is like going to the gym, the hardest part is getting there.  
      What I love about Artisan Dental is the teamwork between the staff. The care for the community, and the patients as a whole. I’m very blessed to be a part of the Artisan Dental community. What I’m looking forward to the most is building relationships with all the patients. We truly have the best ones!

      When I’m not at work I enjoy trying new restaurants, especially Wisconsin supper clubs. All my teeth are sweet teeth so I try to make my desserts low carb friendly. I enjoy reading, but these days it’s mainly children’s books. I take pride in my bargain shopping abilities. I love going up north to Boulder Junction and spending quality time with my family, friends, and all the dogs.
