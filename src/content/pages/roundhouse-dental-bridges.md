      A roundhouse dental bridge is an innovative solution for
      individuals missing multiple teeth. Unlike traditional dental
      bridges, which rely on crowns placed on adjacent teeth for
      support, a roundhouse dental bridge is anchored using dental
      implants. This method offers enhanced stability and functionality,
      closely mimicking natural teeth.


      In a roundhouse dental bridge procedure, dental implants are
      surgically placed in the jawbone to serve as sturdy foundations.
      Once the implants are securely in place and have fused with the
      bone, a custom-made denture is attached to the implants.

      - **Natural appearance and function:** The prosthetic teeth are
