      ### Feel Your Best

      Enhance the level of comfort and confidence you have in your smile at work, at home and with friends.

      ### Enhance the Beauty of Your Smile

      Upgrade and refine the shape, color, brightness and proportions of your teeth to an aesthetic you desire.

      ### Relieve Pain and Stabilize Your Bite

      Teeth can become injured, worn and weakened by a misalignment as they come together during the chewing process. Partial or Full-Mouth Restoration can correct this, reducing or eliminating pain and prolonging the life of your teeth.

      ### Strengthen Your Teeth

      Partial and Full-Mouth Restoration can include the restoration of your oral health using veneers, dental implants, crowns, Invisalign and the most contemporary techniques and materials.

      ### Speak More Clearly

      When teeth are missing, broken, chipped or dentures are improperly fitted, speaking can become a challenge. With a partial or Full-Mouth Restoration, the natural shape of your mouth can be restored, enabling you to more easily communicate with those around you.

      ### Chew Better

      It’s often difficult to chew when your teeth are misaligned or missing. By restoring and aligning your teeth, you can eat without pain or discomfort and enjoy your favorite foods again.

      ### Stop Bone Loss and Decay

      Damaged or missing teeth can affect your overall oral health. When teeth are missing or decayed, there is a risk of bone loss. By replacing teeth with a dental implant, veneers or crowns, you are stabilizing your oral health and jaw bone, enabling you to maintain your facial structure around the mouth and setting the stage for oral health in the future.

      ## Full-Mouth Rehabilitation Procedures

      A Full-Mouth Rehabilitation can consist of one or more procedures. Damaged or decayed teeth can be restored with crowns or repaired with root canals and missing teeth can be replaced with dental implants, bridges or partial dentures. Treatment may also involve veneers or dental bonding to improve the color and shape of existing teeth. Whatever procedure you need, Dr. Andersen can provide it. Your new smile can include:

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/1-1.jpg)

      #### Porcelain Veneers

      Porcelain Veneers are thin porcelain coverings that are bonded to the front of your teeth to change their shape, size and/or color. Porcelain Veneers can usually be completed in two visits and can make a dramatic change to your smile.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/2-1.jpg)

      #### Dental Crowns

      A dental crown is used when the tooth needs cosmetic changes but is also in need of structural repair. A crown can also be used on top of an implant post to replace a missing tooth.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/8.jpg)

      #### Dental Bridges

      A dental bridge is a series of connected crowns that is used to replace one or more missing teeth. Although they have certain disadvantages compared to implants, bridges are a suitable option for replacing missing teeth in certain circumstances.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/4-1.jpg)

      #### Dental Bonding

      Dental bonding is a tooth-colored dental resin that can be used to correct flaws such as chips and minor cracks in a tooth. It can also be used to change the size, shape or color of your teeth, but is not as durable and long-lasting as veneers.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/5-1.jpg)

      #### Laser Gum Sculpting for a Gummy Smile or Uneven Gum Line

      Uneven gumlines and gums that are too large around your teeth can cause your smile to look unattractive and in some cases can affect your overall oral health. For most gum reshaping procedures the doctor uses a dental laser as a minimally-invasive way to sculpt the gum tissue.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/6-1.jpg)

      #### Invisalign & SureSmile® Teeth Straightening

      Effective clear aligner treatment designed to fit your lifestyle. Clear aligners are designed to orchestrate each step of your smile transformation with precision.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/7-1.jpg)

      #### Dental Implants

      Dental implants are considered the modern solution for replacing missing teeth. A titanium post is inserted into your jaw and topped by a crown which acts, looks and feels just like a natural tooth. We are usually able to provide all stages of your implant treatment in-house.
