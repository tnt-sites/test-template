      - The Artisan Dental Wellness Series is a quarterly wellness and community building series, designed to foster whole person wellness, enjoyment and a sense of connection among Artisan Dental stakeholders (patients, staff, suppliers, community and planet).
      - Every quarter, Artisan Dental sponsors a Wellness Series event, featuring an expert or experience designed to support greater levels of health and happiness.  All Artisan Dental patients, together with a friend are welcome to participate, free of charge, on first come, first reserved basis for each event.
      - You can learn about upcoming events by visiting this page of our website. Notices will be placed on our Facebook & Twitter pages. Artisan Dental patients will also receive an announcement in our monthly e-newsletter.
