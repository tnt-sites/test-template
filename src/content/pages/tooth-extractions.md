      Tooth extractions are sometimes necessary to protect your overall oral health when a tooth cannot be saved with other treatments, or if it poses a threat to the other teeth. A few common reasons for extraction include:

      - Severe tooth decay or damage has compromised the tooth beyond repair.
      - Advanced gum disease is affecting the stability of the tooth.
      - A tooth has become impacted, meaning it failed to erupt properly.
      - Teeth need to be removed to make space for orthodontic treatment.
