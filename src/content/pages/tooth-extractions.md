      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <p>Tooth extractions are often the last resort when other treatments—like root canals—are no longer effective. Here are a few common scenarios where tooth extraction may be the best solution:</p>
          <ul>
          <li><strong>Severe Decay or Infection: </strong>When a tooth is too damaged for a filling or crown and even a root canal cannot save it, extraction is often the next step.</li>



          <li><strong>Failed Root Canal: </strong>A poorly cleaned or improperly sealed root canal can leave the tooth vulnerable to infection and structural damage, eventually leading to the need for extraction.</li>



          <li><strong>Accidents or Trauma: </strong>In emergencies, when a tooth is broken beyond repair, immediate extraction may be required. If possible, place any broken pieces in a glass of milk and visit us as soon as possible—we may still be able to save the tooth with bonding or a pulp cap.</li>



          <li><strong>Wisdom Teeth: </strong>Wisdom teeth often become impacted or cause crowding in the jaw, leading to discomfort and gum irritation. Removing impacted wisdom teeth can prevent future complications and protect the alignment of your teeth.</li>
          </ul>
