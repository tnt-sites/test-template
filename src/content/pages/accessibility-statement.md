  - _component: page-sections/info-blocks/accessibility-statement
    heading: Our Commitment to Accessibility
    intro: >-
      R2 Center for Dentistry is committed to making our website usable by as
      many people as possible, including patients who use assistive technology
      such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, or keyboard-only navigation.
    conformanceTitle: Conformance Status
    conformanceBody: >-
      The [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines
      (WCAG)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) define
      requirements for making web content more accessible to people with
      disabilities. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA.


      This website is **partially conformant** with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Partially
      conformant means that some parts of the content do not yet fully meet the
      standard. We are working to identify and correct those areas.
    sections:
      - title: What We Have Done
        body: >-
          - Text and background colours are checked for sufficient contrast.

          - Images that convey information carry descriptive alternative text.

          - The site can be navigated using a keyboard alone, and visible focus
          styles show which element is selected.

          - Headings, lists, and landmarks are used so screen readers can move
          through a page in a meaningful order.

          - The layout adapts to different screen sizes and to increased browser
          text size without loss of content.
      - title: Known Limitations
        body: >-
          Despite our efforts, some limitations remain. Content supplied by
          third parties — including embedded maps, review feeds, and appointment
          booking tools — is not under our direct control and may not fully meet
          WCAG 2.1 Level AA. If any of this content prevents you from completing
          a task, please contact us and we will provide the information or
          service another way.
    feedbackTitle: Tell Us About a Problem
    feedbackBody: >-
      If you encounter a barrier on this site, or need information in a
      different format, please get in touch. Tell us the page address and what
      happened, and we will respond as quickly as we can and arrange another way
      to help you in the meantime.
