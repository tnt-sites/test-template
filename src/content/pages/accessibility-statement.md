          <p>Taylor Dental Care is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, including people with disabilities. We work to improve the usability of taylordentalsmiles.com and to align our digital content with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.</p>
          <p>This statement describes our efforts, known limitations, and how to request assistance. It is not a guarantee of full conformance with any particular standard or law.</p>
      - type: heading
        html: Our Commitment
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <p>We strive to make information about our practice, services, and patient resources available to everyone. Where barriers exist, we will make reasonable efforts to provide the same information in an alternative accessible format upon request.</p>
      - type: heading
        html: Measures We Take
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <ul>
          <li>Ongoing review and remediation of website pages for keyboard access, form labels, heading structure, link names, image alternatives, and color contrast</li>
          <li>Labeling downloadable documents and providing contact options when a format is not fully accessible</li>
          <li>Working with our website vendors to improve shared templates, navigation, and content patterns across the site</li>
          <li>Responding to accessibility feedback and prioritizing fixes that remove barriers for visitors</li>
          </ul>
      - type: heading
        html: Patient Forms and Documents
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <p>If you need patient information or a document in an alternative format, or help completing forms before your visit, please contact our office and we will assist you.</p>
          <p><a href="/contact-us/">Contact Our Office</a> · <a href="tel:+18015659411">(801) 565-9411</a></p>
      - type: heading
        html: Third-Party Content
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <p>Some features on this website are provided by third parties, such as Google Maps, appointment scheduling, analytics, payment or financing partners, video players, and form services. We do not fully control the accessibility of third-party tools. If a third-party feature creates a barrier, contact us and we will help you complete the same task another way whenever possible.</p>
      - type: heading
        html: Known Limitations
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <ul>
          <li>Some older content, images, or interactive elements may still need further remediation</li>
          <li>Third-party widgets and embeds may not meet the same accessibility standards as our own pages</li>
          <li>Some downloadable PDFs may not be fully tagged for assistive technology; we will provide the information another way upon request</li>
          </ul>
      - type: heading
        html: Feedback and Assistance
      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <p>If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, or if you need information in an alternative format, please let us know. Include the page URL, a description of the problem, and your preferred contact method when possible.</p>
