    anchorName: ''
    subtext: >-
      A dental crown, or cap, is a restoration that can be used for a
      number of purposes. One of the most common uses of a dental crown
      is to restore a tooth that is significantly damaged or decayed.
      The crown is custom made to fit over the entire tooth, starting at
      the gumline, in order to restore the tooth to its original shape
      and size.


      Dental crowns can be made of EMAX, zirconia, or gold; zirconia
      crowns are popular because they match the natural color and
      appearance of your teeth.
