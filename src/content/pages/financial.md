      #### Pay Now Directly

      #### Care Credit

      #### Option 1

      -   The **[Artisan Dental Health Plan](/health-plans/)** offers significant savings on regular **[teeth cleanings](/education/teeth-cleaning/)**, diagnostic radiographs and dental exams as well as 15% savings on **[restorative](/category/services/restorative/)** and **[cosmetic](/category/services/cosmetic/)** dental services.  Click **[here](/health-plans/)** to learn more.

      #### Option 2

      -   We accept checks, cash or major credit cards Visa, MasterCard, and Discover.

      #### Option 3

      -   As a service to our patients, we are pleased to offer a financing option: **[CareCredit](https://www.carecredit.com/go/CHG554/)**. You can finance 100% of your dental care and there are no upfront costs, no annual fees, and no pre-payment penalties. So, you can begin your treatment today and conveniently pay with low, monthly payments.

      CareCredit offer a full range of payment plans so you can find one that works best for you. With the promotional no interest payment plans there are no interest charges if you pay your balance in full within the specified time period. For longer payment terms, 24-60 month plan options with low monthly payments are available.

      These options can be used by the whole family for ongoing treatment without having to reapply. And by using one of these financing options for your dental care, you can save your other credit cards for household or unplanned expenses. It only takes a few minutes to apply and you’ll receive an online decision in seconds!
