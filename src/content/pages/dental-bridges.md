      - type: heading
        html: What is a dental bridge?
      - type: prose
        html: A dental bridge, or fixed dental bridge, is a porcelain restoration made to replace one or more teeth in a row. It is made by a dentist and bonded into place on the surrounding teeth.
      - type: heading
        html: How does a dental bridgework?
      - type: prose
        html: A dental bridge works by “bridging the gap” left behind after a tooth has been lost or extracted. It is created using ceramic materials that mimic the appearance of natural tooth enamel. Crowns are used on each side to bond onto adjacent teeth, and false teeth called pontics are fused in-between. When the restoration is bonded to the supporting teeth, the pontic fills the gap and appears like a tooth in its place.
      - type: heading
        html: Are dental bridges permanent?
      - type: prose
        html: Dental bridges are more permanent than a partial or full denture but not as long-lasting as a dental implant. That is what makes it a popular choice for many of our patients, especially those who do not have the funds to move forward with placing a dental implant.
      - type: heading
        html: Can a dental bridge last a lifetime?
      - type: prose
        html: Dental bridges can last many years with proper care, but it is unusual for them to last a lifetime. They often need to be replaced over the years.
