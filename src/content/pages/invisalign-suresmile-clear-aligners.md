      ## Advantages of Invisalign® and SureSmile® over traditional metal braces

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/3.jpg)

      There are numerous advantages of using Invisalign® or SureSmile® for your teeth straightening, including:

      -   They are virtually invisible, so most people won’t notice you’re straightening your teeth
      -   You can take them out to eat, so there’s no worry about food restrictions or getting food stuck in them
      -   There’s no sharp or uncomfortable metal in your mouth, no wires or rubber bands
      -   They are unnoticeable when laughing, talking or eating with other people
      -   It’s easy to keep your teeth and gums healthy – simply pop the aligner out to brush and floss

      ## How Invisalign and SureSmile Work

      Invisalign treatment for adults usually takes about 12 months, about the same amount of time as braces. With SureSmile, you can often reduce that time to approximately 6 months. Because the aligners can be removed at any time such as for special occasions or prior to eating meals, they have a minimum impact on your lifestyle. Invisalign and SureSmile fit well into your busy adult schedule as there are no frequent visits for wire tightening. You simply change aligners every two weeks and can see the positive changes as you go.

      At the completion of each SureSmile treatment, we offer free full-mouth teeth whitening.

      ## Conditions Artisan Dental Can Fix with Invisalign & SureSmile

      Invisalign and SureSmile treatment can fix a wide variety of problems with teeth and bite alignment, from mild cases of crooked teeth to much more difficult problems involving serious bite issues.

      Here are some of the main conditions Artisan Dental can fix with Invisalign or SureSmile:

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/overly-crowded-teeth1.png)

      #### Overly crowded

      When teeth are crowded in the mouth, it can be difficult to clean the teeth properly. This can promote tooth decay and cause a higher risk of gum disease.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/overbite11.png)

      #### Overbite

      An overbite can cause excessive wear on the lower teeth, gum irritation and can also cause jaw problems.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/underbite11.png)

      #### Underbite

      This occurs when the bottom teeth protrude beyond the upper teeth and can interfere with the normal function of front teeth and molars, and can lead to jaw problems.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/spaced-teeth11.png)

      #### Gaps in Teeth

      Gaps can trap food and bacteria and lead to tooth decay and gum disease. It can also cause teeth to shift into the empty spaces and increase misalignment.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/openbite2.png)

      #### Open Bite

      An open bite can interfere with chewing and can cause speech impairment and jaw problems.

      ![](/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/crossbite11.png)

      #### Crossbite

      This is a symptom of jaw misalignment, which can cause worn-down teeth, gum disease or both.
