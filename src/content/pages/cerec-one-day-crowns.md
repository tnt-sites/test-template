        html: What is CEREC dentistry?
      - type: prose
        html: CEREC is a specialized system that uses 3D digital imaging of a prepared tooth to create a restoration right in the dental office. The system includes a milling machine that can make a dental restoration such as a crown out of a block of porcelain while patients wait. That eliminates messy impressions and the time needed to send them out to ceramists who might reside states away. It also eliminates the need for a temporary restoration while patients wait for their final restoration. Patients save time, energy, and money with one-visit crowns while obtaining a beautiful, durable repair.
