    heading: The Process of Getting Veneers
    subtext: |-
      Several steps are required when receiving dental veneers. After meeting with a member of our team for an initial consultation, you will move on to the next phase of treatment, which is the removal of a small portion of tooth enamel. This step is necessary not only to ensure that your veneers lie flat once they are in place but also to give the bonding agent the best chance to adhere the restorations to the front side of your teeth.

      You will then have temporary veneers put in place after impressions are taken and sent to a dental lab. Technicians will build them based on the desired size, shape, and color before sending them back to our office.

      During the few weeks between appointments, you will experience what life will be like with veneers. Although your temporary ones will not look the same as the final product, they will help protect your teeth until it’s time to receive your permanent veneers.

      When they are ready, we will remove the temporary restorations and carefully position each new one until your smile is complete.
