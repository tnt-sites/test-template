      #### **About Dr. Rehermann:**

      Dr. Mark Rehermann is a comprehensive dentist whose commitment to serving others and passion for oral health and facial esthetics has made him an exemplary professional in his field.

      Originally from Pennsylvania, Dr. Rehermann completed his undergraduate studies at Penn State University. His affinity for science and a desire to make a positive impact on people’s lives led him to pursue a career in dentistry. Graduating magna cum laude in 2012, he demonstrated academic excellence from the outset. He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine in 2016.  

      After dental school, Dr. Rehermann embarked on an extraordinary path of service by joining the U.S. Army. He was awarded a scholarship from the military’s Health Professional Scholarship Program and completed a one-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency.

      After his residency, Dr. Rehermann was stationed in various locations, including Kentucky, South Korea, and Georgia. He encountered a diverse range of oral health issues and gained invaluable experience in providing dental care under challenging circumstances. This exposure to different cultures, communities, and dental practices has made him a versatile and well-rounded practitioner.

      In 2020, Dr. Rehermann moved to Madison, Wisconsin, where he joined our team. With a focus on providing comprehensive and compassionate dental services, he has become a trusted professional in the Madison community, known for his gentle chairside manner and commitment to patient education.

      Dr. Rehermann has also received specialized training from the American Academy of Facial Esthetics (AAFE) for administering botox and dermal fillers. This additional expertise allows him to offer a wider range of esthetic services to his patients with the goal of enhancing their overall dental experience.

      Based upon reviews by local dentists and specialists Dr. Rehermann was selected as a “**Top Dentist**” **from 2026**.

      Dr. Rehermann’s journey exemplifies his passion for dentistry and his dedication to serving others. Through his education, military service, and private practice, he has become a highly skilled and compassionate dentist. He strives to make a positive impact on the oral health and overall well-being of his patients, while upholding the highest standards of dental care.
