Dr. Peck completed his general practice residency at Danbury Hospital in Danbury, Connecticut and regularly upgrades his expertise as a dentist in Springfield by taking advanced dental courses at the distinguished Las Vegas Institute. He also completed a course with “Dentist to the Stars” Larry Rosenthal in Palm Beach, Florida.
    subtext: |-

Dr. Peck also specializes in various cosmetic and restorative procedures, including dental implants, crowns and bridges, and dental veneers. He and his team are devoted to providing only the best quality of dental care and excellent customer service to his patients.
