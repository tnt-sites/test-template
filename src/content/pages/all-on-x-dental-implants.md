- _component: page-sections/services/service-split
  heading: 'All-on-X VS Traditional Dental Implants: What are the Benefits?'
  anchorName: ''
  subtext: 'Traditional dental implants replace missing teeth one at a time, with each implant supporting
    its own crown. This is absolutely a viable option when only one or two teeth are missing, but replacing
    an entire arch this way would require several implants, not to mention extensive bone grafting and
    a much longer and more costly treatment process.


    All-on-X offers a smarter alternative for patients missing most or all of their teeth. The key difference
    is that a full arch of replacement teeth can actually be supported with four to six strategically
    placed implant posts. Because the implants are angled to take advantage of the existing, denser bone
    in the jaw, many patients may be able to avoid the need for bone grafting entirely.'
