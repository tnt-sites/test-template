      heading: Scaling &amp; Root Planing
      level: h3
      text: '<p>When gum infection sets in, irritation from calculus or tartar on the tooth roots can cause the gums to recede from the teeth. This causes large pockets to form that give bacteria more places to hide and speeds the progress of periodontal disease. Scaling and root planing can remove the calculus deposits on the tooth roots below the gum line. When combined with infection treatment, this can allow the gums to heal and reattach to the tooth roots. This procedure is one of the most effective treatments for moderate to later stages of periodontal disease.</p>
