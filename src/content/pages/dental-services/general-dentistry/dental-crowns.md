      text: Dental crowns are fitted to completely cover a tooth. First, the tooth is prepared for the crown by removing some of the tooth material. An impression is made of the tooth and bite to create a crown that will be the same size and shape as the tooth. A laboratory creates the crown, which is adhered to the tooth. Crowns are used for repair, as well as for cosmetics as part of a smile restoration.
