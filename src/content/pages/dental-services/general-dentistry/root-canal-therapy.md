    - _bt: prose
      heading: When is Root Canal Therapy Needed?
      level: h3
      text: Root canal therapy is used when the interior, or pulp, of the tooth is compromised. This can be due to decay that has reached the inner canal of the tooth or when trauma or other problems have damaged the core. The pulp and nerve roots of the tooth are cleared from the inner canal and the interior is disinfected to stop any infection. Once cleaned, the canal is packed with a sterile material, resealed and capped to protect it from further damage.
