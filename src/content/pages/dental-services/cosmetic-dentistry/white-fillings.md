      heading: What is White Resin?
      level: h3
      text: There are many different types of dental composite resin available for fillings and bonding. Most contain plastics, glass and ceramics and are applied in a putty form to the teeth. Most require a special light to dry the resin into a hard material. Once hardened, the surface can be smoothed and polished so it matches the tooth. Composite resin is very durable and can last for many years when used in fillings and dental bonding.
