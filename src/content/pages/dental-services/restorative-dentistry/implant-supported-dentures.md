Columbine Creek Dentistry offers implant supported dentures for our patients that need a full mouth restoration. This advanced method of dental restoration can give you back a complete smile that stays in place without adhesives.</p>

        <p>Some of the biggest downfalls of dentures are due to the possible movement while wearing them. Dentures can slip and cause embarrassing moments in social situations. Shifting of the dentures can cause discomfort and it can be difficult to eat certain foods. The adhesives used to hold them in place can be gritty and unpleasant to use.</p>

        <p>With implant supported dentures, you do not need to worry about adhesives or slipping incidents. The dentures are held in place by dental implants, giving them a secure foothold for more natural function. There are several different options for supported dentures; some are removable while others are permanently secured in place. One of the options we offer at Columbine Creek Dentistry is the popular All-on-4 Dental Implants.</p>
