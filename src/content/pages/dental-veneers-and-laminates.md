      - type: image
        source: /wp-content/uploads/2021/08/close-up-of-porcelain-veneer-on-tooth-in-sandy-v1.jpg

      - type: prose
        html: |-
          <p>Veneers are thin, custom-made porcelain restorations that cover the front surface of a tooth. They’re designed to disguise dental imperfections and create a more attractive smile. Because porcelain closely mimics natural enamel, including the “wet” translucence of teeth, veneers blend seamlessly and can totally transform the appearance of your smile.&nbsp; They’re perfect for many kinds of cosmetic dental issues, including:</p>
          <ul>
          <li>Misaligned or crooked teeth</li>
          <li>Tooth gaps</li>
          <li>Unusually shaped teeth</li>
          <li>Permanent stains</li>
          <li>Chips and cracks</li>
          </ul>

          <p>Dental laminates function similarly to veneers but are even thinner, requiring minimal to no enamel removal. They’re excellent for patients who want a more conservative or reversible cosmetic treatment that still produces brilliant results.</p>
          <p>If you want to correct minor chips, cracks, or discoloration without altering the enamel of your natural teeth, dental laminates could be the perfect solution.</p>

        html: The Process of Getting Veneers
      - type: image
        source: /wp-content/uploads/2021/08/sandy-patient-smiling-during-veneer-consultation-v1.jpg
        alt: Sandy patient smiling during veneer consultation.

      - type: prose

          <p>Getting veneers begins with a thorough consultation, where <a href="/dr-michelle-taylor-dds/">Dr. Michelle</a> or <a href="/dr-glenn-taylor-dds/">Dr. Glenn</a> will discuss your goals, including which cosmetic issues you’d like to improve. Once a treatment plan is set, a small amount of enamel is gently removed from each treated tooth to create space and ensure a natural fit.</p>
          <p>Next, impressions are taken and sent to a trusted dental laboratory, where your new veneers are fabricated by skilled dental technicians. Before you leave, we’ll bond temporary veneers over your teeth to protect them and give you a preview of what your smile could look like.</p>
          <p>At your next visit, each veneer is bonded securely in place. Our team will make minor adjustments as needed to ensure your bite feels comfortable and your smile looks flawless.</p>
          <p>For laminates, the process is similar, but enamel removal is minimal or sometimes unnecessary.</p>

          <li><strong>Treat Multiple Flaws:</strong> With one treatment, you can correct decades of cosmetic damage and discoloration.</li>
          <li><strong>Durable Materials:</strong> Veneers can last 15 years or longer, while laminates usually last around a decade if well cared for.</li>
