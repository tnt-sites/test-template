      - type: prose
        html: When it comes to caring for children, it takes special training to ensure children are relaxed in the dental chair and confident in taking care of their smiles. We also know that it is vital that the first few dental visits for a child go well to ensure they are comfortable and have a positive experience with our dental team. Many children who have negative experiences at the dentist will have dental anxiety later in life. That can keep them from getting the care they need to ensure healthy smiles for life. A children’s dentist also performs specific evaluations and procedures that a regular adult dentist may not be as well-versed in providing.&nbsp;

          <p>Below are a few of the more common services that are provided to children in our practice:</p>
