      #### Clinical Assistant Team Lead

      Dental Assisting was a career that found me! And has been the best opportunity I could have ever asked for. I have been a clinical assistant for over 10 years and continue to develop new skills every day! I love the fact that Artisan Dental focuses not only on the clinical side of dentistry but also the community. I believe our B Corp certification is amazing and I am proud to be part of such an important way to start taking care of our planet!

      I am a flea market junkie! And love treasure hunting with my college daughters. I have been married for over 25 years and have lived in Cottage Grove for most of my life! We have 4 crazy German Shorthaired Pointers that I cannot live without! I love to run and be outside as much as possible, though I’m still not sold on the winter thing around here!
