        html: What Are Veneers?
      - type: prose
        html: Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-made shells that cover the front surface of your teeth to improve their appearance. Made from durable porcelain or resin, veneers are designed to change the shape, color, length, or size of your teeth. These shells are bonded permanently to your natural teeth, providing a long-lasting, beautiful smile. Veneers offer a natural look, and porcelain veneers are especially resistant to staining, making them a preferred choice for many patients.

        html: The Process of Getting Dental Veneers
      - type: prose
        html: "The process of applying veneers typically involves three visits:"
      - type: prose
        html: During your initial consultation, our team will assess the health of your teeth and gums to ensure veneers are the right option. We’ll also discuss your cosmetic goals and develop a personalized treatment plan.
      - type: prose
        html: At the next visit, we’ll prepare your teeth by removing a thin layer of enamel to ensure the veneers fit naturally. We’ll then take digital impressions of your teeth, which are sent to a specialized lab to create your custom veneers. The lab typically takes 2-4 weeks to complete the veneers. In the meantime, temporary veneers may be placed to protect your teeth.
      - type: prose
        html: Once your veneers are ready, we’ll check the fit and color to ensure they match your natural teeth perfectly. If everything looks great, we’ll clean your teeth and apply a bonding agent. The veneers are then attached using a special dental adhesive and hardened with ultraviolet light.
      - type: prose
        html: After placement, we’ll polish the veneers to create a seamless finish. If necessary, a follow-up appointment may be scheduled to make minor adjustments and ensure everything feels comfortable.
