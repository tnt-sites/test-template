      #### Why Artisan Dental for Botox and Dermal Fillers

      #### Areas of treatment

      **Lips:** Lips are one of the most popular areas of face for treatment. Lip filler can add volume, definition, hydration, and make a big impact your smile!

      **Cheeks:** As we age, we tend to lose volume most noticeably in our cheeks. Cheek filler can lift, contour, restore volume loss and minimize jowling.

      **Nasolabial folds/Marionette lines:** Filler can soften any lines and creases around your mouth and reverse the aging process.

      **Chin/Jawline:** The lower third of the face is key to overall facial balance. Filler can correct a deficient chin or jawline and improve the overall shape of your face.

      #### 4 Step Sequence

      **1\. Understand You and Your Goals**

      During a complimentary consultation, Dr. Rehermann will listen to your goals and what you hope to see and experience when your treatment is complete. Through this discussion, an individualized treatment plan will be created that incorporates proper facial and dental proportions.

      **2\. Before Treatment**

      Please schedule any filler appointments at least two weeks in advance of any special events. You’ll be asked to avoid alcohol 24-48 hours before treatment and anti-inflammatories at least 3 days before treatment.

      **3\. During Treatment**

      Dr. Rehermann will use topical numbing or local anesthesia to make you as comfortable as possible for the procedure. Treatments usually last around 30-60 minutes.

      **4\. After Treatment**

      Results can be seen immediately! However, there is an initial swelling period and it make take up to 2 weeks for the filler to settle completely. Post-operative instructions will be given at your appointment.

      #### FAQ’s

      **What are dermal fillers?**

      Dermal fillers are gel-like substances injected beneath the skin to minimize facial wrinkles, provide facial volume, and augment facial features. Most fillers on the market are made up of hyaluronic acid, which is a naturally occurring substance in your skin.

      **Am I a candidate for fillers?**

      You may be a candidate if you are looking for a non-surgical way of smoothing fine lines or if you are unhappy with the volume in certain areas of your face. Dr. Rehermann would be happy to discuss your goals over a complimentary consultation and determine the best treatment for you.

      **How long do fillers last?**

      There are many different fillers with variable durations of effect. Hyaluronic acid fillers can last anywhere from 6 months to 2 years.

      **What are the side effects of fillers?**

      There is little to no downtime after the procedure, however the most common side effects are bruising, redness, swelling and mild pain.

      **How soon will I see results?**

      The results can be seen immediately. However, it takes about 2 weeks for fillers to settle completely after the initial swelling period.
