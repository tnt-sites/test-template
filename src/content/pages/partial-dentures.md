        html: What Are Partial Dentures?
      - type: prose
        html: Partial dentures are an ideal solution for patients who still have some healthy teeth and want to avoid full dentures. They are designed to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to your remaining natural teeth or sitting securely on the gumline. These dentures restore your ability to chew and speak comfortably while maintaining the natural contours of your face.

        html: Caring for Your Partial Dentures
      - type: prose
        html: Proper care is essential for maintaining the longevity and comfort of your partial dentures. Avoid brushing them with a regular toothbrush, as the bristles may scratch the surface. Instead, soak your dentures nightly in a gentle, effervescent denture cleaning solution to loosen food particles and debris. After soaking, rinse them thoroughly with water and use a soft cloth to gently wipe them clean.
      - type: prose
        html: By following a consistent care routine, you’ll not only extend the life of your dentures but also keep your gums and remaining teeth healthy. Regular checkups with our team ensure your dentures continue to fit comfortably and perform as intended.
