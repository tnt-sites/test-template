- _component: page-sections/services/service-split
  heading: How Long Does Invisalign Treatment Take?
  anchorName: ''
  subtext: 'For most patients, clear aligner treatment runs somewhere between 6 and 18 months. Where you
    fall in that range depends on what your smile needs: minor crowding or spacing issues tend to resolve
    on the shorter end, while more involved bite corrections take longer.


    The single biggest factor in staying on schedule is consistent wear. Aligners need to be in for 20
    to 22 hours each day to do their job. That leaves time for meals, brushing, and flossing, but not
    much else. Patients who treat their aligners as optional tend to see their timelines stretch.


    A few other factors that influence treatment duration:


    - The severity of crowding, spacing, or bite misalignment

    - How reliably you move through each set of aligners on schedule

    - Whether any mid-course refinements are needed along the way


    During your consultation at Medford Dental Wellness, our team will assess your specific situation
    and provide a personalized timeline estimate so you know exactly what to expect.'
