      #### Additional Information

      **Bike Storage:** The 2nd floor of the parking garage under Artisan has a dedicated bike rack for your use.

      The bike rack is located on the NE corner of the building.

      **B Cycle Stations:** There is a B Cycle station just one block away in front of Breese Stevens Field. (Paterson and E Washington)

      Also, S Livingston and E Main near the Sylvie.

      **Bus Routes:** Accessible on [Madison Metro Routes](http://www.cityofmadison.com/metro/) (2,3,4,5,6,27,29,37,56,57)

      **Free Parking:**  in the [**Constellation Building**](/ "Constellation")

      **Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy:** Please contact us at least 48 hours prior to your scheduled visit to enable other patients to be cared for during your appointment time.

      To make an appointment with our Madison dentist, please complete the form available [here](/contact/appointment). Otherwise, we welcome you to contact our patient care coordinator directly during our normal office hours
