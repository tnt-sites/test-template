      #### Patient Care Coordinator

      I am so excited to be a part of Artisan Dental! A little about myself is that I was born and raised in Appleton, Wisconsin and moved to Madison about 10 years ago. I have a loving husband and four beautiful girls. My girls keep me crazy busy, but when I have time I enjoy doing makeup and playing volleyball. My family and I also enjoy watching movies, playing board games, camping and hiking. We also try to visit grandma, grandpa, aunties, uncles and little cousins when we can, all the way in Appleton. Grandma and grandpa have 30 grandchildren so there is never a dull moment with the family.

      Again, I am so excited to be a part of Artisan Dental, a place where employees are valued and mean more than just a number. The morale here is unreal, I love that they put so much effort into building a great place to work. I can see that Artisan Dental cares deeply about their employees and community and I love being a part of that.
