    anchorName: ''
    subtext: >-
      Our dentists may recommend mini dental implants if you are
      interested in a less-invasive alternative to standard dental
      implants. They have many unique benefits, including:


      - The process for placing a mini dental implant is **less
      invasive** than placing a standard implant, resulting in a more
      comfortable treatment and shorter healing period.

      - Mini dental implants are **more affordable** than standard
      implants.

      - In some cases, we are able to place the mini implants and
      attach the dentures in the same day, resulting in a **faster
      tooth replacement process**.

      - When used to anchor dentures, mini implants can **enhance the
      comfort** of your smile and allow you to chew, eat and speak
      without worrying about slipping dentures.

      - Mini implants, like standard implants, help to **prevent bone
      loss** after tooth loss, which helps to maintain your facial
      shape and volume.
