  - _component: page-sections/ctas/cta-split
    id: what
    heading: What Is Dental Anxiety & How Can It Impact Your Health?
    anchorName: what
    subtext: >-
      Dental anxiety and dental phobia describe the degrees of fear,
      anxiety and stress that patients experience when visiting the
      dentist. Avoiding regular dental care can have major
      consequences—untreated cavities can lead to more severe decay,
      toothaches and even tooth loss, and gum disease can cause gum
      recession and tooth loss.


      Sedation dentistry will help you to deal with your anxiety and
      phobia and receive the care you need without experiencing fear or
      discomfort. Light sedation typically uses nitrous oxide, while
      moderate and deeper levels of relaxation can be reached with oral
      or IV sedation methods.
