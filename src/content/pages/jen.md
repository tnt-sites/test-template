      #### Dental Hygienist

      I began practicing dental hygiene in 2018; I enjoy working with my patients to help get them to optimal health. I am a huge health and fitness advocate and health truly does start in the mouth. Every patient’s needs are different and I look forward to helping you continue your healthy practices or help get you there! I am certified in local anesthetic, nitrous oxide and administering laser therapy. I create a positive, educational and fun experience for each one of my patients and look forward to meeting you!

      I have lived in Madison all of my adult life and love this city! Movement is my passion, you can find me at the gym pumping some iron, biking, hiking, or paddle boarding in my spare time. Food is also a big part of my life, my husband and I cook often and have fun trying new restaurants around town. Madison has a lot to offer and we love to go to the Sylvee and the Farmer’s Market as well.

      There is a saying, that “nothing is consistent but change.” I believe to grow and develop you need change. I am honored to be part of such a great team atmosphere and mission that we stand behind here at Artisan Dental!
