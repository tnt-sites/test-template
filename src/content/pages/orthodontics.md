    heading: Who Can SureSmile Clear Aligners Help?
    subtext: |-
      If you are interested in straightening your teeth with SureSmile, then the natural next question is, “Am I a good candidate?” This is a question that we can only answer after completing a comprehensive oral exam. With that said, this clear aligner treatment has proven to be effective at correcting mild to moderate cases of the following orthodontic problems:

      Read More

      ![Illustration of crowded teeth](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/2-who-suresmile.jpg)

      Do your teeth crowd together because there isn’t enough room in your mouth for them to sit in their ideal places? If so, it’s not just the aesthetics of your smile that’s compromised; you’re at an increased risk of tooth decay and gum disease, too. For that reason, we strongly recommend [scheduling an appointment](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/contact.html) with our team at Taylor Street Dental to discuss your treatment options, like SureSmile!

      ![Illustration of gapped teeth](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/3-who-suresmile.jpg)

      Maybe you’re struggling to smile confidently because you’re dealing with the opposite problem: there are noticeable gaps between your teeth. Fortunately, clear aligners are often still an option. So, don’t just assume that you need metal braces! If we determine that you’re a good candidate for SureSmile, then we can gently and gradually close the gaps with a custom series of clear, virtually undetectable trays.

      ![Illustration of an overbite](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/4-who-suresmile.jpg)

      If your upper teeth extend beyond your lower ones significantly, negatively impacting everything from your self-esteem to your speaking patterns in the process, then you’re likely wondering if SureSmile can fix overbites as well. Good news: the answer is often “yes!” So, don’t hesitate to schedule a consultation with one of our talented [cosmetic dentists](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/cosmetic-dentistry.html) if you’re ready to pursue a straighter, healthier smile.

      ![Illustration of an underbite](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/5-who-suresmile.jpg)

      When you bite down, do your lower teeth sit in front of your upper ones? If that’s the case, then you likely have an underbite, which is an orthodontic problem that can lead to low self-esteem, trouble chewing properly, and chronic jaw pain. If this sounds all too familiar, then take the next step: schedule a consultation with us so we can determine if you’re a candidate for SureSmile.

      ![Illustration of a crossbite](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/6-who-suresmile.jpg)

      One characteristic of a crossbite is that a few of the upper teeth sit behind the lower ones. Again, several issues can arise if this bite problem is left untreated, including premature wear and tear on certain teeth. So, it’s important to schedule an appointment at our Springfield dental office sooner rather than later. That way, we can determine if you’re a good candidate for SureSmile!

      ![Illustration of an open bite](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/7-who-suresmile.jpg)

      Is there an opening between your upper and lower teeth when you bite down? If so, you may have an open bite. Like the other orthodontic problems listed here, SureSmile is often a solution for patients with mild to moderate cases. So, you shouldn’t hesitate to schedule a consultation with us if you are interested in a metal-free path to your best smile.

    heading: Benefits of SureSmile Clear Aligners
    subtext: |-
      While traditional braces may be suitable for some patients, they simply aren’t the right solution for everyone. At Taylor Street Dental, we are dedicated to custom treatment plans for every patient, and when orthodontic treatment with SureSmile is the right option, our team has the flexibility to create a personalized treatment plan that will deliver a healthy, beautiful smile that lasts. Some of the benefits of this orthodontic treatment include:

      Read More

      ![Closeup of patient putting on clear aligner](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/suresmile-1.jpg)

      For many men, women, and adolescents seeking straighter teeth, the thought of years spent in metal braces is enough to keep them from pursuing orthodontic treatment. Sound familiar? If so, you’ll be happy to hear that SureSmile clear aligners are crafted from smooth, see-through plastic and are custom-made to fit the contours of your teeth perfectly. As a result, they are virtually invisible.

      ![Man with glasses smiling while working on computer in office](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/suresmile-2.jpg)

      With clear SureSmile aligners, you can get the results you want without any of the discomfort of regular braces. That’s largely due to the fact that they are custom-made for each patient, which ensures they don’t dig into the soft tissue. However, it is normal to experience some soreness from time to time as your teeth begin to move into their ideal places. If that happens, use a cold compress, avoid crunchy foods, and, if needed, take OTC pain medication as directed.

      ![Couple smiling while enjoying glass of wine in restaurant](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/suresmile-3.jpg)

      With SureSmile, you don’t have to worry about dietary restrictions. In fact, there’s only one strict rule: you need to remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. Outside of that, all we ask is that you eat mostly healthy, well-balanced meals. That way, your teeth and gums stay decay and infection-free throughout your orthodontic treatment.

      ![Woman smiling while brushing her teeth in bathroom](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/suresmile-4.jpg)

      Another benefit of SureSmile: you don’t need to buy special cleaning products or adopt a cumbersome dental care regimen. Just remove your aligners, rinse them with clean, cool water, and place them in their designated case. Then, brush, floss, and rinse with mouthwash, as usual. When you’re done, clean your aligners and put them back on.

      ![Woman smiling at reflection in handheld mirror](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/suresmile-5.jpg)

      Compared with other orthodontic treatments, SureSmile is much faster. Traditional bracket and wire braces, for example, typically take 18 to 36 months. Clear aligners, on the other hand, often deliver results in just 12-18 months. So, if you have a wedding, a vacation, or another special event coming up that you want a straight smile for, consider this popular [smile-enhancing treatment](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/cosmetic-dentistry.html)!

      ![Patient smiling at dental assistant holding clear aligner](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/suresmile-6.jpg)

      Is this upcoming semester going to be your busiest one yet? Maybe you recently started a new job or are a busy parent with a jam-packed schedule. Whatever the case may be, you don’t have to worry – there aren’t monthly adjustment appointments with SureSmile. Instead, the progress visits are every 6-8 weeks, and they are usually less than 30 minutes!

    heading: Living with SureSmile Aligners
    subtext: |-
      If you’re curious what life will look like once you officially begin the teeth-straightening process, you’re in the right place! In this next section, we’re covering helpful information on the topic, starting with what you need to know about wearing your clear aligners.

      Read More

      ![Woman smiling while working on laptop at home](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/2-living-suresmile.jpg)

      Naturally, if you don’t wear your aligners, they won’t work. So, if you want to stay on track with your treatment plan and avoid frustrating delays, you need to keep them on for a minimum of 20 hours each day. If you’re having trouble meeting this goal, try multi-tasking where you can. For example, you can soak your trays in a dentist-approved cleaning solution while you do your oral hygiene regimen at night.

      ![Patient using toothbrush to clean aligners in sink](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/3-living-suresmile.jpg)

      Fortunately, cleaning your aligners only takes a few minutes. Not only that, but there’s a good chance that you already have everything you need: a soft-bristle toothbrush, mild dishwashing liquid, and water. Just make sure to gently scrub away saliva, food particles, and other debris after each meal. Then, if needed, soak them daily for 15 minutes or so in a dentist-approved cleaning solution.

      ![Couple smiling at each other while eating in kitchen](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/4-living-suresmile.jpg)

      Since SureSmile clear aligners are removable, you don’t have to abide by any strict dietary restrictions. In fact, as long as you take them off before eating or drinking anything other than water, nothing will really change!

      Important reminder: If tooth decay, gum disease, or any other common oral health problems develop, then your treatment plan will temporarily be put on hold so we can provide the necessary restorative care. So, it’s important to eat lots of nutrient-dense, well-balanced meals and to keep sugary and starchy snacks to a minimum.

      ![Man smiling while talking on phone](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/5-living-suresmile.jpg)

      Even if you do your best to keep track of your aligners and try to prevent them from breaking, accidents sometimes happen. The best thing to do is to call our team at Taylor Street Dental so we can learn what happened and take a look at your treatment plan. Then, we can determine how to get you back on track, like having you wear the previous set in the series while we work on a replacement.

      ![Patient smiling while holding mirror in treatment chair](https://www.mygreatsmile.com/assets/images/6-living-suresmile.jpg)

      A crucial part of life with SureSmile is the progress visits. These usually occur every 6-8 weeks, and they are important because they allow us to monitor the movement of your teeth. If you’re on track with your treatment plan, then we can send you on your way with the next several pairs of aligners in the series. If you aren’t on track, then we need to figure out why that is and what adjustments need to be made to avoid lengthy delays.
