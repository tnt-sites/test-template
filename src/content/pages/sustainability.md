      \t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t<p>As a sustainable business we aim to enhance the social, ecological and economic relationships we have with all of our stakeholders as we work together to achieve the <a href=\"https://sdgs.un.org/goals\"><strong>United Nations Sustainable Development Goals</strong></a>. In 2017, Artisan Dental sustainability efforts were further assessed and verified when we became a <a href=\"https://bcorporation.net/directory/artisan-dental-llc\" target=\"_blank\" rel=\"noopener\"><strong>Certified B Corporation</strong></a> by <strong><a href=\"https://bcorporation.net/\" target=\"_blank\" rel=\"noopener\">B Labs</a></strong>. As a <strong><a href=\"https://bcorporation.net/directory/artisan-dental-llc\" target=\"_blank\" rel=\"noopener\">Certified B Corporation</a></strong>, we are demonstrating our commitment to meeting one of the highest standards of transparency, accountability, and performance available globally for sustainable and socially responsible businesses. You can learn more about our performance in the <a href=\"https://bcorporation.net/directory/artisan-dental-llc\"><strong>Governance, Community, Environment, Worker, and Customer categories of the B Impact Assessment</strong></a> used to qualify companies to become a <strong><a href=\"https://bcorporation.net/directory/artisan-dental-llc\" target=\"_blank\" rel=\"noopener\">Certified B Corporation</a></strong> here.</p><p>Our philanthropic donations are also strategically allocated to support achieving the United Nations Sustainable Development goals. You can learn more about our outstanding non-profit partners and the Sustainable Development goals they are addressing at our <a href=\"community-giving.html\"><strong>Community Giving</strong></a> page.</p><p>You can learn more about our overall impact by reviewing the <a href=\"impact-report.html\"><strong>Artisan Dental Impact Report</strong></a>.</p><p>Some examples of our sustainability initiative include the&nbsp;<strong><a title=\"Artisan Dental Recycling Program\" href=\"program-details.html\">Artisan Dental Recycling Program</a></strong>, the first and only oral care products recycling program in the Dane County area.</p><p>100% of our electricity is purchased through <a href=\"https://www.mge.com/our-environment/green-power/green-power-tomorrow\"><b>MG&amp;E Greener Tomorrow</b></a> program.&nbsp;</p><p>In 2020, Artisan Dental completed a comprehensive carbon footprint analysis in cooperation with <a href=\"https://carboncreditcapital.com/\"><strong>Carbon Credit Capital</strong></a>.&nbsp; This analysis included a complete assessment of our Scopes 1, 2, &amp; 3 greenhouse gas emissions associated with the operation of Artisan Dental.&nbsp; After completing this analysis we determined a total carbon footprint of slightly less than 12 metric tonnes.&nbsp;</p><p>Artisan Dental became the United States’ first carbon-neutral general dentistry practice by offsetting 100% of the carbon generated by purchasing carbon credits that are part of <strong><a href=\"https://green-trees.com/\">Green Tree’s</a>, <a href=\"https://green-trees.com/why-forests/nature-based-solutions/\">ACRE (Advanced Carbon Restored Eco-system)</a></strong>.&nbsp; Green Tree’s ACRE system plants new forests in the Mississippi Alluvial Valley to sequester carbon, restore bio-diversity through new eco-system creation and enhance water quality through the water filtering benefits forests provide right here in the United States.</p><p>We are a member of the <strong><a href=\"https://connect.wsbcweb.com/members-3/\" target=\"_blank\" rel=\"noopener\">Wisconsin Sustainable Business Council</a></strong>, <a href=\"https://sustaindane.org/going-sustainable/at-work/sustainable-business-network/\" target=\"_blank\" rel=\"noopener\"><strong>Sustain Dane’s Sustainable Business Network</strong></a> &amp; <a href=\"http://www.danebuylocal.com/\" target=\"_blank\" rel=\"noopener\"><strong>Dane Buy Local</strong></a>.</p>\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t
