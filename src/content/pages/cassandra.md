      #### **Equipment and Maintenance Team Leader**

      In 2018 I became a Dental Assistant. I am so lucky to have been working at Artisan Dental for 5 years now! We focus on offering treatment that is empathetic and compassionate. I couldn’t be more overjoyed to be working with our team.  

      My husband and I are recently married. We both love the outdoors, hiking, TRAVELING,  hanging out with friends, going to badger games, trying new restaurants and different food Madison has to offer.  I am originally from Menomonie WI, but have been in Madison for 13 years. I am also very passionate about being healthy. I love going to the gym and also taking cycling classes in the area. 

      We love traveling to different locations and would love recommendations! I couldn’t be happier to be working at Artisan Dental. I can’t wait to meet you!
