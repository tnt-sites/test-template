            Dr. Rehermann is delighted to become a new member of the Artisan Dental team and the surrounding Madison community. 

            Dr. Rehermann was born and raised in Pennsylvania. He attended Penn State University and received his B.S. in 2012, graduating magna cum laude. He then went on to dental school at the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine and earned his D.M.D. in 2016.

            During dental school, Dr. Rehermann joined the U.S. Army and received a scholarship from the military’s Health Professional Scholarship Program. 

            Upon graduation from dental school, Dr. Rehermann completed a one-year Army Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency in Fort Campbell, KY. After his residency, he worked in Dongducheon, South Korea for a yearlong tour. He also practiced in Savannah, Georgia for two years before arriving in Madison. 

            Throughout his time in the Army, he gained vast experience with the latest dental technologies, techniques, and materials. He has devoted hundreds of hours of continuing education so that he can offer patients the best treatment options out there.

            After four years of military experience, Dr. Rehermann is excited to redirect his dedication and service to the Artisan Dental community.

            Outside of the dental chair, Dr. Rehermann tries to enjoy a healthy lifestyle. He loves to exercise, cook, attend concerts, and travel with his partner. He’s also a pretty big fan of Netflix. As a new transplant to the Midwest, he is looking forward to all that Madison has to offer!
