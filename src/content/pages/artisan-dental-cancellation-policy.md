      - To better serve and honor the time of our providers and our patients we will be enacting a 24 hour cancellation policy as of August 18, 2025.
      - If you need to reschedule or cancel any of your appointments, we ask that you call our office at 608.467.8022, no later than 24 hours prior to your appointment.
      - If calling less than 24 hours prior to your appointment or not coming to a scheduled appointment, our cancellation fee will be collected over the phone or billed to your account.
      - Thank you for your understanding!
