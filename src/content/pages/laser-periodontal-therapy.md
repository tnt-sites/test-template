    heading: Laser Periodontal Therapy Promotes Regrowth
    text: >-
      Laser periodontal therapy or surgery offers a much more conservative
      solution to gum disease. Rather than a subtractive approach that removes
      gum and bone, laser periodontal therapy actually helps bone and gums
      re-grow.


      The laser uses high-energy photons to clean the gums and surfaces of the
      teeth and stimulate the bone around teeth. The laser ablates a microscopic
      layer of the tissues that destroys bacteria and calculus and sanitizes
      these surfaces. The action of the laser also cleans the tooth surface in a
      way that does not leave behind a smear layer or debris but rather leave
      the tooth surface almost identical to its original surface. Because of
      this, gums and bone can easily reattach to the tooth and new bone, and gum
      growth is often observed following treatment. Supplementary bone grafting
      and guided tissue regeneration can also be performed in tandem with this
      procedure to further increase the amount of tissue that regrows.
