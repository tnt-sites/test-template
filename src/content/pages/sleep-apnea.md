      \t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t<p><!-- /wp:heading --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>

      <p>Sleep Apnea is a sleep disorder characterized by repeated interruptions&nbsp;in breathing that cause&nbsp;fragmented, nonrestorative sleep.&nbsp;These airflow interruptions can occur several or hundreds of times per night and can last between seconds and minutes. If left untreated sleep apnea can lead to serious health problems including high blood pressure, heart disease, stroke, diabetes and excessive daytime sleepiness. At Artisan Dental we aim to treat obstructive sleep apnea. According to the American Association of Sleep Medicine, an oral appliance can be used as first line therapy in mild and moderate sleep apnea cases as research&nbsp;shows it is an effective&nbsp;form of treatment.&nbsp; There are three type of sleep apnea:</p>

      <p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:heading {\"level\":3} --></p>

      <h4 class=\"wp-block-heading\">Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA)</h4>

      <p><!-- /wp:heading --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>

      <p>Is caused by restrictions or blockages of the nose, mouth, upper and throat. These restriction can be a result of collapse or relaxation of tissues surrounding the airway. Examples of these tissues include the tongue, tonsils or soft palate. This restricted airway can cause snoring, snorting or gasping for air.</p>

      <h4><span style=\"color: #292929;font-family: Poppins, sans-serif;font-size: 30px;font-weight: bold\">Central Sleep Apnea (CSA)</span></h4>

      <p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:image --></p>

      <p><!-- /wp:image --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>

      <p>Is caused by the brains failure to communicate to the bodies muscles to enable continuously breathing. This can result in the stopping and restarting of breathing due to inconsistent communication from the brain. Unlike obstructive sleep apnea, the airway is not blocked.</p>

      <p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:heading {\"level\":3} --></p>

      <h4 class=\"wp-block-heading\">Complex Sleep Apnea Syndrome</h4>

      <p><!-- /wp:heading --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>

      <p>Is caused by both Obstructive Sleep Apnea and Central Sleep Apnea.</p>

      <li>Hormonal disorders</li>

      <li>Family history</li>
