      - type: heading
        html: What is obstructive sleep apnea?
      - type: prose
        html: Drs. Glenn and Michelle Taylor describe obstructive sleep apnea, or OSA, as a sleeping disorder that can cause a cycle of poor breathing throughout a single night’s sleep. Due to an obstruction in the airway, usually from the tongue collapsing into the airway space during sleep, the patient can no longer breathe and awakens. Once they fall back asleep, the cycle continues. It has been found that patients with obstructive sleep apnea will often fall asleep and awaken hundreds of times in a single night, leaving them still feeling fatigued after what they have believed to be a whole night’s rest.
      - type: heading
        html: Is obstructive sleep apnea dangerous?
      - type: prose
        html: When left untreated, obstructive sleep apnea can be a factor in many systemic diseases.&nbsp; The causes of stroke, heart attack, high blood pressure, memory loss, dementia and diabetes all have ties to sleep apnea.&nbsp; It is critical to get a proper diagnosis and treatment for sleep apnea to help prevent these diseases from happening.&nbsp; We recommend a sleep study for most adult patients, especially those with evidence of tooth grinding, snoring or poor sleep and tiredness during the day.&nbsp; These are all symptoms that you may have obstructive sleep apnea.
      - type: heading
        html: Can obstructive sleep apnea be cured?
      - type: prose
        html: OSA cannot be cured but can be managed to ensure patients are getting a good night’s rest. While the CPAP machine effectively maintains open airways, many patients avoid wearing them because they are cumbersome and uncomfortable. Therefore, the dentists at Taylor Dental Care are excited to offer another treatment option with oral appliance therapy. Patients can wear a customized mouth appliance while they sleep to address the problems associated with this condition.
