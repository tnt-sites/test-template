    heading: What is Sleep Apnea?
    text: Sleep apnea is a serious sleep-related disorder that affects millions of
      people worldwide. The condition is often caused by the soft tissues at the
      back of the throat relaxing while a person is sleeping. This causes a
      blockage to the upper airway, which then forces the person to wake up
      abruptly to essentially catch their breath. The condition can have a
      serious impact on your quality of life, and it can even be the catalyst
      for many other medical conditions.
