          <ul>
          <li><strong>Root canal treatment</strong> – infected teeth need to be addressed as soon as possible, especially when patients are in pain. Root canal therapy, or endodontic treatment, is performed to save a tooth from permanent extraction.</li>



          <li><strong>Composite fillings</strong> – areas of tooth decay impact the health of the smile and the function of the tooth. Composite fillings are tooth-colored materials used to fill areas of decay to restore the smile and avoid further development.</li>



          <li><strong>Dental veneers</strong> – covering imperfect teeth near the front of the smile with veneers are an excellent solution to problems such as breakage or permanent staining.</li>



          <li><strong>Dental bonding</strong> – using a composite resin material, our dentists can repair a tooth that has an imperfection quickly and easily. This material mimics the appearance of natural tooth enamel and blends in with the smile for same-day repair.</li>



          <li><strong>Dental crowns</strong> – teeth with significant damage may be improved using a customized dental crown that is bonded over the tooth to provide an extra layer of strength and protection. These restorations are made with porcelain to look as natural as possible.</li>



          <li><strong>Invisalign orthodontics</strong> – patients with significant smile misalignment, may find that orthodontic services can help, but most want to avoid traditional bracket-and-wire braces. Invisalign orthodontics is a tray aligner therapy that can realign the teeth and improve the appearance and function of the smile without noticeable metal appliances.</li>
