        title: How Long is a Smile Makeover?
        contentSections:
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
            text: |-
              A smile makeover’s duration varies from patient to patient. Depending on the number (and type) of procedures it includes, the overall treatment could take a short or long time.

              To give an example, say that your makeover only includes non-invasive procedures. These might range from standard in-office teeth whitening and dental bonding to gum recontouring. In this scenario, your makeover should only take a day; the shortest of the procedures could even finish in an hour.

              Alternatively, perhaps your makeover features invasive dental work. The dentist may need to place dental veneers or crowns as part of the overall treatment. If that’s the case, your makeover could potentially take weeks or even months. The process of placing dental restorations requires several dental visits, with each appointment playing a crucial role.
      - _component: building-blocks/wrappers/accordion/accordion-item
        title: Is a Smile Makeover Safe?
        contentSections:
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
            text: |-
              You can rest assured that a proper smile makeover is safe. When performed by a qualified dentist, the treatment won’t harm your teeth, gums, or the rest of your mouth.

              First, the procedures included in a makeover often aren’t invasive. Common ones like teeth whitening and dental bonding don’t alter your tooth structure; they just apply gels and resins. The applied materials are non-toxic as well, so they don’t damage tooth enamel or gum tissue.

              Dentists also take precautions with invasive cosmetic work. For example, they’ll only remove the minimum amount of enamel necessary to place veneers and crowns. These prosthetics also compensate for the removed bits by protecting your otherwise-exposed teeth.
      - _component: building-blocks/wrappers/accordion/accordion-item
        title: How Many Treatments Can You Include in a Smile Makeover?
        contentSections:
          - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
            text: |-
              Your smile makeover can potentially include as many procedures as you want. All in all, the final number will depend on what options your dentist offers.

              Imagine a patient who only wants their makeover to brighten their smile. For treatment, they could get a combination of teeth whitening, dental bonding, and porcelain veneers. Each of these procedures can help a grin look cleaner and brighter, and a makeover can easily incorporate all three of them.

              In contrast, maybe you just want to hide tooth chips or cracks. Your makeover could thus feature both dental veneers and metal-free crowns. These two restoration options hide smile damage, and there’s no reason a makeover can’t include both.
